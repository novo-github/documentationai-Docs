<a id="enable-warnings" />

## Who can turn on warnings for my machine?

Only administrators can change warning settings. In the machine configuration, open **PDA Assistant** and turn on **Enable warnings on this machine** before setting delays, severity, and sound. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).

<a id="warning-action" />

## What should I do when a warning appears on the terminal?

Select **View all warnings** and read the affected order and issue. Check the machine, then use the available action to select or end an order, set a downtime reason, or refresh the view. **Live data is stale** means other warnings may be unreliable. Follow [Respond to an Assistant Warning](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition).

<a id="sound" />

## How do I stop the alarm sound, and why is it sometimes silent?

Select **Mute** to silence sound on your device or **Enable sound** to turn it on. The browser needs a tap on the page first. **Info** is always silent; other sounds depend on administrator settings and may be limited to active shifts. See [PDA Assistant sound](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition).

<a id="snooze" />

## Why does a warning come back after I snooze it?

Snoozing hides and silences a warning only for the configured duration. It stays listed under **Snoozed** and returns if the cause remains. **Resume now** restores it sooner. Follow the [operator workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition).

<a id="copy-settings" />

## Will copying warning settings include my unsaved changes?

Yes. **Copy settings** copies the settings shown on the page, including unsaved changes. It replaces the selected machines’ PDA Assistant settings and leaves their other settings unchanged. Review the confirmation and result counts. See [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

<a id="template" />

## Does choosing an Automator template start sending alerts?

No. A template only prefills the rule. Administrators must review its prerequisites, machines, recipients, thresholds, and schedule, then save it. A switched-off rule stays saved but does not run. Follow [Create an Automator Rule](/en/platform-intro/workflows/automator-rule-creation).

<a id="email" />

## Why is there an Automator alert but no email?

Without email recipients, the alert appears in Events but nobody receives an email. Escalation also needs selected escalation recipients to notify anyone when an alert is not handled in time. Review the recipients in **Review → Your settings**. See [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator).

<a id="metrics" />

## Why do setup and scrap values stay the same and then jump?

These values update when an operator records a PDA entry. Setup compares cumulative preparation with planned time; scrap uses the current job’s PDA production entries. A sensor alone does not update entered scrap. See [Automator measures](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator).

<a id="active-shift-alerts" />

## Why did my alert stop working after I switched to active shifts?

Some alert conditions do not fire for machines with active shifts. When creating or editing the rule, check the notice: it can say that the alert never fires for these machines or show how many selected machines will be skipped. Review the rule before relying on it. See [Alert Rules](/en/platform-intro/pages/alert-rules).

<a id="work-center-alert" />

## Why does my rule never trigger for a work center?

Work centers have no AVA. Rules using machine or sensor measurements never trigger for them. **Work center: never triggers** identifies this limit in Alert Rules; Automator warnings name the affected measurements and work centers. Review the conditions and destinations before relying on the rule. See [Alert Rules](/en/platform-intro/pages/alert-rules) and [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator).

<a id="delivery-alert" />

## Can I get an alert before an order misses delivery?

Yes. An administrator can create an Automator rule with **Forecaster → Delivery Date at Risk** greater than 0. Choose email recipients before saving. Open the resulting event and use **Show in Gantt** to inspect the delay. Follow [Create an Automator Rule](/en/platform-intro/workflows/automator-rule-creation).

<a id="schedule-rule" />

## Why can I not combine a delay warning with my machine conditions?

Schedule conditions must stay in a separate rule from machine or order conditions. Their active-shift restriction is also unused. Create a separate Forecaster rule and review its recipients and timing. See [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator).
