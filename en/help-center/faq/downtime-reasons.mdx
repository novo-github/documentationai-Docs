<a id="manage" />

## Who can change the downtime reasons?

Only administrators can create, edit, delete, or restrict reasons. Use **Machine Settings → Downtime reasons** or **Admin Panel → Configure Analyzers → Downtime reasons**. Editing a shared reason changes it for all machines. Follow [Create and Manage Downtime Reasons](/en/platform-intro/workflows/timeline-reason-creation).

<a id="missing" />

## Why is a downtime reason missing on my machine?

The machine may use **Only selected reasons**, or an administrator may have changed the reason’s availability. Choose an available reason or ask an administrator to check the list. An open PDA page picks up changes on its next refresh; a rejected choice also updates the list. See [Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition).

<a id="future" />

## Why do new reasons appear on one machine but not another?

**All reasons (default)** includes future additions. A fixed list does not. Unticking a machine marked **All reasons** in **Available on machines** also gives it a fixed list of the other current reasons. See [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

<a id="copy" />

## Can I use the same reason selection on several machines?

Yes. Administrators can use **Apply to other machines** in **Downtime reasons**. Review the current selection and destination machines before confirming: their previous lists are replaced. Follow [Create and Manage Downtime Reasons](/en/platform-intro/workflows/timeline-reason-creation).

<a id="code" />

## What does the code beside a downtime reason mean?

It is an optional company code, such as F12, that helps you recognize a reason. Codes are unique within the company and use uppercase letters. Categories group the choices; chart explanations show the full code, name, and category. See [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

<a id="last-reason" />

## Why can I not untick this machine for a reason?

The change would leave that machine without any reason. Give it another reason first, or keep the current one ticked. See [availability settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

<a id="delete" />

## What happens if I delete a downtime reason?

The deletion notice shows where the reason is used. Past downtimes remain without a replacement reason. Reason charts and reports are updated for the affected days. Reports you already received stay as they were. The reason is also removed from machine lists, downtime reason rules, defaults and breaks. Break times stay. A restricted machine list stays restricted even if it loses its last reason. To clear only one timeline label, use **Remove label** instead. Follow [delete a shared reason](/en/platform-intro/workflows/timeline-reason-creation#delete-reason).

<a id="create-retry" />

## The reason was created, but its machines were not saved. Should I create it again?

No. The reason already exists. Check the machine checklist in the same form and save again. If the machine selection changed elsewhere, review the reloaded choices before retrying. See [Create and Manage Downtime Reasons](/en/platform-intro/workflows/timeline-reason-creation).

<a id="live-reason" />

## Why does the reason I chose for the current downtime disappear?

This was a display problem fixed in v1.5.153. Your chosen reason now stays in the running downtime’s tooltip after a page refresh, even when the downtime began before the last 8 hours shown. See [choose a downtime reason](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition#choose-a-downtime-reason).

<a id="after-restart" />

## Can I still choose a reason just after the machine starts again?

Yes, if the reason tiles are still offered shortly after the restart. Your choice is saved onto the downtime that just ended. A reason chosen while the downtime was running is also kept when it ends. Follow [choose a downtime reason](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#label-downtime-with-an-available-reason).

<a id="changed-totals" />

## Why do my downtime reason totals look different since this update?

Since November 5, 2026 (v1.5.153), **Downtime reason rules** apply to more idle and off downtimes when no reason was chosen by the machine operator. On machines with rules, more downtime can receive a reason, so totals by reason in reports and charts can change. Downtimes from before the update remain unchanged. See [automatic rules](/en/platform-intro/workflows/timeline-reason-creation#automatic-rules).

Since November 6, 2026 (v1.5.155), administrators can also enable automatic break labels. After activation, eligible finished downtime can move from a rule reason to a break reason, and labelling only the break can change counts and averages. A person’s choice stays. See [Break Settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/break-settings).

<a id="reason-priority" />

## Which wins, my chosen reason or the rules?

A reason chosen by the machine operator or set by hand takes priority. When [automatic break labels](/en/platform-intro/pages/break-settings) are enabled, an eligible break's reason comes next. **Downtime reason rules** apply to the remaining idle or off time; without a matching reason, the time stays unlabelled.

<a id="split-break-downtime" />

## Why is one downtime now counted as several?

**Label only the break (recommended)** can split downtime into the break and time before or after it. Each part counts separately in counts and averages by reason. Total time in each machine state stays the same. **Label the whole downtime** avoids that split but can hide a long breakdown under the break reason. See [automatically labelled breaks](/en/platform-intro/pages/timeline-analysis#read-automatically-labelled-breaks).
