## How do I create a ticket by speaking?

On the PDA page, open **Tickets** and tap the microphone in **Add Ticket**. Say what happened, who should take care of it and by when. Correct **Your words**, choose **Fill with AI**, check the details and press **Create Ticket**. Until that last step, no ticket is created. [Follow the steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/pda-ticket-voice).

<a id="no-microphone" />

## Why is there no microphone on my Add Ticket card?

You need AI assistant access and permission to create tickets. The machine’s template must also allow **Fill in tickets with AI**. Ask an Admin to check these settings. Without a template, the microphone is off. It is on the PDA card only; other ways of opening a new ticket do not have this whole-ticket microphone. [Check access](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition#tickets).

<a id="not-found" />

## What do I do when a person or order is not found?

Read the **Not found:** message and choose the matching person, machine, order or tag yourself in the ticket. For **Orders**, type at least 2 characters of the order number. Check all filled-in details before **Create Ticket**. [Review the ticket](/en/platform-intro/workflows/pda-ticket-voice).

<a id="several" />

## I described two problems. Where is the second ticket?

Only the first ticket is filled in. Create it, then tap the card’s microphone again: **Your words** opens with the remaining problem. Fill in, check and create that ticket too. If the hint asks you to create a new ticket for the rest, describe the remaining problem again. [Follow the steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/pda-ticket-voice).

<a id="cost" />

## Does filling in a ticket with AI use credits?

Yes. Each filling in counts as one AI credit, like a **Fast** answer. When credits are used up, fill in the ticket yourself. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition#tickets).

<a id="orders" />

## How do I link an order to a new ticket?

In the new-ticket panel, type at least 2 characters of the order number under **Orders**, then select the matching order. This field appears when creating a ticket, not when editing an existing one. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/kanban#ticket-panel).

<a id="fields" />

## Can I dictate just the ticket name or description?

Yes. Use the microphone inside the field; your words are added at the end. The person who created the ticket can also use these microphones while editing. Only one dictation can run in the panel at a time. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/kanban#ticket-panel).

<a id="closing" />

## Why does closing my ticket ask me to save?

The ticket has unsaved input. **Create ticket** creates a new ticket; **Save** keeps changes to an existing one. **Discard** removes unsaved input, while **Keep editing** returns you to it. Closing the question also keeps you editing. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/kanban#unsaved-changes).

<a id="waiting" />

## Why can’t I create or save my ticket yet?

Creating and saving wait while a dictation is running and until its words are ready. A new ticket also needs a name: enter one if **Please give the ticket a name.** appears. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/kanban#unsaved-changes).
