## How do I change my sign-in email?

Open **Profile → Change email**, enter **New email**, and select **Send verification**. Enter your current password if requested. Open the link at the new address and select **Confirm email change**, then sign in with the new email. Follow [Change Your Email Address](/en/platform-intro/workflows/change-email).

<a id="pending-email" />

## Why does my old email still work after I entered a new one?

The current email stays active until you confirm the new one. **Email change waiting for confirmation** means the change is not finished. Open the confirmation link sent to the address shown in the notice. See [User Profile](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile).

<a id="missing-link" />

## What if the email does not arrive or the link no longer works?

Check the address in the pending notice. Use **Resend** after the displayed wait. If a link is invalid, expired, or replaced, request a new verification link from your profile or your administrator and use the latest one. Follow [Change Your Email Address](/en/platform-intro/workflows/change-email).

<a id="cancel-change" />

## Can I cancel an email change?

Yes. Select **Cancel change** while the request is pending. Your current address stays active. See [User Profile](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile).

<a id="admin-change" />

## Can an administrator change my email for me?

Administrators can request changes for users who are not operators using **Change email** in user management. The user must still confirm the link sent to the new address. The current email works until confirmation. See [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).
