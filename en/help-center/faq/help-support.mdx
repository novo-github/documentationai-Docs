## Where can I find instructions for the page I am on?

Open **Help & Support → Learn this page**. Start the guide with its round play button when one is offered, or open a documentation card. **All documentation** is available even without a matching guide or card. [Follow the steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/help-support).

<a id="report" />

## How do I report something that is not working?

Choose **Help & Support → Let us know**. The feedback form opens with **Bug** selected. Describe what happened, complete the form and send it to Novo AI. Opening the form alone sends nothing. This is separate from company tickets. [Learn about feedback](/en/platform-intro/pages/feedback).

<a id="ideas" />

## Where can I suggest an improvement?

**Feedback** at the right edge opens the feedback form with **Feature request** selected. Choose the type that fits and send your suggestion to Novo AI. [Learn about feedback](/en/platform-intro/pages/feedback).

<a id="missing" />

## Why can’t I open Help & Support on my phone?

The window is available on a computer or tablet with a wide view. It has no phone action-bar entry and is unavailable on the PDA page. You can still read this documentation on your phone. [Check access and limits](/en/platform-intro/pages/plan-a-meeting).
