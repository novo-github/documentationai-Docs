<a id="operation-priority" />

## Can I give one operation a different priority from its order?

Yes. In Assignment Planner, select the dashed **OP** chip to change **Operation priority**. The **P** chip changes **Order priority** independently. Follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

<a id="missing-work" />

## Why did my work disappear after I set a priority filter?

With **Order priority** and **Operation priority** both selected, work must match both. Filters affect unassigned operations and machine columns. Also check any order, product, or customer filter chip above the board. The information icon opens **Assignment Planner Guide**; see the [planner page](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).

<a id="planning-dates" />

## Which dates should I use to sort the steps of an order?

Use **Operation Planned Start** or **Operation Planned End** for individual steps. **Order Planned Start** and **Order Planned End** apply to the whole order and keep its steps together when sorting. Follow the [sorting steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

<a id="grouping" />

## How can I see an order’s work by machine?

On the order page, choose **Group by → Machine**. Choose **Operation** to return to a view by production step. See [Order Detail](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail).

<a id="actual-dates" />

## Why do planned dates change when I expand an order?

The grouped row shows the whole order’s planned window. Expanded rows show each assignment’s own window. The planned/actual choice beside the date pickers decides whether the date filter searches scheduled or recorded work. See [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products).

<a id="import-priority" />

## Why didn’t a repeat import change the priority?

Existing orders and operations always keep their priority. **Initial order priority (new orders)** and **Initial operation priority (new operations)** apply only to new work with empty priority cells or no selected priority column. An empty initial operation priority uses 100. See the [import settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/erp-upload-planner).

<a id="invalid-priority" />

## Why is my operation priority marked even though I set an initial value?

The initial value fills empty priorities only. Text, fractions, and negative operation priorities are marked for correction. Enter a whole number of zero or more, then review the import again. Follow the [import workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/order-upload-planner).

<a id="recorded-work" />

## Why does an imported operation stay on its old machine?

Recorded work prevents it from moving, but the operation is still imported. Choose **Keep both** if it should also run on the new machine. Production history stays unchanged. See [re-import protection](/en/platform-intro/pages/erp-upload-planner).

<a id="split-quantity" />

## Why does the import ask me to review machine quantities?

Quantities already split across machines stay unchanged and are marked for review. Compare **Planned quantity (machine)** with **Planned quantity (work order)**, then inspect the affected work in Assignment Planner. Follow the [import workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/order-upload-planner).

<a id="work-center-planning" />

## Can I plan work at a station without a sensor?

Yes. An administrator can create a **Work center**. Select it as the destination for an order or assignment, then review running and planned work under **Machines → Work centers**. Follow [Add a Machine or Work Center](/en/platform-intro/workflows/add-machine) and [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

<a id="work-center-switch" />

## What happens to the sensor when I change a machine to a work center?

The change removes its AVA assignments and stops all KPI calculation. Only administrators can change the kind. To collect readings again, save it as **Machine** and assign an AVA in the AVA tab. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).

<a id="work-center-kpis" />

## Why does my work center have no OEE or machine status?

A **Work center** has no AVA. It shows running and planned orders instead of sensor KPIs and is excluded from state counters and analytics. Missing KPIs do not mean zero performance. See [Work Centers](/en/platform-intro/pages/work-centers).

<a id="planner-view" />

## How do I switch from planning cards to the timeline?

Use the view switch in Assignment Planner or Assignment Gantt. Review dates in the timeline, then return to the planner to change assignments. Follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

<a id="forecast-meaning" />

## What does the Forecaster tell me?

It shows when work is expected to start and finish using current progress, queues, shifts, and earlier steps. Your planned dates stay unchanged until you save a planning change. Open **Forecaster** on Orders & Products, the order page, or Assignment Gantt. See the [Forecaster guide](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).

<a id="forecast-filters" />

## Why are late orders outside my date range still shown?

The Forecaster’s late-order filters pause date range and status filters. Order, product, customer, and machine filters still apply. Remove the Forecaster filter to use dates and status again. See [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products).

<a id="forecast-counts" />

## Why do Orders and the Gantt show different warning counts?

Orders & Products counts open orders late for delivery across the company. Assignment Gantt includes other warnings on selected machines, from last week to three weeks ahead and any week you have scrolled to. See [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).

<a id="unplanned-orders" />

## How do I find orders that are missing from every planning board?

Open **Orders & Products → Forecaster → Not planned at all → Show list**. These orders have a delivery date but no operation on a machine or in a hall pool. Open an order to **Assign steps**. A step also needs a planned start before it counts as planned. Follow the [planning workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

<a id="forecast-risk" />

## What does “at risk” mean, and can the expected finish be too early?

**at risk** (previously **tight**) means the work barely fits or an earlier step is not planned. Missing steps, planned durations, or shift plans can make the expected finish too early. Review these before relying on the date. See the [Forecaster explanation](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).

<a id="held-work" />

## Why does work move ahead of an assignment that is on hold?

After **Hold limit (working hours)**, the forecast treats that assignment as parked and lets work behind it move up. The paused work is not finished. Only working time counts; without shifts, 24 hours count as the full limit. Administrators change the limit in **Planning settings**. See [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).

<a id="save-plan" />

## Does trying a different ordering change my plan?

No. **Show on board** is a preview. **Save as plan** changes eligible queue positions and Watchmen planned dates after review. Running, on-hold, and blocked work keep their place, so the saved result can differ from the preview. Delivery dates stay unchanged. Follow [Compare and Save a Plan](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

<a id="move-undo" />

## Why can I not move an assignment or undo its move?

Running, on-hold, and finished assignments cannot use the Gantt move controls. Recorded work prevents changing the machine. Automatic ordering by delivery date prevents manual queue reordering. If the queue changed after a move, automatic undo is unavailable; review it and move the assignment back by hand. See [move limits](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).

<a id="parallel-jobs" />

## How do I tell the forecast that a machine runs several jobs at once?

An administrator sets **Planning → Jobs in parallel** in the machine’s basic information. Enter a whole number from 1 to 100 that matches actual capacity, then save. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).

<a id="forecast-rule" />

## Who can change what the Forecaster runs next?

Administrators choose the plant’s ordering under **Planning settings** in Assignment Gantt. Other users with planning access can compare alternatives in **Improve plan** without changing that setting. See the [ordering choices](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).

<a id="forecast-help" />

## Where can I open the Forecaster explanation?

Click the information icon beside **Forecaster** on Orders & Products, the order page, or Assignment Gantt. In Assignment Planner, open **Forecaster check → How does the Forecaster work?**. The help explains the colours, expected times, and why a finish may be too early. Opening it changes nothing in your plan. See the [Forecaster guide](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt).
