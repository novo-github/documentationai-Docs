<a id="mode" />

## Which answer mode should I choose?

Use **Auto** (recommended) for most questions, **Fast** for short, simple questions and **Deep analysis** (previously **Thorough**) for causes and recommendations from a close look at the data. It takes longer. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#answer-modes).

<a id="confirm" />

## Can the assistant change things without asking me?

No. **Review this change** shows the proposal, and nothing changes until you press **Confirm**. Forms are only filled in for you to check and save yourself. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#opening-and-changing).

<a id="credits" />

## What are AI credits?

Your company’s monthly AI credits are used for answers: **Fast** costs 1 credit, **Auto** 1–3 and **Deep analysis** 5, plus 1 when the assistant searches the web. Failed answers are free. The chat shows what is left. The company has one monthly allowance for everyone, not one per person; unused monthly credits do not carry over to the next month. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#credits).

Explanations through **Ask AI** in ⓘ help cost no credits; questions you type afterwards use credits as usual.

<a id="credits-run-out" />

## What happens when the credits run out?

Your Admin’s **When credits run out** choice applies. **Pause** is the default: **Auto** and **Deep analysis** wait until next month; only **Fast** still works for a few questions per person per day. **Allow overage** continues answers up to the **Extra credits per month** set by the Admin; extra credits are billed separately. The chat shows what is used up, whether **Fast** still works and when credits reset. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel#ai-settings).

Free answers through **Ask AI** in ⓘ help remain available when company credits are used up, provided you have access to the AI assistant and AI chats.

<a id="no-access" />

## Why can’t I use the AI assistant?

An Admin may have switched off your **AI access**, or your page access may not include **AI Chats**. Ask an Admin to check both. Admins always have access. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel#ai-settings).

<a id="forget" />

## How do I make the assistant forget something?

Use **Undo** right after **Saved to memory**, or open your profile → **My AI assistant → Memory** to **Edit**, **Delete** or **Forget everything**. Forgetting everything cannot be undone. Switch off **Remember things across chats** if you do not want memory across chats. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile#ai-memory).

<a id="memory-privacy" />

## Who can see what the assistant remembers about me?

Only you can see your personal memory. Admins never see it, even though they can switch memory off for the company. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile#ai-memory).

<a id="connect" />

## How do I connect ChatGPT or Claude to Watchmen?

An Admin first enables **AI apps**. Open your profile → **Connected apps → How to connect**, choose **Claude**, **ChatGPT** or **Other apps**, copy the **Connector URL** and follow the displayed steps. Sign in with your own Watchmen account, check the page naming the app and what it can read, then press **Allow**. It sees only what you may see. **Disconnect** in **Connected apps** removes access at once. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#connected-apps).

<a id="more-credits" />

## How do I get more AI credits?

Admins see the company’s credits this month under **Admin Panel → A.I. → AI assistant → Usage**. **Need more credits? Write to** gives the contact [tech@novoai.de](mailto:tech@novoai.de), also shown to Admins in the chat when credits are used up. Everyone else asks their Admin. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel#ai-credits).

<a id="keep-chatting" />

## Can I keep chatting while I open a page?

Yes. On the AI chat page, the assistant always asks first. **Open &lt;page&gt; and keep chatting in a floating window** opens the named page and continues the same conversation, with **Continued from the AI chat page** shown in the floating window. **Stay here** keeps you on the chat page. **Pop out** in the chat page header moves the conversation without opening a page. In the side panel and floating window, **Opening pages** with **Ask first** or **Automatic** works as before. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#opening-and-changing).

<a id="attach-files" />

## How do I show the assistant a file or a photo?

Use the paperclip in the side panel, floating window or AI chats page, drop files onto the chat or paste a screenshot. This also works on the PDA page. Send up to 5 files per message, each up to 25 MB: PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text or pictures. You can send only files, without text. [Follow the steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/chat-attachments).

<a id="files-waiting" />

## Why can’t I send my message after adding a file?

The assistant waits while documents are being read. Wait for **Ready**. Pictures can be sent once uploaded. If a file shows **Can't be read** or **No text found**, check its reason and remove it with ×; failed files are not sent. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#chat-files).

<a id="picture-only" />

## Can the assistant use a photo with no text in it?

Yes. The assistant looks at the picture, for example a photo of a broken part. **Picture only** means no readable text was found. The picture is used in this chat only and cannot be kept in the knowledge base. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#chat-files).

<a id="files-preference" />

## How do I stop being asked where to keep my chat files?

Choose **Keep in knowledge base** or **Only this chat** with **Remember my choice** selected. You can change this later in your profile → **My AI assistant → Files added in the chat**: **Ask each time**, **Keep in my knowledge base** or **Only in the chat**. Kept files are **Private**. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile#ai-preferences).

<a id="files-no-keep" />

## Why is there no option to keep my file in the knowledge base?

You need Knowledge Base page access, and an Admin must allow the assistant's knowledge base area under **Admin Panel → A.I. → AI assistant**. Without both, the keeping options and profile setting are hidden; files still work in the chat. A picture marked **Picture only** cannot be kept. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#keep-chat-files).

<a id="files-delete" />

## Will deleting a chat delete my files too?

Yes, for files used only in that chat. **Delete this conversation?** explains this before you confirm. Files you kept in your knowledge base stay. Deleting the chat cannot be undone. Files attached but neither sent nor kept in the knowledge base are removed after about a day. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#delete-chat).

<a id="message-box" />

## Where are the send button and answer mode now?

The paper-plane send button is at the bottom right inside the message box, after attach and dictate. The answer mode is at the bottom left, with your text above. This layout is the same in the side panel, floating window and AI chats page. Answers now appear as open text with spacing and headings, and a line with a dot beside each answer. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#message-box).

<a id="dictation-limit" />

## Why does dictation stop on its own?

Every recording stops after 2 minutes and keeps what you said as text. In the last 15 seconds, the timer warns that the end is approaching. This applies throughout the Watchmen Platform, including chat, knowledge, tickets and feedback. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#dictation).

<a id="ticket-details" />

## Can the assistant fill in the people and order for a ticket?

Yes. Ask the assistant to open a new ticket and name the people, order, tags and due date you need. It can fill these in along with the name, description and machines. Check the details in the ticket panel and press **Create Ticket** yourself. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#opening-and-changing).

<a id="help-cost" />

## Does Ask AI in the ⓘ help cost credits?

No. **Ask AI** explanations cost no credits on every page, including the PDA page, even when company credits are used up. On other pages, the answer opens in the assistant’s side panel, where questions you type afterwards use credits as usual. [Help on every page (ⓘ)](/en/platform-intro/concepts/field-help).

<a id="help-access" />

## Why is there no Ask AI in my ⓘ help?

**Ask AI** appears only when you are signed in and have access to the AI assistant and AI chats. Help on sign-in, sign-in code, registration and feedback forms has no **Ask AI**. Ask an Admin to check your access on other pages. [Help on every page (ⓘ)](/en/platform-intro/concepts/field-help).
