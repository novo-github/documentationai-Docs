## Which answer mode should I choose?

Use **Auto** (recommended) for most questions, **Fast** for short, simple questions and **Thorough** for deeper research that takes longer. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#answer-modes).

<a id="confirm" />

## Can the assistant change things without asking me?

No. **Review this change** shows the proposal, and nothing changes until you press **Confirm**. Forms are only filled in for you to check and save yourself. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#opening-and-changing).

<a id="credits" />

## What are AI credits?

Your company’s monthly AI credits are used for answers: **Fast** costs 1 credit, **Auto** 1–3 and **Thorough** 5, plus 1 when the assistant searches the web. Failed answers are free. The chat shows what is left. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#credits).

<a id="credits-run-out" />

## What happens when the credits run out?

Your Admin’s **When credits run out** choice applies. **Pause** is the default: **Auto** and **Thorough** wait until next month; only **Fast** still works for a few questions per person per day. **Allow overage** continues answers up to the **Extra credits per month** set by the Admin; extra credits are billed separately. The chat shows what is used up, whether **Fast** still works and when credits reset. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel#ai-settings).

<a id="no-access" />

## Why can’t I use the AI assistant?

An Admin may have switched off your **AI access**, or your page access may not include **AI Chats**. Ask an Admin to check both. Admins always have access. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel#ai-settings).

<a id="forget" />

## How do I make the assistant forget something?

Use **Undo** right after **Saved to memory**, or open your profile → **AI assistant → Memory** to **Edit**, **Delete** or **Forget everything**. Forgetting everything cannot be undone. Switch off **Remember things across chats** if you do not want memory across chats. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile#ai-memory).

<a id="memory-privacy" />

## Who can see what the assistant remembers about me?

Only you can see your personal memory. Admins never see it, even though they can switch memory off for the company. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile#ai-memory).

<a id="connect" />

## How do I connect ChatGPT or Claude to Watchmen?

An Admin first enables **AI apps**. Open your profile → **Connected apps → How to connect**, choose **Claude**, **ChatGPT** or **Other apps**, copy the **Connector URL** and follow the displayed steps. Sign in with your own Watchmen account, check the page naming the app and what it can read, then press **Allow**. It sees only what you may see. **Disconnect** in **Connected apps** removes access at once. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history#connected-apps).
