## How do I book a meeting?

Choose **Help & Support → Talk to our team** at the right edge of the Watchmen Platform. Select a meeting card, even if only one is offered, choose a day and time, and complete the booking in the calendar. Opening it alone books nothing. [Follow the steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/plan-a-meeting).

<a id="missing" />

## Why can’t I find a meeting to book?

**Talk to our team** in **Help & Support** appears only while meetings are offered. Help & Support remains available without meetings on wide screens. It is unavailable on phones and the PDA page. Use a computer or a tablet with a wide view. [Check access and limits](/en/platform-intro/pages/plan-a-meeting).

<a id="loading" />

## What do I do if the calendar does not load?

If **The calendar could not be loaded here.** appears, choose **Open in a new tab**. You can also use the round button at the top of the dialog with the tooltip **Open in a new tab**. [See loading help](/en/platform-intro/pages/plan-a-meeting).

<a id="email" />

## Why do I have to enter my email again?

Your account email is filled in only if it is verified. Otherwise, the booking form asks you to enter it. Your name and company are filled in when available. Check the details before completing the booking. [Learn about booking details](/en/platform-intro/pages/plan-a-meeting).

<a id="confirmation" />

## Is my meeting booked if I see Request sent?

No confirmation has been given yet. **Request sent** means the meeting still needs confirmation; you will get an email once it is confirmed. **Meeting booked** means confirmed and shows email information. **Open meeting link** appears when a meeting link is available. [Read the result](/en/platform-intro/workflows/plan-a-meeting).

<a id="language" />

## Why is the booking calendar in a different language?

The booking tool controls the calendar language. Meeting cards and the confirmation text follow your app language; a card uses its other available language if needed. [Learn more](/en/platform-intro/pages/plan-a-meeting).
