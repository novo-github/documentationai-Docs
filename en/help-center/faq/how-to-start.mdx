## Can I try the platform first?

Yes. [Request demo access](mailto:contact@novoai.de?cc=tech@novoai.de&subject=Request%20demo%20access&body=Company%3A%0D%0AName%3A%0D%0APhone%20%28optional%29%3A%0D%0AWhat%20I%20would%20like%20to%20see%20%28for%20example%20machine%20monitoring%2C%20planning%2C%20data%20acquisition%29%3A%0D%0AApproximate%20number%20of%20machines%3A) by email to receive your personal login and see the Watchmen Platform with a running factory.

## Start with Your Goal

- To review current machine state, open [Machines](/en/platform-intro/pages/machines).
- To work with products or production orders, open [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products).
- To schedule work, open [Scheduler](/en/platform-intro/pages/scheduler) or [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).
- To maintain shared platform setup, open the [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).
- To understand the documentation structure, open [Platform Intro](/en/platform-intro/overview).

## Where do I find the guides?

Open the graduation cap at the top of the screen. On a phone, the graduation cap button is hidden. Open the ⋮ menu at the top and choose **Interactive guides**. A small dot on the ⋮ button shows when something is new. Use **Getting started**, search, or **This page** with its guide count. You see only guides for your role and pages you can open. [Find your guide](/en/platform-intro/pages/interactive-guides).

<a id="continue-guide" />

## Can I continue a guide later?

Yes. If you go elsewhere to work, the guide pauses. Choose **Resume** on **Guide paused**, or return to the guides panel and use the play button with **Continue**. **End** stops the current walkthrough; your saved progress remains. [Follow the steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/interactive-guides).

<a id="guide-points" />

## What are guide points for?

Your first finish of each guide earns 10 points; finishing all guides available to you earns a one-time 50-point bonus. Repeats earn no more. If you have AI access and your company limits AI use with credits, **Convert** turns your whole balance into personal AI credits at 1 credit per point, usable for one month. Otherwise your points stay saved. [Read the points rules](/en/platform-intro/pages/interactive-guides).

<a id="assign-guides" />

## Can I give my team guides to complete?

As an Admin, open **Admin Panel → Interactive guides**. Use **People** for one person or **Guides** to assign a guide to people or a whole role. Role assignments include people who receive that role later. You can review completion there. [Assign guides](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assign-interactive-guides).

<a id="guide-emails" />

## How do I stop emails about assigned guides?

In your profile’s **Newsletter Preferences**, turn off **Email me when interactive guides are assigned to me** and choose **Save Preferences**. You must verify your email address before changing it. The assignments stay visible under the graduation cap. [Manage guide emails](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile).
