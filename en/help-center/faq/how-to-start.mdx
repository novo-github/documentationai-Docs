## Start with Your Goal

- To review current machine state, open [Machines](/en/platform-intro/pages/machines).
- To work with products or production orders, open [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products).
- To schedule work, open [Scheduler](/en/platform-intro/pages/scheduler) or [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).
- To maintain shared platform setup, open the [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).
- To understand the documentation structure, open [Platform Intro](/en/platform-intro/overview).
