## Overview

Novo AI's Watchmen Platform brings machine monitoring, production planning, operational analysis, and shared factory configuration into one workspace.

Use this documentation to understand an individual screen, follow a task from start to finish, or review the public REST API.

<Callout kind="info">
  The features and procedures documented here reflect the current platform documentation. Available pages and actions can differ according to your access.
</Callout>
