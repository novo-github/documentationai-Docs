## Overview

Novo AI's WatchMen AI Platform empowers you to optimize your industrial machines with cutting-edge artificial intelligence. You monitor performance in real-time, predict potential failures before they occur, and automate optimizations to boost efficiency and reduce downtime. Our mission is to transform machine management by making AI accessible and actionable for teams of all sizes.

Whether you operate a small workshop or a large manufacturing facility, WatchMen integrates seamlessly with your existing hardware and provides actionable insights that drive cost savings and productivity gains.
