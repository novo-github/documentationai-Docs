## Overview

Help users compare machines directly under the same time context.

Users open it from the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Admin, Manager, Operator
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/versus`
</Callout>

Managers, planners, and operators who need quick comparison.

## What You Can See

- Machine selectors and date controls.
- Comparative charts and summary blocks for the chosen machines.

## What You Can Do

- Choose the machines to compare.
- Change the time window.
- Review which machine is performing better or worse on the selected metrics.

## Main Entities

- Machine
- Time Range
- Performance Metric

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users identify gaps between machines and decide where to investigate next.

### Limits and Empty States
- The page is only useful when the selected machines have comparable data.
