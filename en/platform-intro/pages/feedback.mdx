## Overview

**Access: All roles.** Route: `/feedback`.

Feedback is the platform channel for reporting bugs, suggesting improvements, and requesting features from Novo AI. Describe the page, machine or order context, expected result, and observed result so the issue is understandable.

## Follow the Conversation

Conversations can be **Sent**, **In review**, **Resolved**, or **Closed**. Check replies and the displayed response date. A conversation closes automatically if it is not answered by that date.

For access changes, contact your company admin; [roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access) explains permissions. For public feature plans and votes, use [Roadmap](/en/platform-intro/pages/roadmap).
