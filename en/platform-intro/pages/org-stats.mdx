## Overview

**Access:** Admin only. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Provide a consolidated organization-level view of production performance instead of a single-machine view.

Users open it from the main navigation when the role allows it.

## What You Can See

- Live distribution of producing, idle, stopped, and preparation states.
- Performance views by day, week, month, custom date range, or shift.
- Machine and operator rankings for OEE and performance.
- Energy consumption, energy wastage, associated costs, and rated targets.
- Operational-impact indicators and daily energy trends.

## What You Can Do

- Compare production performance across the selected period or shift.
- Review machine and operator rankings.
- Monitor energy and operational-impact indicators.
- Use the page as a starting point before drilling into more focused machine or report pages.

## Understand Snapshot, Recovery, and Remaining Potential

**Live Utilization** is the share of machines producing right now, not an average for the period. Work centers are excluded from sensor analytics. **OEE**, **AVL**, and **PRO** use Watchmen's definitions; PRO means Performance.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Downtime Reduced** | Idle time reduced above each machine's best-days baseline, based on the lowest 25% idle rate; not all idle time. |
| **Revenue Recovered** | Reduced idle hours × Hourly Revenue. Already recovered value, not a forecast. |
| **Still reachable** | Current wasted-energy cost plus value of recovering half the remaining idle hours. Some idle time is unavoidable. |
| **Production shifts recovered** | Recovered time in shifts of 8 hours. |
| **Workflow Time Saved** | Impact indicator whose exact formula is not established here. |

**Example:** 4 recovered hours × €100/h gives €400 recovered value and half an 8-hour shift. Do not interpret every idle hour as avoidable loss. [Calculations](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations#organization-impact) explains the figures together.

## Energy, Sustainability, and Targets

**kWh** measures energy. **CO₂** is an emission estimate based on optional **Sustainability Check** inputs: plant area, waste percentage, renewable share, coolant output (Lts.), transport, and material efficiency. The complete CO₂ formula is not established here.

**Energy wastage** is described as energy consumed while not producing. Costs use configured **Energy Price**. The exact state coverage is unconfirmed; the FAQ idle example is illustrative. **Compared to machine rating** compares actual figures with configured targets.

## Read Downtime Reason Codes

Downtime chart labels include a reason's code when one is set. Short labels may be cut off; hover over a bar or pie slice to read the full name, code, and category. Categories help identify reasons; the charts still group by reason.

See [downtime reason settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel) for how administrators maintain these labels.
