## Overview
Provide a company-level view of operational performance instead of a single-machine view.

Users open it from the main navigation when the role allows it.

## Access
<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Admin, Manager
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/org-stats`
</Callout>

Managers and admins reviewing overall plant performance.

## What You Can See
- Company-level summary metrics and visualizations.
- Comparative indicators across the organization rather than one machine.

## What You Can Do
- Review performance at a broader level.
- Use the page as a starting point before drilling into more focused machine or report pages.

## Main Entities
- Company
- Machine
- Performance Metric

## Outcomes and Limits
### Common Outcomes
- Users identify whether the issue is local to one machine or visible across the organization.

### Limits and Empty States
- This page is more useful for broad review than for task-by-task execution.
