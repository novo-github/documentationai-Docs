## Overview

Help users review energy peaks and understand when machine activity may be creating avoidable load spikes.

Users open it from the main navigation when their role allows it.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/peakOptimizer`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Energy-oriented charts and time-based views.
- Filters that help narrow the review period.

## What You Can Do

- Review when high-load periods happen.
- Compare operational timing against energy-heavy windows.
- Use the page to support later scheduling or improvement decisions.

## Main Entities

- Machine
- Energy Use
- Time Window

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users identify periods that deserve rescheduling or closer review.

### Limits and Empty States
- The page depends on energy-related machine data being available.
