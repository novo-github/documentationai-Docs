## Overview

**Access:** Any role with access to this machine. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Give users a detailed, date-aware view of one machine’s live or recent activity.

Users open this page by selecting a machine from the Machines page.

- Change the selected date.
- Focus on a specific shift or all shifts.
- Open PDA, machine history, or alert rules.
- Review current machine state and recent events.

## Live Data, Pulse, and Last Heartbeat

**Live Data** streams current readings. **Last Heartbeat** is the device's last reported contact; it is not the machine's last production time. **Pulse** is the raw sensor-signal graph beneath the timeline. Its resolution can be 1 minute, 30 seconds, 5 seconds, or 1 second.

The timeline uses Production green, Idle orange, Off red, Preparation blue, and Unknown grey. Downtime is Idle or Off. A missing reason can show an unlabeled-timeline marker; use PDA to record the context.

## Read OEE, AVL, PRO, and Cycle Figures

**AVL** is production time divided by total recorded time. **PRO** is production plus preparation divided by switched-on time; it means Performance, not Production. The dashboard Quality tooltip shows **95%** and directs changes to an admin. These time definitions differ from textbook OEE. The exact server OEE formula remains unconfirmed.

Cycle-time averages differ by source: full interval time per detected cycle, active production time per detected cycle, or production time per operator-entered quantity. Compare the same period and source. See [calculations and examples](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations).

Shift energy totals use the company’s local shift start and end times.

## Understand Active Shifts

**Active shift** identifies a machine whose shifts follow machine activity. A shift starts at idle, production, or preparation and ends according to the machine's shift settings. Its assigned shift group is not used. See [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

**Waiting for the next active shift** or **waiting for machine start** means the next shift has not started yet. It starts automatically when the machine begins idle, production, or preparation. Until then, planned shifts from the assigned shift group are shown. This display does not switch off active shifts.
