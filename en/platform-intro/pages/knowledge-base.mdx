## Overview

Store and find operational knowledge, then review how documents relate to machines, orders, and products.

Users open it from the Workspace section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/knowledge-base`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Stored files, images, and written notes with processing status.
- Suggested and confirmed relationships with machines, orders, and products.
- A visual Knowledge Graph for exploring those relationships.
- Search and document review controls.

## What You Can Do

- Upload files and images or add written notes.
- Monitor document processing and search stored documents.
- Review, confirm, or reject suggested relationships.
- Explore confirmed and suggested links in the Knowledge Graph.

## Understand the Page

### Check Whether Knowledge Is Ready

After adding a file, image, or note, review its processing status. Uploading content and having it ready for use are separate stages: check readiness before expecting newly added knowledge to be available in AI conversations.

Review the document and its sources to understand what information is available. Ready knowledge can be used in AI chats, and voice input is available when you want to speak your input. Reopen saved conversations through [Chat History](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history).

### Review Document Connections

A relationship connects a document to a machine, order, or product. A suggested relationship is a connection for you to review; a confirmed relationship is one you have accepted. Confirm relevant suggestions and reject those that do not describe the document correctly.

The Knowledge Graph is a visual view of these connections. Use it to explore the documents related to a production item, while distinguishing suggestions from confirmed links.

## Main Entities

- Document
- Machine
- Production Order
- Product

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users build a searchable collection of processed operational documents.
- Users confirm useful relationships and explore them visually.

### Limits and Empty States
- Processing status determines when newly uploaded content is ready to review.
- The Knowledge Graph depends on suggested or confirmed relationships being available.
