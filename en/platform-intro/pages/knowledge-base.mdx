## Overview

Help users find reusable answers, prior investigations, and related operational knowledge.

Users open it from the Workspace section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Admin, Manager, Operator
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/knowledge-base`
</Callout>

Anyone who needs past guidance or linked context.

## What You Can See

- A list view of knowledge entries.
- A graph view that shows relationships between knowledge, machines, and orders.
- Search and refresh controls.

## What You Can Do

- Search for knowledge by title, description, tag, machine, or order context.
- Switch between list and graph views.
- Open or reuse a knowledge entry while investigating a problem.

## Main Entities

- Knowledge Entry
- Machine
- Production Order
- Tag

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users find a prior solution and avoid repeating the same investigation.

### Limits and Empty States
- The graph view depends on linked data being available.
- Search may return no results when wording differs from the stored entry.
