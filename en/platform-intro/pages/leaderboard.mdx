## Overview

Show ranked performance results so users can see leaders and laggards quickly.

Users open it from the main navigation when the role allows it.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/leaderboard`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Ranked views and leaderboard-style summaries.
- Tabs or filters that switch the ranking lens.

## What You Can Do

- Review who or what is leading on the selected metric.
- Change the ranking scope through the available filters or tabs.

## Main Entities

- Company
- Machine
- Rank

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users identify top performers and areas that need improvement attention.

### Limits and Empty States
- Rankings are only meaningful when enough comparable data exists.
