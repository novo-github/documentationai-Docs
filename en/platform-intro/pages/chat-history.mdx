## Overview

**Access:** Admin or Page Access `ai_chats`. Watchmen AI is Beta. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Let users revisit prior AI conversations and continue them when needed.

Users open it from the Workspace section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/aiChats`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- A list of recent and starred conversations.
- The full message thread for the selected conversation.
- Controls for search, renaming, starring, and deleting conversations.

## What You Can Do

- Start a new conversation.
- Continue an existing conversation.
- Search conversation titles.
- Rename, star, copy, or delete conversation entries.

## Watchmen AI Assistant Modes (Beta)

The **AI Assistant / Watchmen AI** is **Beta**. **Documentation only** uses platform documentation. **Context-aware** may also query live platform data in areas enabled by a company admin: Knowledge Base, Tickets, Machines and Production, Organization Statistics, Planning, and Alerts.

No platform data is shared with external AI models unless an admin enables it. Do not assume a documentation-only answer includes live machine readings. **Dictate** is voice input; **AI / KI** means artificial intelligence.

## Ask a Clear Question and Keep the Source

Include the exact label and context. For example, “What does PRO mean on the Hall Plan?” can be answered from the [glossary](/en/platform-intro/concepts/glossary). “Compare Machine A's idle time for this shift” needs the relevant context-aware data access.

For metric questions, include machine, date range, shift mode, and quantity source. Consult [calculations](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations) for confirmed definitions and limits. Search stored conversation titles, rename useful conversations descriptively, and star them for later retrieval.

## Main Entities

- Chat Conversation
- Message

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users resume earlier context instead of starting over.

### Limits and Empty States
- A new conversation has no history until the first message is sent.
