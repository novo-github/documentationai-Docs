## Overview

**Access:** Admin, or an account with **Assignment Planner** page access, plus the relevant machine access. **Improve plan** and **Re-sort** also need planning access: an Admin account, or a User with access to **Orders & Products**.

Assignment Gantt shows assignments across selected machines and time. Use it to inspect planned windows, spot overlaps, and open related orders.

Use the view switch to move between Assignment Planner and Assignment Gantt. Review dates in the timeline, use **Move** for eligible assignments, or return to the planner to work with queues.

## What Can I Change?

Use **Move** on an assignment to change eligible waiting work. With planning access, use **Improve plan → Save as plan** to review and save a different arrangement for the machines on the board. Reading the forecast or showing a preview changes nothing.

**Drag-to-reschedule is labelled Experimental and read-only.** Use the supported move and save controls described below to change the plan.

## Read the Planned Times

An assignment is one operation placed on a machine or hall pool. Its planned production time uses the quantity assigned to that machine. The forecast also uses remaining work and the machine’s capacity for simultaneous assignments. **Load** compares booked working time with shift time in the visible range. Without shift time, this share is unknown.

For example, 2 minutes per part × 50 assigned parts gives 100 planned production minutes, plus the operation's fixed preparation time. See [calculations](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations#deviations) and [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

## Understand the Forecaster

Your plan says when work should happen. The **Forecaster** shows when it is expected to happen, based on open orders, machines, shifts, and current production. It checks expected finishes against delivery dates so you can see which orders may be late, by how much, and why.

### Open the Help

Click the information icon beside **Forecaster** in the toolbar to open **How does the Forecaster work?**. You can also open it from the heading of **Attention** or **Improve plan**. Anyone who can access this page can read the help.

The same help is available on [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products), the [order page](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail), and in [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).

### How Expected Times Are Worked Out

- Running work continues from its current progress, using the quantity produced and time worked so far.
- Waiting jobs follow your plant's rule under **View options → Planning**: planned dates, queue order, or delivery date first. A job that takes longer pushes back the work behind it.
- Machines work within their planned shifts. Nights, weekends, and breaks without a shift are skipped.
- Each operation waits for the previous operation of its order to finish, even when it uses another machine.

The Forecaster updates by itself about every minute.

### Example

Order 4711 is due Thursday. Cutting on Laser 2 is planned for Tuesday, followed by bending on Press 1 on Wednesday. The job ahead on Laser 2 takes eight hours longer. Cutting moves to Wednesday and bending to Thursday. Press 1 has no late shift on Thursday, so bending now ends Friday at 10:00. The order shows **late +10 h**. The expected times have moved even though nobody changed the plan.

### What the Colours Mean

| Help label | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On time** | Green | Expected to finish before the date. |
| **At risk** | Amber | Not late yet, but the remaining work barely fits or an earlier operation is not planned. |
| **Late +3 h** | Red | Expected to finish three hours after the date. |
| **Late, date passed** | Red | The date has passed and the order is unfinished. |
| **Finish not known** | Blue | The date is still ahead, but no expected finish is available, for example because an operation is still in a hall pool. |

The **delivery date** is your promise to the customer; missing it is red. The **planned end** is the finish in your own plan; missing it is amber. See the [Forecaster glossary](/en/platform-intro/concepts/glossary#forecaster) for the renamed labels.

### When Times May Be Too Early

The Forecaster only knows what is recorded in the Watchmen Platform. Expected times can be too early when:

- An operation has no machine yet. Its work is treated as done when working out the expected times.
- A job has no planned duration. One hour is assumed.
- A machine has no shift plan. It is treated as available around the clock.
- Work is not started, paused, and finished on the PDA/BDE as it happens.

It cannot know about missing material, a broken tool, or someone off sick. Check these conditions before relying on an expected finish.

### Your Plan Stays Under Your Control

The Forecaster never changes your plan by itself. Dates and queues change only when someone moves a job or selects **Improve plan → Save as plan**. These changes can be undone. Delivery dates are never changed by the Forecaster.

## Compare Expected Times and Planned Dates

Open **View options → Board → Bars show → Expected times** to see when each job is expected to run, including delays. Choose **Planned dates** to see each job at its planned time. Open the information icon beside **Bars show** for an explanation. These are your own display choices and take effect at once.

If expected times are unavailable, jobs appear at their planned times without delays. The Forecaster tries again by itself. If some times are still being worked out, they are close but may move by a few minutes.

## Review Attention and Waiting Work

**Attention** lists what is expected to go wrong on the displayed machines, worst first. Its count covers last week through three weeks ahead, plus any week you have scrolled to. [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products) counts only orders late for delivery across the whole company, so the numbers can differ.

**Times may be too early** means you should review missing machines or dates before relying on the displayed finish. When no problems are expected, the panel shows **All good: nothing is expected to go wrong on these machines.**

Choose **By order** to review affected orders or **By cause** to find assignments holding up other work. Open a warning and use **Show the assignment it is waiting for** to trace a delay. Notes distinguish **Measured against the delivery date**, **Measured against the original plan**, and **Measured against the plan in Watchmen**.

- **On hold for a long time** (previously **On hold too long**): the hold limit has passed. The forecast assumes the operation continues in its queue position, so later work still waits for it.
- **Never started**: this note appears only with **Follow planned dates** or **Delivery date first** under **View options → Planning** in Assignment Gantt; work planned more than three days ago is forecast to start as soon as its machine is free. With **Follow the queue** or **Follow the queue, not before the planned date**, neither this note nor **No planned start** appears, and the operation keeps its place in the queue.
- **No planned start**: this note appears only with **Follow planned dates** or **Delivery date first**. The forecast places the operation after the machine’s dated work.
- **Waits for an earlier operation**: that operation is not on a machine yet or is still in a hall pool. Expected times may be too optimistic.

These notes explain the expected times. They do not remove the work from the forecast or mean that it is finished.

### Read Machine Availability

The machine summary shows the remaining work booked by the forecast. If an assignment has no expected end, the displayed free time is only the earliest possible time. The machine may be busy for longer. Long holds, missed starts, and missing planned starts now keep a forecast with assumption notes.

## Follow a Whole Order

For handed-over work, the order route shows **Partly done:** with good parts made and where the rest is waiting. Once finished, **Done on** lists the machines and their quantities. The original machine's assignment shows **Handed over** in its tooltip and details. Handover details show where the remainder went or where it came from. Recorded times stay on the machine that did the work. To hand work over, use [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner#hand-over-work); this action is not available in Gantt.

Select **Order view** and type at least two characters of the order name or number. **Show the whole order on the board** highlights its assignments and outlines work it is waiting for. Other work is dimmed. **Show operations** lists its route; **Back to machines** returns to the machine view.

An operation in a hall pool is not yet on a machine. A missing expected end means the order will finish later than shown. If only part of the route can be shown, the page says so. Open **Earlier operation not planned** and choose **Assign to a machine** when available. The operation keeps its own planned dates and the later operations are forecast again. Without a planned start, assigning a machine alone still leaves the operation unplanned.

## Move Waiting Work

Open an assignment and choose **Move → Earlier**, **Later**, **Run next**, or **To machine…**. Check its expected start and end, the other assignments delayed, and the number of late orders before saving. The review also shows how many assignments on that machine will be replanned.

Running, on-hold, and finished assignments cannot move this way. Work already recorded prevents changing the machine. A move cannot put an operation before an earlier operation of the same order on that machine. When the plant orders work by delivery date, you can change the machine but cannot reorder the queue by hand. Claim hall-pool work in the Assignment Planner.

Use **Undo** to reverse a move. If the queue has changed since, automatic undo is refused; move the work back by hand after reviewing the current queue.

## Compare Queue Orders Before Saving

With planning access, open the sparkle icon **Improve plan** beside **Forecaster** in the toolbar to compare plans for the machines on the board. The sparkle on the last line of a machine row starts with **This machine** and also offers **All machines in &lt;hall&gt;**. The dialog is the same as in Assignment Planner.

Under **1 · Choose an option**, compare **Best operation sequence** with the four fixed rules. Each shows late orders and delay after saving against today. **Today** marks the company’s current rule; **Best result** marks an improvement. Within **Best operation sequence**, choose **Keep priorities**, **Least delay**, or **Fewest changes**. The search keeps running and on-hold operations in place. Clear **Can move** to fix other operations at their exact places. Fixed rules do not offer these checkboxes.

Under **2 · What changes**, review new places, remaining work, and **Expected end** before and after. Choose **Columns** for optional details. Click a row for its forecast explanation, planned end, delivery date, and full verdict. Close the details with the close button or by clicking outside. **Show on board** previews an alternative fixed rule without saving.

Review changes on other machines, priority changes, and any planned-date count. More planned dates can change than operations move. When work waits behind an unplanned earlier operation, the dialog shows **N operations wait behind an operation that isn't planned.** with an information icon. Those waiting operations cannot be moved, and their times are too optimistic. Planning the earlier operations helps most.

With **Follow planned dates** or **Follow the queue, not before the planned date** as the company’s forecast rule, saving **Best operation sequence** also sets new planned dates for waiting operations on the reordered machines, so the forecast follows the new sequence. The selected option shows how many planned dates change. With **Follow the queue**, saving the sequence sets no planned dates; any operation-priority changes remain as shown.

With **Delivery date first**, users with planning access can still open **Improve plan** in Gantt and compare and save the fixed rules. **Best operation sequence** is greyed out and no search starts. Its explanation reads **Your forecast always sorts by delivery date, so a searched sequence can't change expected ends.** The Planner hides its sparkle under this rule.

Choose **Save as plan** to save the displayed changes. The searched sequence can change operation priorities when the company links priorities to queue order. It never changes order priorities. Saving keeps original operation dates, delivery dates, and the company’s forecast rule. **New plan saved** offers **Undo**, which restores the saved changes. If a conflict appears, the dialog stays open; use **Recalculate** and review again. After saving or undoing, the board refreshes. If **Undo** cannot be saved, the message **The old sequence could not be put back. The board now shows the current plan.** appears and the board reloads. Check the current plan before making another change.

For search choices and limits, see [Improve a Plan](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner#improve-a-plan). Follow [Compare and Save a Plan](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner#compare-and-save-a-plan).

### Re-sort One Machine

With planning access, choose **Re-sort** beside the sparkle on the last line of a machine row. Pick **Order priority**, **Operation priority**, **Planned end**, **Delivery date**, or **Order date**. Clear **Can move** for operations that must keep their place. Review, then choose **Apply sequence**. **Undo** restores the previous sequence; the board refreshes after either action. If **Undo** cannot be saved, the message **The old sequence could not be put back. The board now shows the current plan.** appears before the board reloads. Review its current order. Unlike the searched sequence, re-sorting can include running and on-hold work. See [Re-sort a Queue](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner#re-sort-a-queue).

## View Options: Board and Planning

The tune icon in the Gantt toolbar opens **View options**. Its tooltip has the same name. The separate **Planning settings** button, previously available only to Admins, is gone. **View options → Board** contains your own display choices, which apply at once. **View options → Planning** contains the company settings.

Only Admins can change **View options → Planning → How does your plant decide what runs next?** and choose **Save**. This rule is used by the forecast and delay notifications. Comparing or previewing alternatives does not change it.

Everyone else who can open Gantt can read the company settings but cannot change them. The dialog says **Ask an admin to change these.** If the board shows a preview, the note reads **The board is showing a preview: &lt;rule&gt;.** with the rule’s name.

| Ordering | Effect on waiting assignments |
| --- | --- |
| **Follow planned dates** | Runs by planned start; no assignment starts before that date. |
| **Follow the queue** | Uses the Assignment Planner’s queue; planned dates are ignored, so work may start earlier. |
| **Follow the queue, not before the planned date** | Keeps queue order but waits for each planned start. A later-dated assignment can hold up everything behind it. |
| **Delivery date first** | Orders work that can still arrive on time by delivery date. Already-late work goes first only if it does not make other work late. |

**Hold limit (working hours)** determines when **On hold for a long time** appears. It does not remove work from the forecast or free its machine. The operation keeps its queue position and is expected to continue there. Enter a duration between 1 minute and 720 hours. Only the machine’s working time counts; a weekend without shifts uses none. Where no shifts are planned, 24 hours count as the full limit.

For example, with an 8-hour limit on a machine working 8 hours a day, an operation paused on Monday at 10:00 gets the note on Tuesday at 10:00.

For machine capacity, administrators set **Jobs in parallel** in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

## View Controls and Limits

Select up to 25 machines. You can tick several machines or a hall group while the selection menu is open. Close it to apply the selection and update the timeline. Clearing the selection while the menu is closed takes effect immediately. Gantt has no hall-pool columns; use [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner) for pools.

Use **View options → Board → Bars show** to choose **Expected times** or **Planned dates**. Compare **Planned (original)**, **Planned (Watchmen)**, **Expected**, and **Actual** in the assignment details. **Row height** offers **Normal**, **Compact**, and **Large**. Both display choices are your own and take effect immediately. Drag to pan, use Ctrl with the mouse wheel to zoom, or press N to return to now.

**Forecaster unavailable** means you see planned times without calculated delays. **Forecaster: approximate** means expected times may still shift slightly. **Incomplete** means some assignments could not be loaded; select fewer machines.

Without a shift plan for the displayed period, the machine is assumed to be available around the clock. This also applies when shifts follow machine activity or have not yet been planned that far ahead. Check this assumption before relying on the finish.
