## Overview
Help users begin the password recovery process.

Users follow the forgot-password path from sign-in or a direct link.

## Access
<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Any User
- Visibility: Public
- Primary routes: `/forgotPassword`
</Callout>

Users who cannot sign in with their current password.

## What You Can See
- Recovery inputs and submission actions.

## What You Can Do
- Request the next step in password reset.

## Main Entities
- User

## Outcomes and Limits
### Common Outcomes
- The user moves into the reset flow.

### Limits and Empty States
- Recovery only works for recognized accounts.
