## Overview
Let users enter the platform with their account credentials.

Users open the sign-in route directly or are redirected there when not authenticated.

## Access
<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Any User
- Visibility: Public
- Primary routes: `/signin`
</Callout>

Anyone who needs platform access.

## What You Can See
- Credential inputs and sign-in actions.
- Access links for other account-recovery steps.

## What You Can Do
- Sign in.
- Move to password reset or other auth-related pages.

## Main Entities
- User

## Outcomes and Limits
### Common Outcomes
- Users enter the protected platform routes.

### Limits and Empty States
- Invalid credentials block access until corrected.
