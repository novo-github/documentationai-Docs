## Overview

Give users a place to review their account information and manage signed-in devices.

Users open it from profile-related navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/user`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Personal account information, including your phone number and its verification.
- Active sessions with device, browser, operating system, IP address, location, and login time.

## What You Can Do

- Review your profile details.
- Add, verify, change, or remove a phone number.
- Sign out an individual device.
- Sign out all other active sessions.

## Verify Your Phone Number

Verification confirms the phone number on your account using a code sent by SMS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a code" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open your **Profile**, enter your phone number, and select **Request Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the number" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Enter the six-digit code received by SMS and confirm it to verify the number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the SMS does not arrive, wait for the short waiting period to end before requesting another code.

To remove or change the number, select **Remove phone** on the same page. To use a different number, add it and repeat the verification steps.

## Review and End Active Sessions

A session represents a sign-in to your account on a device or browser. Review the device, browser, operating system, IP address, location, and login time to recognize your active sign-ins.

Use the sign-out action for an individual session when you no longer need it or do not recognize it. To end all sessions except the one you are currently using, choose the option to sign out all other active sessions. This lets you keep working on your current device while ending the other sign-ins.

## Main Entities

- User
- Session
- Device

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users confirm their account information and review where their account is signed in.
- Users end an individual session or all sessions except the current one.

### Limits and Empty States
- Session location and device details depend on the information available for each login.
