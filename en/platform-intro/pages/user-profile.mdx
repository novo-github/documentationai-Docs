## Overview

**Access:** Every signed-in role for their own account. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Give users a place to review their account information and manage signed-in devices.

Users open it from profile-related navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/user`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Personal account information, including your phone number and its verification.
- Active sessions with device, browser, operating system, IP address, location, and login time.

## What You Can Do

- Review your profile details.
- Add, verify, change, or remove a phone number.
- Sign out an individual device.
- Sign out all other active sessions.

## Choose Your Newsletter Report Version

Open your profile → **Newsletter Preferences** card → **Newsletter report version**. Choose the **Version 1** or **Version 2** tab. **What you get** describes the selected version; **Download an example** lets you compare a workbook with made-up data before choosing.

**Version 1** is the default. **Version 2** starts with what needs attention and adds target and shift comparisons, cycles, downtime reasons, orders and planned operations for the next 7 days. See [Report Versions](/en/platform-intro/pages/report-versions) for the contents and limits.

Select **Save Preferences** at the top right of the **Newsletter Preferences** card header. The button is available when you have changed a preference. Wait for saving to finish before changing tabs again. The saved choice applies to both weekly and monthly report emails from the next report; keep the subscriptions you want enabled. You can switch back at any time.

The export dialog on the Timeline Analysis page starts with this saved choice, but lets you choose another version for one export. If you receive the introduction email, you can also confirm a version through it without signing in. Follow [choose your report version](/en/platform-intro/workflows/reports#choose-email-version).

## Verify Your Phone Number

Verification confirms the phone number on your account using a code sent by SMS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a code" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open your **Profile**, enter your phone number, and select **Request Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the number" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Enter the six-digit code received by SMS and confirm it to verify the number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the SMS does not arrive, wait for the short waiting period to end before requesting another code.

To remove or change the number, select **Remove phone** on the same page. To use a different number, add it and repeat the verification steps.

## Review and End Active Sessions

A session represents a sign-in to your account on a device or browser. Review the device, browser, operating system, IP address, location, and login time to recognize your active sign-ins.

Use the sign-out action for an individual session when you no longer need it or do not recognize it. To end all sessions except the one you are currently using, choose the option to sign out all other active sessions. This lets you keep working on your current device while ending the other sign-ins.

## Password, Email, and Two-Factor Authentication

Your profile also covers password, email, **2FA / OTP**, notifications, and newsletter settings. Follow [Set Up Two-Factor Authentication](/en/platform-intro/workflows/two-factor-authentication) for email or authenticator codes. Authenticator setup requires both QR scanning and code confirmation; incomplete setup can prevent the next sign-in.

Use [Change Your Email Address](/en/platform-intro/workflows/change-email) to request a new address and confirm it. The old address stays active until the new one is verified. **Trust this device for next 30 days** skips the second authentication step on that device for 30 days.

## Request and Confirm an Email Change

Open **Profile → Change email**. In **Change email address**, review **Current email**, enter **New email**, and enter your current password if requested. Select **Send verification**. Your current address stays active until you open the link at the new address and select **Confirm email change**. Then sign in with the new email.

**Email change waiting for confirmation** shows the address that still needs confirmation. Select **Resend** when the displayed wait has ended, or **Cancel change** to stop the request. If a link is invalid, expired, or replaced, request a new one from your profile or your administrator. Follow the [email change workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/change-email).

## Distinguish Machine States with Patterns

**Accessibility patterns** in your profile also applies to the [Machine History](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-history) state row and its legend. Idle uses dots and off uses diagonal lines alongside their colours. You can review the pattern preview in the [Machines legend](/en/platform-intro/pages/machines).

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users confirm their account information and review where their account is signed in.
- Users end an individual session or all sessions except the current one.

### Limits and Empty States
- Session location and device details depend on the information available for each login.
