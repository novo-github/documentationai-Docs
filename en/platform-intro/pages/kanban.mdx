## Overview

**Access:** Admin or Page Access `tickets`. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Give users a shared board for operational tickets and support work.

Users open it from the Workspace section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected

</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Status columns such as open, in progress, resolved, and closed.
- Ticket cards with searchable ticket details.
- Ownership filters and quick-create controls.

## What You Can Do

- Search tickets.
- Create new tickets.
- Move tickets between columns.
- Open a ticket card to review or update it.

<a id="ticket-panel" />

### Fill In and Edit a Ticket

In a new ticket, use **Orders** to link work to the issue. Type at least 2 characters of the order number, then choose a matching order. This field is available when creating a ticket; it is not shown when editing an existing ticket.

Use the microphone inside the name or description field to dictate into that field. The words are added at the end. The name wraps across several lines but stays one paragraph. The person who created the ticket can also dictate when editing its name or description. Only one dictation can run in the panel at a time. Creating or saving waits until it has finished. Each recording stops after 2 minutes and keeps what you said as text. The timer warns you in the last 15 seconds.

The AI assistant can open a new ticket with the name, description, people, machines, orders, tags and due date filled in. Check the details and press **Create Ticket**. To describe a whole ticket by voice, use the microphone on the PDA page's **Add Ticket** card; follow [create a ticket by voice](/en/platform-intro/workflows/pda-ticket-voice).

In an existing ticket, choose **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed** in the status tabs. Choosing a status saves it. The tabs wait while that change is being saved.

<a id="unsaved-changes" />

### Close a Ticket with Unsaved Input

If you close the panel, open the chat or switch to another ticket with unsaved input, the Watchmen Platform asks what to do:

- **Create this ticket?** for a new ticket: choose **Create ticket** to create it, **Discard** to remove your input, or **Keep editing** to return to it.
- **Save your changes?** for an existing ticket: choose **Save** to keep your changes, **Discard** to return to the saved values, or **Keep editing** to continue.

Closing the question with ×, Esc or a click outside keeps you editing. With no changes, the panel closes without asking. While dictation is running, creating and saving wait; **Discard** stops the recording and removes the unsaved input. Discarded input cannot be restored from this dialog.

If a new ticket has no name, **Please give the ticket a name.** appears and the panel stays open. Enter a name and create it again.

## Ticket Types and States

Ticket states are **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**, **Closed**, and **Archived**. Types are **Internal** and **External/Support**. Tickets can link to machines and can be moved into the Knowledge Base so their information can be retained as operational knowledge.

The ticket board is separate from [Feedback to Novo AI](/en/platform-intro/pages/feedback), which has its own conversation states. Use [Manage Kanban Tickets](/en/platform-intro/workflows/kanban-ticket-management) for board work.

## Main Entities

- Ticket
- Assignee
- Tag
- Due Date

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users keep active issues organized and visible to the team.

### Limits and Empty States
- Filters can narrow the board more than expected.
- Closed work may need to be reopened instead of recreated.
