## Overview

**Access:** Admin or Page Access `tickets`. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Give users a shared board for operational tickets and support work.

Users open it from the Workspace section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/ticket-kanban`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Status columns such as open, in progress, resolved, and closed.
- Ticket cards with searchable ticket details.
- Ownership filters and quick-create controls.

## What You Can Do

- Search tickets.
- Create new tickets.
- Move tickets between columns.
- Open a ticket card to review or update it.

## Ticket Types and States

Ticket states are **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**, **Closed**, and **Archived**. Types are **Internal** and **External/Support**. Tickets can link to machines and can be moved into the Knowledge Base so their information can be retained as operational knowledge.

The ticket board is separate from [Feedback to Novo AI](/en/platform-intro/pages/feedback), which has its own conversation states. Use [Manage Kanban Tickets](/en/platform-intro/workflows/kanban-ticket-management) for board work.

## Main Entities

- Ticket
- Assignee
- Tag
- Due Date

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users keep active issues organized and visible to the team.

### Limits and Empty States
- Filters can narrow the board more than expected.
- Closed work may need to be reopened instead of recreated.
