## Overview

**Access:** Admin or Page Access `reports_timeline`, with relevant Machine Access. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Show how machine time is distributed across states and timeline categories.

Users open it from the Reports section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/reports/timeline-analysis`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- Date and machine filters.
- Timeline-focused charts and summaries.
- Shift-aware reporting views.

In **Reports → Timeline**, a period of several days now includes time since the last state change, just like [**Machine History**](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-history). After more than 20 minutes without a sensor signal, the last state counts only until the last signal; for a sensor powered by the machine, the silent time counts as **Off**.

## What You Can Do

- Narrow the analysis to specific machines and dates.
- Compare how time was spent across machine states or reasons.
- Use the page to spot recurring downtime patterns.

When you request an export, the report may take time to create. If it takes longer than usual, Administrators can find it in **Report archive** once it is ready. Open the archive with the button next to the **Timeline** / **Trend** switch at the top of the page. Other users do not see the archive and should try the export again later. See [Download and Update Reports](/en/platform-intro/workflows/reports).

In the archive, **Revision** identifies an updated report, while **Version 2** beside the period identifies its layout. Point to either for an explanation. Downloads always contain the latest revision; emails already sent are not sent again. See [Report Archive](/en/platform-intro/pages/report-archive).

## Choose a Version for an Export

Open Timeline Analysis through **Reports** in the side menu. Choose machines and dates, then select **Generate report → Download Report (XLSX)**. The dialog now asks everyone who can export to choose **Report version**. Your current newsletter preference is selected each time it opens; without a saved choice, it starts with **Version 1**.

Select **Version 1** or **Version 2**, compare **What you get**, or use **Download an example** for made-up data. Select **Generate report** to create the selected workbook. Follow the progress in the step list.

Your choice applies to this export only. Version 2 includes cycles, downtime reasons, and orders and operations worked on in the selected period. Version 2 also separates work centers from machine figures and shows **Production without an order** when your company recorded at least 5 intervals of real operations in the report period, excluding test orders. Work centers are excluded from that measure. With fewer than 5 intervals, order figures and the orders sheet are also absent. **Get more out of this report with the PDA page** on the first page explains why and how recording operations on the PDA page helps. Test orders are excluded from report lists and counts. Exports have no outlook for planned work. See [Report Versions](/en/platform-intro/pages/report-versions) or follow [create an export](/en/platform-intro/workflows/reports#create-export).

## States, Reasons, and Duration Buckets

**Timeline Analysis** examines the state timeline of a period; **Trend Analysis** compares KPI trends across days. Switch between the Reports views and match their machine, date, and shift scope.

Downtime is Idle (orange) or Off (red). **Downtime Analysis (labeled)** groups it by assigned reason. An unlabeled warning means a reason is still missing. **Timeline ranges / Time range** are duration buckets, such as 1–5 or 5–30 minutes, used in **Idletime Distribution**. **Focus Mode** enlarges the analysis by hiding surrounding controls.

Automatic timeline reasons follow the specific reason rule, its DEFAULT rule, then Global rules. Admin-defined reason type and level 1–5 effects are unconfirmed. The [calculations reference](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations) explains AVL, PRO, and the role of the selected period.

## Read Downtime Reason Codes

Downtime chart labels include a reason's code when one is set. Short labels may be cut off; hover over a bar or pie slice to read the full name, code, and category. Categories help identify reasons; the charts still group by reason.

See [downtime reason settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel) for how administrators maintain these labels.
