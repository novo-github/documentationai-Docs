## Overview

Show how machine time is distributed across states and timeline categories.

Users open it from the Reports section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Admin, Manager, Operator
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/reports/timeline-analysis`
</Callout>

Managers, analysts, and advanced operators.

## What You Can See

- Date and machine filters.
- Timeline-focused charts and summaries.
- Shift-aware reporting views.

## What You Can Do

- Narrow the analysis to specific machines and dates.
- Compare how time was spent across machine states or reasons.
- Use the page to spot recurring downtime patterns.

## Main Entities

- Machine
- Shift
- Timeline Event
- Timeline Reason

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users identify which time categories are taking too much of the day.

### Limits and Empty States
- Sparse data windows can make the report less useful.
