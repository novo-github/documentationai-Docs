## Overview

**Access:** Any role with access to this machine. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Support live shop-floor execution and event recording for a single machine.

Users usually open it from the Machine Details page.

To switch machines on the PDA page, select the machine name in the top navigation bar. The list groups accessible machines by hall and marks the current machine with a check. With more than 8 machines, use **Search machines…**. Choose with ↑/↓ and Enter, or close with Esc. Selecting another machine opens its PDA page directly.

## What You Can See

- Assignment lists and live order context in open-assignment or time-window views.
- Personalized assignment columns, ordering, and sorting.
- Start, preparation, and stop controls.
- Interval history, notes, tickets, and knowledge context.

## What You Can Do

- Start or stop work.
- Record produced quantity, material scrap, and work scrap when stopping or continuing an interval.
- Create or use live orders.
- Search for unplanned operations and assign them directly to the machine.
- Configure the information and ordering shown on assignment cards.
- Apply downtime reasons and review related support content.

If you have [AI assistant access](/en/platform-intro/pages/chat-history), you can attach files from its side panel or floating window on the PDA page. Use the paperclip, drop files onto the chat or paste a screenshot. For example, send a photo of a broken part with your question. The limit is 5 files per message, each up to 25 MB. Follow [ask the assistant about a file](/en/platform-intro/workflows/chat-attachments).

## Create and Review Tickets at the Machine

Open **Tickets** to review machine issues. Status tabs show **Open / In Progress / Resolved / Closed**. Tap the **Add Ticket** card to open an empty new ticket.

### Describe a Ticket by Voice

**Access:** You need AI assistant access and permission to create tickets. The machine's BDE access template must allow [**Fill in tickets with AI**](/en/platform-intro/pages/bde-access-templates#pda-ai). This enables the microphone in the card's corner. Without a template, it is not available.

Tap that microphone to start recording. Say what happened, which machine, who should take care of it and by when. Stop when finished. In **Your words**, correct the text or use **Dictate more** to add to it. Choose **Fill with AI** to open the new-ticket panel with the title, description, people, machines, orders, tags and due date filled in. Tapping elsewhere on the card still opens an empty ticket.

A sparkle marks a field filled by AI; a pencil appears after you change it. A **Not found:** message names people, machines, orders or tags that could not be matched. Choose the right ones in the ticket. Check every filled-in detail before pressing **Create Ticket**. Nothing is created until you do.

If your words describe several tickets, only the first is filled in. After you create it, tap the microphone again to open **Your words** with the rest. Follow [create a ticket by voice](/en/platform-intro/workflows/pda-ticket-voice).

Each filling in counts as one AI credit, like a **Fast** answer. When credits are used up, fill in the ticket yourself. If filling in fails, the pop-up keeps your words so you can correct them or try again.

Every recording on this page stops after 2 minutes and keeps what you said as text. The timer warns you in the last 15 seconds. This also applies to dictation in the knowledge tab. See [ticket editing and unsaved changes](/en/platform-intro/pages/kanban#ticket-panel).

## Save and Find Knowledge at the Machine

**Access:** The **Knowledge Base** tab appears when you have Knowledge Base page access and access to this machine. Entries added here go into the knowledge base instead of becoming tickets.

### Add a Note, Photo or File

In **Add to knowledge base**, type or dictate what others should know. Use **Take or add a photo** to add a picture; on tablets, it opens the camera. Use **Add a file** for a document.

Choose the relevant order in the order picker. **Running now** is pre-selected when an operation is running. You can also choose work marked **Up next** or **In the pool**, or **No order**. The product follows the chosen order.

Check **Machine**, **Order** and **Product** above the box. The machine always stays linked. Use the remove control with **Do not link** on the order or product to leave that link out. For example, keep only the machine for a tip that applies to every order.

Choose **Save**. Your text becomes a note; each photo or file becomes its own entry with the same links. New entries appear at the top while they are read, then show a title and summary.

Entries use **Whole company** visibility and follow machine access. Everyone in your company who may see this machine can find them here, on the Knowledge Base page and through the AI assistant. Admins find them on the Knowledge Base page; these entries send no Admin email. For other ways to add or share documents, see [visibility and sharing](/en/platform-intro/pages/knowledge-base#visibility).

### Find, Read and Correct an Entry

**Knowledge for this machine** shows the newest entries first, with photo previews. Use **This machine**, **This order** and **This product** to show or hide entries for the selected work. Use **Search** to find an entry and **Load more** to see older ones.

Tap an entry to open its preview. **Open in knowledge base** opens the same entry on the Knowledge Base page in a new browser tab.

The person who added the entry or an Admin can choose **Edit** to change **Title**, **Type**, **Summary** and **Note**. For notes written in this tab, you can also change **Text**. You can dictate into the text, summary and note boxes. Older notes and uploaded files allow changes to their details, but their text cannot be changed here. Choose **Save** when finished.

### Ask the AI Assistant Here

**Ask AI** appears only when you have AI assistant access and the machine's template allows **Ask AI in the knowledge base tab**. Write your question in the same box and add a photo or file if useful. The assistant uses the machine, chosen order and product to understand your question. The answer appears below the box.

- Tap a source to read it in the tab.
- Send a follow-up or select an offered option to continue the same conversation.
- Choose **Keep going** when offered to let the assistant continue.
- Review any prepared change before choosing **Confirm** or **Cancel** on its card.
- Choose **New question** to start a new conversation.

Answers keep you on the PDA page. **Open in chat** opens the conversation on the AI chats page in a new browser tab. See the [existing chat file guide](/en/platform-intro/workflows/chat-attachments) for using files in other chat views.

Follow [save and use knowledge on the PDA page](/en/platform-intro/workflows/pda-knowledge).

## Settings Set by a Machine Template

An administrator can assign a [BDE access template](/en/platform-intro/pages/bde-access-templates) to this machine. It applies to everyone on Data Acquisition V3, including administrators.

In **Card Display Settings**, required fields and highlights stay ticked and locked with **Set by the template**. Required highlights apply only when a field is visible. You can still choose additional fields and highlights. **Sorting is set by the template** means you can review the sort order but cannot change or reset it here. A fixed automatic refresh interval is also locked.

Your own choices stay saved. They apply again when the template no longer fixes those settings, and on machines without a template. Without a template, AI in the knowledge tab and when creating tickets from this page is off. The sorting instructions below apply when the template leaves sorting open.

The template can block order creation, taking or returning pool work, reopening completed operations, interval corrections, and saving order notes or details. Their buttons and edit icons stay visible and readable, but disabled. Hover over or tap one to read **Locked for this machine (BDE template).** Notes and details stay readable only; **Save** remains visible even when nothing has changed. A blocked status still looks normal, but tapping it opens no menu. If adding orders is off, search still filters this machine’s work but cannot find unplanned operations. Ask an administrator to review a blocked action.

Preparation, production start, stop, quantity booking, downtime reasons, **PDA Assist**, and warnings stay available as before. Even with status changes off, **Planned quantity reached** still lets you choose **Complete** or **Keep on hold**. Reopening completed work has its own **Reopen completed operations** switch. Other pages, such as the Planner, are unaffected.

Under **AI on the BDE page**, the template separately allows **Ask AI in the knowledge base tab** and **Fill in tickets with AI**. Both are off by default. Saving in the knowledge tab stays available with Knowledge Base access. These choices do not affect other pages.

Template changes appear at the next automatic refresh, every 5 minutes unless another interval is set. Use the page’s refresh button or reload the page to pick them up immediately. Other devices do not update instantly; a screen left open keeps the old choices until one of these happens.

## Choosing and Understanding Work

Use open-assignment or time-window views to find the work for the current machine. If an operation has not been planned, you can search for it and assign it directly from this page. Operation sequence numbers on job chips help identify the order of work, and operation and assignment notes provide instructions and context.

In Data Acquisition V3, **Sort by Queue Position** displays the order set by dragging assignments. Turn it off to display work by rules such as priority or date. For these rules, choose ascending or descending order. The rules form a numbered list: rule 1 decides first, and later rules break ties. To change their precedence, change the selection in a line’s list, remove a rule with the red remove button, or add a line with **Add sort rule**. These general sorting choices are also available in the [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).

### Choose Where Running and On-Hold Orders Appear

**Running and on-hold orders**: Orders in progress or on hold. Example: an order that was started and is on hold for a tool change. This differs from ⚠️ **Waiting on a previous operation**, where an earlier operation is unfinished.

Open **Card Display Settings → Sort Order** using the gear icon. Your sorting mode determines the choices:

- With **Sort by Queue Position** on, **Running and on-hold orders** appears above the waiting-order choice. Previously, these orders stayed on top automatically. They now keep their place in the queue by default with **Keep in place**. Choose **Running and on-hold orders → At the top** to bring them above other orders. In Assignment Planner, queue-position mode follows the dragged order, including running and on-hold work. The PDA page’s choices can still show these groups first.
- With **Sort by Queue Position** off, use **Add sort rule** and select **Running and on-hold orders**. Choose **At the top** or **At the bottom**. This rule stays first. The waiting-order rule comes next if you add it, followed by priority and date rules.

Select **Save** to apply the display choices to the machine's order list and **Pool**. These choices are separate from the company sorting default. Follow the [display setup steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#configure-the-data-acquisition-v3-display).

### Put Waiting Orders After Work That Can Start

Orders marked ⚠️ **Waiting on a previous operation** have an earlier operation in the same work order that is neither completed nor cancelled. Operation numbers determine the sequence. An earlier operation without an assignment also counts as unfinished. For example, operation #70 waits until #60 is finished.

Use the gear icon in the top bar to open **Card Display Settings → Sort Order**. Choose the option for your sorting mode:

- With **Sort by Queue Position** off, click **Add sort rule**. A new numbered rule line appears. Select **Waiting on a previous operation** in that line’s list. Instead of the up/down arrow, this rule has a button that starts at **At the bottom**, placing waiting orders after other work. Click it to switch to **At the top** and review waiting orders first. This rule comes before priority and date, but after **Running and on-hold orders** when that rule is present. Your remaining rules sort work within each group.
- With **Sort by Queue Position** on, use **Orders waiting on a previous operation**. **Keep in place** keeps the usual queue display. **At the bottom** places waiting work after other orders. Use the separate **Running and on-hold orders** choice to bring active work to the top. The queue order set in Assignment Planner stays the same within each group.

Select **Save** to apply your choice. Neither option moves waiting work until you choose it. Your choice is saved for your account and applies to the machine's order list and the **Pool** tab. These waiting-order options are only available in Data Acquisition V3, not in Assignment Planner.

Once no earlier operation remains unfinished, the next refresh places the order in the group that can start. Follow [Configure the Data Acquisition V3 Display](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#configure-the-data-acquisition-v3-display).

### Use the Company Sorting Default

If you have no personal sorting saved for Data Acquisition V3, it starts with the company's sort mode and shared rules. Administrators set these in [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner). Saving unrelated display changes keeps you following that default.

When a company default exists, the reset control is labelled **Reset to company sorting**. Open **Card Display Settings → Sort Order → Reset to company sorting** to copy the current company choices. When your choices already match, the button shows a tick and is unavailable. Otherwise, it resets the choices in the open settings; select **Save** to apply them. With no company default, the icon labelled **Reset to default (no company default set yet)** restores the standard choices.

Resetting keeps the queue-mode choices for running and waiting orders. It replaces the sort rules, so add any running- or waiting-order rules you need again. These special rules are not part of the company default. Once you save your own sorting or a reset, later company changes do not replace it.

## Check Parts for an Operation

Open **Parts list** to the left of the card’s notes button. Its count is the number of open parts; the icon turns green when all are ticked. The small badge shows the revision of the product being made and turns blue for 24 hours after a new version replaces an earlier one. Without a revision, it shows **New** during that time. No list means no button. Pool cards and read-only completed cards have no button either.

Tap a part to tick it or remove its tick, or choose **Tick all**. You see progress, who ticked each part, and the date and time. Ticking is optional and never blocks production. Use the arrows beside the version number to inspect older versions; only the current version allows ticking.

A template can switch off **Tick off parts in the parts list**. The list stays readable, but parts cannot be ticked or reopened here. Editing the list itself requires an Admin in the Planner or order details. See [Parts Lists](/en/platform-intro/pages/parts-lists) for revision rules and follow [check parts](/en/platform-intro/workflows/parts-lists).

## Send Work Back to the Hall Pool

In Data Acquisition V3, you can return unfinished work to its own hall pool if you have access to this machine and its template allows **Send orders back to the pool**. Select the operation: **Create** changes to **Back to pool**. Tap the selected card again to clear the selection and restore **Create**.

For a running operation, **Back to pool** opens the normal stop dialog first. **Stop first – then back to pool** appears at the bottom. Record quantities and stop the interval. If **PDA Assist** asks you to choose a time, finish or close that choice before the return dialog opens. Closing the stop dialog or booking without stopping cancels this sequence.

In **Send back to the pool**:

- If work has not started, the whole operation moves to the pool.
- If work has started, recorded times and good parts stay on this machine. **Planned**, **Done here**, and **Left** summarize the quantities. **Quantity left** starts with planned quantity minus good parts, never below zero. Scrap does not reduce the remainder. Change it if parts need to be made again, or leave it empty if the quantity is unknown. It starts empty when the planned quantity is unknown.
- **Reason (optional)** passes a note of up to 500 characters to the next machine, such as “Spindle broken”.

Confirm with **Back to pool**. The work goes to the top of the hall pool, where another machine in the hall can claim it as usual. The list refreshes, the selection clears, and a confirmation appears at the bottom. After partial work, the original card shows **Handed over** instead of **Completed**. Choose the third view tab with the history icon, **Time Window + All Statuses**, to find the handed-over part. It shows all operations in the chosen date window, including completed and cancelled ones. **All Open** and **Time Window + Open** hide the handed-over part.

The pool card shows where the work came from and how many parts were already made, or who sent back an unstarted operation and when. Hover over its grey badge for details and the reason. Operation context and waiting-operation details show **Partly done:** with quantities and the remaining destination. Handing work over does not finish the whole operation or release later operations that are waiting for it.

Finished or cancelled operations cannot be sent back. Without a hall, the button is unavailable; ask your planner. If all planned parts are made, finish the operation unless more are needed. There is no automatic undo for a handover. If **This operation was changed in the meantime. The list has been refreshed.** appears, review the refreshed list before trying again. Follow [return work to the pool](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#back-to-pool).

## Interval Times and Timeline Display

If the machine’s template switches off **Correct interval times and quantities**, the interval list is readable only and the edit icons are disabled. Hover over or tap an icon to read **Locked for this machine (BDE template).** Start and end times stay plain text. The editing steps below apply when corrections are allowed.

An interval records a stretch of preparation or production. In Data Acquisition V3, open the order's interval list. Tap a start or end time once to open **Edit interval** on **Start** or **End**. The clock icon and **Tap to change** hint identify editable times. The pencil opens **Quantities**.

Times show hours and minutes. A time from another day also shows the date as day/month, for example 20/10 14:30. A running end reads **Still running**.

### Review and Change an Interval

The summary above the controls shows the order, interval number, saved type, times, and quantities. **Start** sits on the left and **End** on the right. The other time stays visible; tap its card to switch to it. Your edits stay when you switch tabs. Dots mark unsaved changes. When both times exist, changing either time shows the new duration beside **New length:**.

- On a touch screen, turn the day, hour, and minute wheels.
- On a computer, use **−** and **+**, hold a button to repeat, or type hours and minutes. You can also use the mouse wheel. Minute and hour steps carry into the next hour or day.
- **−5 min** and **+5 min** move the selected time by five minutes. Repeated taps add up.
- Under **Quantities**, correct **Good qty.**, **Material Scrap**, and **Work Scrap**. Type quantities with a keyboard on a computer or use the number pad on a touch screen. In cumulative mode, enter the full machine counter reading.
- Choose **Production** or **Preparation** to correct the interval type. The summary shows the proposed type beside the saved one.

On screens with little height, the summary at the top takes two lines and the number pad is smaller. On very short screens, such as 1024×600 screens, the wheels show fewer rows with **−5 min** and **+5 min** beside them, and the hint below the controls is hidden.

Select **Save** to apply your edits together. **Interval saved.** confirms success; the interval table and timeline update. **Cancel** discards edits before saving. If you already selected **Save**, cancelling does not undo a save that finishes afterwards; check the table.

### Limits When Editing

You can move the date back as far as seven days before the saved time. You cannot choose a time later than now.

When you change a time, the end must be later than the start, and neither time may be in the future. **Save** stays unavailable if nothing changed, a time error remains, or saving is still in progress. If saving fails, the dialog stays open with a message so you can try again.

For a running interval, **End** explains: **This interval is still running. Its end is set when it is stopped.** Use the normal stop action to end it. You cannot clear a saved end. A running interval cannot change to a type that already has another running interval on the same order; the unavailable option has an explanation.

Tapping outside does not close the dialog while you have unsaved changes. Follow [Correct an Interval](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#correct-an-interval).

### Timeline Display

Data Acquisition V3 settings offer two separate choices:

- **Timeline display → Show intervals in graph** controls whether interval bars appear on the graph.
- **Auto-refresh interval** controls how often assignments and hall pools refresh automatically.

Timeline entries for idle and stopped periods update as you switch between running, paused, and stopped states. Review these entries and their reasons to keep a clear record of interruptions. See [Run Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition) for the correction and display procedures.

## Production Quantities, Scrap, and Completion

When stopping or continuing an interval, enter good quantity, material scrap, and work scrap separately. Review the breakdown in [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products) or [Order Analysis](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail).

In the quantity dialog, tap the information icon in the corner to read what each button does:

| Button | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Cancel** | Closes the dialog without booking anything. |
| **Continue** | Books quantities, ends the running interval, and starts a new interval immediately. No completion question appears. |
| **Stop** | Books quantities, ends the running interval, and puts the operation on hold. If the planned quantity is reached, you choose whether to complete it. |

After **Stop**, **Planned quantity reached** appears when good parts plus material scrap, work scrap, and any older scrap reach or exceed the planned quantity across all intervals. Scrap counts once. For example, 97 good parts plus 3 material scrap reaches a plan of 100; 96 plus 3 does not. The question shows the order, operation sequence and product, with the same quantity readout as its card.

- **Complete** is preselected. Choose it when all parts have been booked; the operation leaves the open list.
- **Keep on hold** leaves the operation open so you can book missing parts later. Closing the question without choosing also keeps it on hold.

Reaching the quantity does not complete the operation automatically. If the planned quantity is zero or empty, the question appears after every **Stop**. Both choices work even when the template blocks ordinary status changes. Below the planned quantity, this question is not offered: ask your planner to close the operation, or use the status menu if your template allows it. If material runs out and work must continue elsewhere, use [Back to pool](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#back-to-pool).

If **PDA Assist** offers a time choice after stopping, finish or close it first; the completion question follows. During **Back to pool**, the return dialog takes its place. Closing that return dialog after stopping leaves the operation on hold.

Notices identify the operation. After **Continue**, you see **Interval ended – new interval started**. After stopping without a follow-up question, you see **Interval ended – on hold**, or **Interval ended** if it is not on hold. After the completion choice, **Operation completed** or **Operation on hold** confirms the result. Follow [Review Quantities and Completion](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#review-quantities-and-completion).

## PDA / BDE Buttons and Quantity Labels

**PDA (Production Data Acquisition)** and **BDE (Betriebsdatenerfassung)** are the same machine operator screen. Tabs include **Assignments**, **Pool**, **Downtime Reason**, **Tickets**, and **Knowledge Base**.

**Prep** starts preparation (German **Vorber.**), **Start** starts production, **Stop** ends an interval, and **Continue** books quantities, ends the running interval, and starts a new one immediately. Explicit actions include Start/End Preparation and Start/End Shift.

**Good qty. / Gutmenge** is good output. **Mat. / MA** is Material Scrap; **Work / AA** is Work Scrap. **Total = Input + MA + AA**. **Rework Qty** is the amount requiring rework. **Cycle Count** is sensor-detected; **PDA Entry / BDE-Eintrag** is entered by a machine operator. Switching the progress source changes only the display.

**Example:** 95 entered good units, 3 material scrap, and 2 work scrap produce a total of 100. In **Cumulative** mode, enter the full machine counter, for example 1450, rather than this interval's increment.

## Mid Booking, Hall Pools, and Dependencies

**Mid Booking (Zwischenbuchung)** saves quantities while the job continues by closing and restarting the interval. If restart fails, quantities stay saved; press **Start** to resume. Check the running state before booking again.

In the **Pool** tab, claim work from the machine's hall pool. If no assignment exists, search unplanned operations or use **Find/Create Immediate Order**. **Job Context** combines order, work-order, operation, and assignment notes.

In **Find/Create Immediate Order** on the PDA page, use **Search Product** to find a product by name or number.

**Waiting on a previous operation / Blocked by** means an earlier operation is unfinished. **Start anyway** remains available; review the dependency before proceeding. The concurrent-order prompt asks for confirmation if another assignment is running. When the planned quantity is reached after **Stop**, choose **Complete** or **Keep on hold**.

Unbound or unlabeled machine time belongs to no order. Deleting a recorded interval is irreversible.

### Review and Reopen Completed Work

On the PDA page, choose **Recently completed**, the fourth view mode. It shows only the last five completed operations of this machine, newest first. The time-window control is unavailable here. The view is remembered, and search filters these five cards. To review older work, use **Time Window + All Statuses**.

Cards follow your display settings and show booked quantities and **Completed** with the completion time; a missing time appears as “–”. The cards are read-only: you cannot select them, start or stop them, change their status from a menu, or edit their notes or intervals here. Switching to this view keeps any previously selected open operation selected; the page’s main controls still refer to that operation.

If the machine’s template allows **Reopen completed operations**, choose **Set on hold** on a card. In the confirmation, check the order, operation sequence and product. Under **Put in queue**, choose:

| Choice | Where the operation returns |
| --- | --- |
| **By company sorting** | Uses the company’s saved priority and date rules. **Company sorting:** shows those rules below the choices. It places the operation behind the last active operation that ranks before it, never ahead of running work. Other operations keep their relative order. |
| **At the top** | Returns it to the top of this machine’s queue. |
| **At the bottom** | Returns it to the bottom of this machine’s queue. |

Your last successful choice is remembered. Otherwise, **By company sorting** is selected when company sorting exists, and **At the top** when it does not. Without saved company sorting, **By company sorting** is unavailable. If that sorting is removed while the confirmation is open, the choice falls back to **At the top**. Company rules update when the page refreshes and when you open the confirmation.

Confirm with **Set on hold**. To leave the operation completed, tap outside the confirmation. Reopening changes its queue placement without changing priorities, dates, or the saved company sorting. Your display sorting can affect where the card appears.

After reopening, the card leaves this view. Choose **Show** in the notice to switch to **All Open** and select it. You can then book more parts. **Reopened** with the time appears on PDA and Planner cards. Its explanation names the person, date, time, machine when available, and queue placement. The badge disappears when the operation is completed or cancelled again.

**No completed operations yet** means this machine has none to show. Refresh if the list cannot be loaded. If reopening fails, review the message before trying again. **Set on hold** stays visible but disabled when the template blocks reopening. Hover over or tap it to read **Locked for this machine (BDE template).** It is absent on **Handed over** parts. Ask your planner about reversing a handover. Follow [Reopen a Completed Operation](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#reopen-completed).

## Check Assisted Interval Times

When an administrator enables **PDA Assist**, late starts and stops can move to times shown by the machine data. It applies to production and preparation on Data Acquisition V3. Manual time edits and mid-bookings do not receive these automatic adjustments.

The choice dialog compares **Now** with **After** and shows which order gains or loses time. Choose a main option or expand **Other times** for earlier choices, newest first. You can also tap a marker on the timeline. Keep the time you pressed if none fits. Closing the initial choice dialog keeps that time.

After an automatic adjustment, **Undo** restores the time you pressed. **Change…** opens the alternatives; **Back to the time you pressed** restores the original time there. One suitable time within the automatic limit can apply immediately. With several times, your machine's settings decide whether to ask or choose automatically. Times beyond the automatic limit always need your choice.

The interval list marks changes as **Adjusted**, **Auto-ended**, or **Improved**. Open the explanation to see the changed time and reason. **Auto-ended** means a forgotten production interval ended after the configured off or idle period. It works even when the PDA page is closed. Check quantities afterwards: automatic ending does not book them. Preparation never ends this way.

When automatic ending is enabled, **Machine off, order still running** can show the remaining minutes. Snoozing or muting this warning does not stop automatic ending. Ask an administrator to change it in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

Interval changes appear immediately on the timeline. The update after a change keeps your selected shift if it still exists. Regular and manual refreshes return to the live shift. To review past days, use **Improve intervals** on [Machine Details](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-details), following [Review and Improve Intervals](/en/platform-intro/workflows/improve-intervals).

### See What Assistance Is Active

Tap **PDA Assist** at the right edge of Data Acquisition V3. It appears when at least one interval-assistance option or warning is enabled. On a phone it sits beside the action bar, above any warning banner.

The dialog lists what assistance does on this machine. **Right now** shows current information, such as the remaining time before automatic ending or the last corrected start or end. The dot is amber only during the automatic-ending countdown; otherwise it is green. It briefly glows after assistance changes a time. The countdown says the order will end unless the machine restarts. To prevent automatic ending, ask an administrator to change the [machine settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

### Understand the Offered Times

Only times shown by machine activity or the previous interval's end are offered. The main choices use the most recent activity. Earlier choices appear under **Other times**, newest first, with up to 20 offered times in total. Times before the previous order's end or beyond the machine's asking limit are left out, never moved to a limit.

Open the information icon beside the choice dialog's title for a short explanation. Missing data shorter than 10 minutes and idle, off, or preparation interruptions shorter than 2 minutes do not split an activity stretch. For example, a five-minute data gap during production does not turn the return of data into a new production start.

## Choose a Downtime Reason

As a machine operator, you can choose a reason while the downtime is still running. Under **Downtime Reason**, select **Current downtime** and tap a tile, or tap the latest timeline segment and choose a reason. The running downtime's tooltip shows your choice and keeps it after the page refreshes. This also works when the downtime began before the last 8 hours shown on the timeline.

When the machine starts again, your choice is saved onto that downtime. To correct it afterwards, use that period on the timeline. The tiles now pre-set a reason for upcoming downtimes.

A reason chosen by the machine operator or set by hand takes priority. When [automatic break labels](/en/platform-intro/pages/break-settings) are enabled, an eligible break's reason comes next. **Downtime reason rules** apply to the remaining idle or off time; without a matching reason, the time stays unlabelled. Follow [choose a downtime reason](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#label-downtime-with-an-available-reason).

Automatic break labels appear only after downtime ends, not in the running downtime's tooltip. If the machine operator chooses a reason after the downtime ended, that reason replaces the break label for the whole downtime, also when the downtime had been split into parts around the break. Parts whose reason someone set or cleared by hand keep that choice. A reason you set or clear by hand is never replaced by automatic break labelling.

**Downtime Reason** offers only the reasons available on this machine. An administrator can leave the machine with no available reasons. You then cannot choose a downtime reason; ask an administrator to check the selection if you need one. A tile can show a code in its corner. Use the category chips above the tiles to narrow the list; these appear only when reasons have categories.

Saved tile arrangements keep newly available reasons and drop deleted ones. Other downtime reason pickers show reasons alphabetically with the code before the name. **Remove label** clears the selected reason without deleting the downtime period.

If a reason is no longer allowed, the list updates. Choose an available reason or ask an administrator to check the machine's selection. A historical reason may still appear even when it is no longer selectable. See [Downtime Reasons FAQs](/en/help-center/faq/downtime-reasons).

### Pre-set a Reason for Upcoming Downtimes

Machine operator, User and Admin can use this if they can already choose downtime reasons on the machine's PDA page. No Admin setting is needed.

While the machine is running, **Downtime Reason** shows **Pre-set reason for the next downtime**. Tap an available reason tile. Every downtime that starts in the next 15 minutes gets that reason automatically, including several stops within that time. If you tap just after the machine stops, while the page still shows it producing, that just-started stop also gets the reason.

During a downtime, the switch above the tiles offers:

- **Current downtime**: the tile applies to the downtime running now. Every new downtime starts with this option selected. Choosing a reason does not change the option.
- **Pre-set downtime**: the tile pre-sets a reason for new downtimes after the machine runs again. The current downtime keeps its own reason. The 15 minutes start at your tap, even while the current downtime continues.

Only you change the switch during a downtime. Tap the information icon beside it for an explanation. For example, if the machine is stopped for loading and a tool change follows the restart, use **Pre-set downtime** for the tool change. If the current stop lasts beyond the remaining time, the pre-set expires before it can apply to a new stop.

### Adjust or End the Pre-set

The bar sits at the bottom of the middle part of the page, in the chosen reason's colour. For example, it shows **Pre-set: Tool change · 12 min left** and **Every downtime that starts in this time gets this reason automatically.** The tabs on the left and assignments on the right stay usable. Minutes are rounded up; below a minute, the bar says **less than 1 min left**. It disappears when the time runs out.

- **+5 min** extends the time, up to 2 hours ahead. Near that limit, only the time that fits is added.
- **−5 min** shortens the time. At 6 minutes or less it is unavailable; tap the bar's cross to end the pre-set instead.
- To change the downtime reason, tap another tile while the machine is running or with **Pre-set downtime** selected. The remaining time stays the same.
- To end the pre-set, tap ✕ in the bar or on the selected tile in pre-set mode. The tile's tooltip says **End pre-set**. Downtimes that already received the reason keep it.

The selected tile is white with dark text and a border in the reason's colour. With **Current downtime** selected, the tile's ✕ has the tooltip **Remove reason**. It removes the running downtime's reason. If it ends without a reason, the downtime reason rules can apply again.

A downtime that starts while the pre-set is active keeps the reason for its whole length. This also applies if it lasts beyond the 15 minutes or changes between idle and off. The pre-set reason counts as your own choice. Break labels and automatic downtime reason rules cannot replace it.

Follow [pre-set and manage a downtime reason](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#next-downtime-reason).

## PDA Assist Warnings

**PDA Assist** shows warnings for this machine when an administrator enables them. Only administrators can change warning settings. It is separate from classic Alert Rules and Automator.

| Warning | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Producing without an order** | The machine is producing but no production order is running, so the production time is in no interval. It clears when an order starts. |
| **Machine off, order still running** | The machine has been off for the configured delay while the order’s interval keeps running on the stopped machine. It clears when the order ends or the machine restarts. |
| **Order running too long** | One order has run longer than the configured threshold. |
| **Order carried across shift change** | An order from an earlier shift is still open after the current shift's delay. This needs shifts assigned to the machine. |
| **Live data is stale** | No fresh machine data has arrived for the configured delay. Other warnings may be unreliable. |
| **Multiple orders running** | More orders are running than **Jobs in parallel** allows for this machine. |
| **Downtime without a reason** | A downtime block is waiting for a reason. |

**Info** is a silent notice. **Warning** shows an orange banner and orange light around the whole page; **Critical** shows a red banner and flashing screen border. The warning appears after the delay selected by your administrator. A running-too-long warning uses its hour threshold instead of a separate delay.

Use the floating warning button at the bottom left: select **View all warnings** to open **Active warnings**. Read the affected order and duration, then use the available action: **Select an order**, **End order**, **Set downtime reason**, or **Refresh now**. Check the actual machine and order before acting. Follow [Run Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition).

### Snooze and Sound

You can snooze one warning or choose **Snooze all**. Snoozing hides the warning from the banner and silences it for the configured duration. It remains listed under **Snoozed** and returns if the problem persists. **Resume now** brings it back sooner.

If sound is allowed and enabled for that warning, **Warning** plays two beeps and **Critical** three alternating high and low beeps. The alarm repeats while the warning is active and not snoozed. **Info** is always silent. Select **Enable sound** to turn sound on or **Mute** to silence it on your device. The browser needs a tap on the page before sound can start.

Your administrator can limit sound to active shifts. This limits sound, not the warning display. See [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration) for delays, severity, sound, and copying settings.
