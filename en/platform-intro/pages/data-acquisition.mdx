## Overview

**Access:** Any role with access to this machine. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Support live shop-floor execution and event recording for a single machine.

Users usually open it from the Machine Details page.

To switch machines, select the machine name in the top navigation bar. The dropdown groups the other machines you can access by hall and opens the selected machine's Data Acquisition screen directly.

## What You Can See

- Assignment lists and live order context in open-assignment or time-window views.
- Personalized assignment columns, ordering, and sorting.
- Start, preparation, and stop controls.
- Interval history, notes, tickets, and knowledge context.

## What You Can Do

- Start or stop work.
- Record produced quantity, material scrap, and work scrap when stopping or continuing an interval.
- Create or use live orders.
- Search for unplanned operations and assign them directly to the machine.
- Configure the information and ordering shown on assignment cards.
- Apply timeline reasons and review related support content.

## Choosing and Understanding Work

Use open-assignment or time-window views to find the work for the current machine. If an operation has not been planned, you can search for it and assign it directly from this page. Operation sequence numbers on job chips help identify the order of work, and operation and assignment notes provide instructions and context.

In Data Acquisition V3, **Sort by Queue Position** displays the order set by dragging assignments. Turn it off to display work by rules such as priority or date. Each rule supports ascending or descending order; further rules break ties, and dragging the rules changes their precedence. These choices are also available in the [Assignment Planner](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).

## Interval Times and Timeline Display

An interval records a period of machine activity with a start and end time. The quick time editor lets you correct either timestamp in steps of two, five, or thirty minutes in either direction. Repeated adjustments add up; the full date/time picker remains available for precise changes.

Data Acquisition V3 settings offer two separate choices:

- **Timeline display → Show intervals in graph** controls whether interval bars appear on the graph.
- **Auto-refresh interval** controls how often assignments and hall pools refresh automatically.

Timeline entries for idle and stopped periods update as you switch between running, paused, and stopped states. Review these entries and their reasons to keep a clear record of interruptions. See [Run Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition) for the correction and display procedures.

## Production Quantities, Scrap, and Completion

When stopping or continuing an interval, you can enter produced quantity and record **material scrap** and **work scrap** separately. Keeping the two scrap categories separate lets you review the breakdown and their combined total in [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products) and [Order Analysis](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail). Displayed production totals include the submitted scrap quantities.

Starting or stopping production updates the related order status. An assignment is marked complete when the produced quantity exactly matches the planned quantity. An over-production prompt can help you review completion when production exceeds the plan.

## PDA / BDE Buttons and Quantity Labels

**PDA (Production Data Acquisition)** and **BDE (Betriebsdatenerfassung)** are the same operator screen. Tabs include **Assignments**, **Pool**, **Downtime reasons**, **Tickets**, and **Knowledge Base**.

**Prep** starts preparation (German **Vorber.**; also called Rüsten), **Start** starts production, **Stop** ends an interval, and **Continue** resumes. Explicit actions include Start/End Preparation and Start/End Shift.

**Good qty. / Gutmenge** is good output. **Mat. / MA** is Material Scrap; **Work / AA** is Work Scrap. **Total = Input + MA + AA**. **Rework Qty** is the amount requiring rework. **Cycle Count** is sensor-detected; **PDA Entry / BDE-Eintrag** is operator-entered. Switching the progress source changes only the display.

**Example:** 95 entered good units, 3 material scrap, and 2 work scrap produce a total of 100. In **Cumulative** mode, enter the full machine counter, for example 1450, rather than this interval's increment.

## Mid Booking, Hall Pools, and Dependencies

**Mid Booking (Zwischenbuchung)** saves quantities while the job continues by closing and restarting the interval. If restart fails, quantities stay saved; press **Start** to resume. Check the running state before booking again.

In the **Pool** tab, claim work from the machine's hall pool. If no assignment exists, search unplanned operations or use **Find/Create Immediate Order**. **Job Context** combines order, work-order, operation, and assignment notes.

**Waiting on a previous step / Blocked by** means an earlier operation is unfinished. **Start anyway** remains available; review the dependency before proceeding. The concurrent-order prompt asks for confirmation if another assignment is running. Over-production offers **Put on hold** or **Mark as finished**.

Unbound or unlabeled machine time belongs to no order. Deleting a recorded interval is irreversible.

## Choose the Assignment View

| Mode | Included work |
| --- | --- |
| **All Open** | All open assignments, with no date filter. |
| **Time Window + Open** | Open assignments in a ±1/3/5/7-day window; useful for daily work. |
| **Time Window + All Statuses** | Includes completed and cancelled work for looking back. |

**Card Display Settings / Columns / Highlight** are saved per user. Queue Position follows manual order; running and on-hold cards remain at the top.

## Check Assisted Interval Times

When an administrator enables **PDA Assist**, late starts and stops can move to times shown by the machine data. It applies to production and preparation on Data Acquisition V3. Manual time edits and mid-bookings do not receive these automatic adjustments.

The choice dialog compares **Now** with **After** and shows which order gains or loses time. Choose a main option or expand **Other times** for earlier choices, newest first. You can also tap a marker on the timeline. Keep the time you pressed if none fits. Closing the initial choice dialog keeps that time.

After an automatic adjustment, **Undo** restores the time you pressed. **Change…** opens the alternatives; **Back to the time you pressed** restores the original time there. One suitable time within the automatic limit can apply immediately. With several times, your machine's settings decide whether to ask or choose automatically. Times beyond the automatic limit always need your choice.

The interval list marks changes as **Adjusted**, **Auto-ended**, or **Improved**. Open the explanation to see the changed time and reason. **Auto-ended** means a forgotten production interval ended after the configured off or idle period. It works even when the terminal is closed. Check quantities afterwards: automatic ending does not book them. Preparation never ends this way.

When automatic ending is enabled, **Machine off, order still running** can show the remaining minutes. Snoozing or muting this warning does not stop automatic ending. Ask an administrator to change it in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

Interval changes appear immediately on the timeline. The update after a change keeps your selected shift if it still exists. Regular and manual refreshes return to the live shift. To review past days, use **Improve intervals** on [Machine Details](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-details), following [Review and Improve Intervals](/en/platform-intro/workflows/improve-intervals).

## Choose a Downtime Reason

**Downtime reasons** offers only the reasons available on this machine. A tile can show a code in its corner. Use the category chips above the tiles to narrow the list; these appear only when reasons have categories.

Saved tile arrangements keep newly available reasons and drop deleted ones. Other timeline reason pickers show reasons alphabetically with the code before the name. **Remove label** clears the selected reason without deleting the downtime period.

If a reason is no longer allowed, the list updates. Choose an available reason or ask an administrator to check the machine's selection. A historical reason may still appear even when it is no longer selectable. See [Downtime Reasons FAQs](/en/help-center/faq/downtime-reasons).

## PDA Assist Warnings

**PDA Assist** shows warnings for this machine when an administrator enables them. Only administrators can change warning settings. It is separate from classic Alert Rules and Automator.

| Warning | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Producing without an order** | The machine is producing but no production order is running. It clears when an order starts. |
| **Machine off, order still running** | The machine has been off for the configured delay while an order keeps counting time. It clears when the order ends or the machine restarts. |
| **Order running too long** | One order has run longer than the configured threshold. |
| **Order carried across shift change** | An order from an earlier shift is still open after the current shift's delay. This needs shifts assigned to the machine. |
| **Live data is stale** | No fresh machine data has arrived for the configured delay. Other warnings may be unreliable. |
| **Multiple orders running** | More orders are running than **Jobs in parallel** allows for this machine. |
| **Downtime without a reason** | A downtime block is waiting for a reason. |

**Info** is a silent notice. **Warning** shows an orange banner; **Critical** shows a red banner and flashing screen border. The warning appears after the delay selected by your administrator. A running-too-long warning uses its hour threshold instead of a separate delay.

Select **View all warnings** to open **Active warnings**. Read the affected order and duration, then use the available action: **Select an order**, **End order**, **Set downtime reason**, or **Refresh now**. Check the actual machine and order before acting. Follow [Run Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition).

### Snooze and Sound

You can snooze one warning or choose **Snooze all**. Snoozing hides the warning from the banner and silences it for the configured duration. It remains listed under **Snoozed** and returns if the problem persists. **Resume now** brings it back sooner.

If sound is allowed and enabled for that warning, **Warning** plays two beeps and **Critical** three alternating high and low beeps. The alarm repeats while the warning is active and not snoozed. **Info** is always silent. Select **Enable sound** to turn sound on or **Mute** to silence it on your device. The browser needs a tap on the page before sound can start.

Your administrator can limit sound to active shifts. This limits sound, not the warning display. See [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration) for delays, severity, sound, and copying settings.

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- The machine’s production story is captured in a detailed, shift-level form.
- Recorded totals include the submitted scrap quantities.

### Limits and Empty States
- Users need the right live assignment or order context before the page is fully useful.
- The page warns users before production starts when another assignment is already running.
