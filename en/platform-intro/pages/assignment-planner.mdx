## Overview

**Access:** Admin or Page Access `assignment_planner`, with relevant Machine Access. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Give planners a drag-and-drop workspace for deciding which machine should run which work next, plus a timeline for reviewing assignments across machines and time.

Users open it from the Planner section in the main navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/assignment-planner`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- An unassigned work list and search in **Filter & Sort**.
- Machine columns and a Hall Pool column for each hall that act as queues.
- Hall and machine filters to focus the planning surface.
- A selectable set of machines in the Gantt timeline.
- Scheduling overlaps and links to the related orders.

## What You Can Do

- Search assignments and unassigned operations, with results updating as you type.
- Drag work into a machine queue or hall pool, or move it between columns.
- Reorder assignments inside a queue.
- Update assignment details such as timing, status, or priority.
- Automatically arrange future assignments without changing active production.
- Review warnings before overlapping assignments are rearranged.
- Open Assignment Gantt to inspect the production schedule and use automatic assignment.

## Machine Queues and Hall Pools

A machine queue assigns work to a specific machine. A **Hall Pool** holds work for a hall when several machines could perform it and the machine choice can stay open. By default, each hall pool appears beside its machine columns. You can drag an operation into it or assign a new operation there.

The readiness badge indicates when pooled work is ready to be claimed by a machine. Placing work in a hall pool leaves the machine choice open until it is claimed; the badge helps you decide when work can be picked up.

You can move an assignment directly between machine and hall-pool columns and choose the start or end of the destination queue. Assignments already in progress or completed cannot move to another column, but can still be reordered within their current queue.

You can also assign work to a destination marked **Work center**. Its queue contains orders and assignments without sensor KPIs. Review the destination before confirming a move. See [Work Centers](/en/platform-intro/pages/work-centers).

### Choose Where Hall Pools Appear

On a wide screen, open **Hall pools** above the board. Under **Where to show them**, choose:

- **In the board**: the default. Pools sit beside their hall’s machines and follow the board’s column or row layout.
- **Beside the list**: each pool stays beside **Unassigned operations** while you scroll through machines. Pools remain vertical columns in either layout. Each is as tall as the unassigned list and scrolls its cards separately.

Your placement choice is saved for your account. Under **Which pools to show**, tick the halls you need. This is the same selection as the hall filter. Hall pools do not count toward the 25-machine limit. While a pool loads, it shows a loading indicator.

With **Beside the list**, use **Collapse pool** in the pool header to leave a narrow strip with its name and count. It still accepts cards. A drop places work at the end of the pool, including cards not yet shown. If your search covers the pool, close it first so the work lands at the very end. Otherwise, the drop can land among the cards. Click the strip or use **Expand pool** to open it again. Collapsed pools are remembered in this browser for the current company.

Hiding the unassigned list leaves the pools at the left edge. The placement menu and these columns are available on wide screens. Follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

## Search, Filters, and Card Details

Open the information icon for **Assignment Planner Guide** when you need help with filters, sorting, or moving cards.

Press Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) or click the search icon in the always-visible **Filter & Sort** bar. Enter an order, product, or customer. Under **Search in**, choose the area to filter:

| Choice | What the search filters |
| --- | --- |
| **Unassigned** | Only the unassigned operations list. This is the default. |
| **Hall pools** | Only work in hall pools. |
| **Machines** | Only work on machines, not machine names. |
| **Everything** | The list, hall pools, and machines together. |

Search only filters the area chosen under **Search in**. Status, priority, and tag filters apply to the unassigned list and all queues. The import filter narrows only the unassigned list to one ERP upload. Areas outside your search choice keep showing their cards. Your search-area choice is saved for your account. When the search includes the unassigned list, it also shows how many unassigned operations were found. Esc or × closes and clears the search; other filters still apply.

A link that opens the Planner with a search uses **Everything** for that visit without changing your saved choice. Opening it from an order, product, or customer can also add a filter chip above the board. Remove that chip to clear its filter; matching unassigned cards are outlined.

Select machines by hall; click the hall checkbox or hall name to select or clear the whole hall. Empty space in the heading does not change the selection. You can show up to 25 machines plus hall pools. Status filters apply to individual assignments. Order tags appear when your company has defined them. The import filter narrows only the unassigned list to one ERP upload.

**Columns** controls the values shown on cards. Some values cannot be hidden. **Highlight** emphasizes visible values. Both choices are saved to your profile. Highlighting a hidden value has no visible effect until you show it. You can also read and edit operation or assignment notes here.

### Reveal More Cards in a Queue

Each machine or pool queue initially shows 40 matching cards. Select **Show 40 more** to reveal the next 40; the button shows a smaller number when fewer remain. The count and summary include every card in the queue, also the ones not shown yet. This works in both layouts. Search filters the work before the first 40 cards are shown.

Revealed cards stay visible after a refresh. Dropping at the end places work after every card in the queue, even if you have not revealed them all. If your search covers that queue, close it first so the work lands at the very end. Otherwise, the drop can land among the cards. **Unassigned** does not filter machine queues or pools; **Hall pools**, **Machines**, and **Everything** filter the queues in their chosen area.

If a change cannot be saved, the Planner shows an error and reloads the queues involved. Check them before trying again.

## Set and Filter Priorities

**Order priority** (previously **Priority filter**) and **Operation priority** are independent. Select the **P** chip for the order or the dashed **OP** chip for one operation to change its priority. Changing an operation's priority leaves the order priority unchanged.

Each priority filter accepts several values. Type a number to find a value that has no entry in the company legend. When you use both filters, a card must match both. For example, you can find a particular operation priority within orders of a selected priority. See [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

## Queue Position and Sorting Rules

**Sort by Queue Position** displays assignments in the order set by dragging them. When this option is off, sorting rules determine the displayed order instead. Each rule has a field and an ascending or descending direction. Additional rules break ties, and their order determines which takes precedence.

The same sorting choices are available in [Data Acquisition V3](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition). Priority values follow the company levels configured in the [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

### Sort a Queue by Priority and Date

You need an administrator account, or a user account with access to the Orders page, plus access to the machine. Operators do not see this button. Under **Filter & Sort**, enable **Sort by Queue Position**. Each machine and hall-pool header then offers **Sort by priority and date**, in both column and row layouts.

**Sort queue by priority and date** opens a preview for that queue. Each opening starts with **Order priority**; choose **Operation priority** if you want to use the priorities of individual steps. Higher values come first. For equal priorities, the earliest planned operation start comes first. Missing priorities or dates come last in their comparison. Equal priority and date keep the current order.

Running orders stay first, followed by on-hold orders. Each group keeps its current order and shows **Stays on top**. The preview shows proposed positions, previous positions for moved orders, priorities, planned starts, and how many orders would change place. It covers the active queue, including work hidden by your filters.

Choose **Apply order** to save the shared queue order, or choose **Cancel** to leave it unchanged. **New order applied** confirms the change and offers **Undo**. This is a one-time sorting action; you can adjust the order by dragging cards afterwards. Imported orders still join the end of the queue.

If the queue changed during review, a notice appears and the preview updates. Review it again before applying. If loading fails, retry; applying stays unavailable until a proposal loads. Applying is also unavailable when nothing would change. If undo fails, the queue reloads; check its current order before making another change. Follow the [queue sorting steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner#sort-a-queue-by-priority-and-date).

### Read Queue Position Numbers

The number at the bottom left of a card means **Place in this queue**. Numbers count active assignments in each machine or hall pool, starting at 1. They work in both layouts and continue when you reveal more cards. Completed and cancelled work has no number.

Numbers appear only with **Sort by Queue Position** on, no priority, operation priority or tag filters, and the usual status selection. A search covering machines or pools hides numbers in that area. Clear the relevant search and filters to see them again. Data Acquisition cards do not show these numbers.

### Save or Use Company Sorting

Administrators can set the sort mode, rules, and **Moves** choice under **Filter & Sort**, then select **Save as company default**. **Saved as company default** confirms the change. The **Moves** choice controls where moved orders land in rule mode; in queue mode, the drop position still decides.

People without their own saved sorting start with the company default. Personal sorting takes precedence. Data Acquisition V3 uses the company's sort mode and shared rules; its running- and waiting-order options are separate.

Choose **Reset to company default** to copy the current company mode, rules, and **Moves** choice into your Planner settings. This saves your own sorting, so later changes to the company default do not replace it. Without a company default, the reset shows **Reset to default (no company default set yet)** and restores the standard choices. For the terminal's reset and required save step, see [Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition).

### Choose the Date for Sorting

Cards can show **Operation Planned Start** and **Operation Planned End**. These describe one operation's planned window. Use them to sequence individual steps within an order.

**Order Planned Start** and **Order Planned End** describe the whole order. Every operation in that order shares these dates, so sorting by them keeps its steps together. **Work Order Planned Start** and **Work Order Planned End** describe the work order's planned window.

## Check Dates with the Forecaster

Open **Forecaster check** to choose which date the cards are measured against. The coloured tab on the left of each card shows whether the job will make that date: red means late, amber means **at risk**, and green means on time. An **at risk** result means the remaining work barely fits or an earlier step is not planned. The machine summary also counts jobs with this warning.

If the Forecaster has no expected end for a job, only its date is compared with today. This does not confirm that the work will finish on time. The queue's expected finish tells you when the machine is expected to complete all its queued work. Waiting working time tells you how much work remains.

For the explanation, open **Forecaster check → How does the Forecaster work?**. The menu closes and the help opens without changing your selected date check. It explains the colours, how shifts and earlier steps affect expected times, and why missing planning details can make times too early. You can also read the [Forecaster explanation](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt) or follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

Under **Forecaster check**, choose **Delivery date**, **Planned end**, **Delivery date & planned end**, or **Off**. Read the card’s expected end and remaining buffer to understand the warning. An unplanned earlier step makes the expected end too optimistic.

The machine summary’s free time comes from the last expected finish in its queue. If some work has no expected end, that time is only the earliest possible availability. The displayed load is work still booked by the forecast.

Switch the layout to put machines side by side with queues downward, or machines top to bottom with queues to the right. Use **Show unassigned operations** or **Hide unassigned operations** to adjust the workspace. You can display up to 25 machines; hall pools do not count toward that limit.

## Assignment Gantt and Scheduling Feedback

Use the view switch to open [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt). Review expected starts and finishes, trace delays under **Attention**, and compare waiting work with **Improve plan**. Its **Move** controls let you review the effects of an eligible assignment’s move before saving.

**Save as plan** updates eligible queue positions and planned dates. Running and on-hold work keep their place. Review the work left unchanged, especially assignments behind an unplanned earlier step. Follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner) for review and saving steps.

## Why Can I Not Move a Card?

Only **Unplanned**, **Planned**, and **Released** assignments can move to another machine or hall. In-progress, on-hold, and completed work can only be reordered within its current queue. The UI mentions Released in move rules, but when work becomes Released is not confirmed here.

At most 25 machines can be shown at once, plus hall pools. Search only filters the area chosen under **Search in**. Status, priority, and tag filters apply to the unassigned list and all queues. The import filter narrows only the unassigned list to one ERP upload. If the queue changes while you reorder, it refreshes and you must redo the reorder.

With **Queue Position** on, dropping a card sets its exact position; running and on-hold cards stay at the top. With it off, sort-rule precedence controls the view, and the **Moves** dropdown offers **Start of Queue** or **End of Queue**. This also applies to newly assigned operations dropped from the unassigned list onto a machine or hall pool.

## Read OP, AG, P, Seq, Qty, and Utilization

- The dashed **OP** chip shows **Operation priority**; **P** shows the independent **Order priority**.
- **Seq / Nr.** is the operation sequence. **Qty / Menge** is quantity; **Dur / Dauer** is duration.
- **Prep / Rüst** is preparation; **Prod.** is production. **util. / Ausl.** is planned queue load from planned durations, not actual utilization.
- Order Planned dates are shared across an order; Operation Planned dates belong to one step.

For example, 50 parts at 2 minutes per part add 100 planned production minutes, plus fixed preparation time. [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt) shows time windows; its drag-to-reschedule mode is Experimental and read-only. See [calculations](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations#deviations).

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users create or adjust the planned machine sequence for upcoming work.

### Limits and Empty States

- A hall pool loads up to 500 assignments; a machine queue loads up to 100.
- With **Queue Position** on, dropping an unassigned operation into a large queue adds the work but does not save its chosen position. This affects hall pools with more than 500 active assignments and machine queues with more than 100. The Planner shows **Could not persist queue reorder. Local changes were rolled back.** and reloads the queue. Check the queue before trying again. Reordering work already in a large pool is still saved.
- Up to 25 machines can be shown at once. Hall pools do not count toward this limit.
- Filters can hide both machines and unassigned work.
- In-progress and completed assignments cannot be moved to another column.
- Automatic arrangement applies to future assignments and leaves active production unchanged.
