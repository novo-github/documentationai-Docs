## Overview

**Access:** Admin or Page Access `orders`; never Operator. [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

Monitor production orders, find work that needs attention, and open an order's details when you need to inspect or change its operations and assignments.

Users open it from the dashboard navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/orders-products`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- An order table with controls for moving between result pages.
- Filters for customer, order, product, machine, date range, assignment status, and live production.
- Summary cards for **Total**, **Planned**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, **Live**, and **Forecaster**.
- Customer codes, operation sequence numbers, planned cycle times, duration, deviation, and production-performance explanations.
- Grouped orders with individual assignments.
- Progress based on either PDA production entries or detected cycle counts.
- Material scrap, work scrap, and produced totals that include recorded scrap.

## What You Can Do

- Search, filter, and sort across the complete order dataset.
- Expand grouped orders to review individual assignments.
- Open a specific order detail page.
- Start product or order creation flows.
- Identify orders that need attention because of exceeded durations, degraded cycle times, or overdue dates.
- Select multiple orders for detailed analysis.
- Switch the progress source, enable automatic refresh, and save column visibility preferences.

Choose a destination marked **Work center** when an operation belongs at a workplace without an AVA. Assignment details and selection lists identify work centers so you can distinguish them from machines. See [Work Centers](/en/platform-intro/pages/work-centers) for the limits of sensor readings.

## Understand the Page

### Understand Orders, Work Orders, and Assignments

A production order brings the planned work together. Within it, work orders contain operations, and assignments connect that work to its planned production destination. Expand a grouped order to inspect its individual assignments, or open [Order Detail](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail) to review the full structure.

### Read Totals and Progress

Summary totals count each order once, even when it has several assignments. An expanded table can show several assignment rows for that same order, so the number of detail rows can be greater than the order total.

Use **Forecaster** to find open orders expected to miss delivery or already past their delivery date. It replaces the broader **Needs Attention** summary. Review an order’s warnings before deciding what to change.

The progress source determines which production record you are reviewing: PDA entries are quantities recorded in Data Acquisition, while detected cycle counts come from machine cycles. Check the selected source when comparing progress. Material scrap and work scrap are shown separately, and produced totals include recorded scrap.

### Find Orders That May Miss Delivery

The **Forecaster** summary card counts open orders expected to finish after their delivery date, or whose delivery date has already passed. Click the card for details about orders that will miss delivery if nothing changes. Orders with steps that have no machine yet may finish even later than shown.

In the **Forecaster** column, the top result checks the delivery date and the bottom result checks the planned end. Red means late for delivery. Amber means **at risk** or past the planned end. Green means on time. The step count tells you how many steps are on a machine; it does not mean those steps are finished.

While the Forecaster's late-order filter is active, you see every late order regardless of date range and status. Order, product, customer, and machine filters still apply. The company-wide count can differ from [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt), which shows warnings for the displayed machines from last week to three weeks ahead.

In the card details, choose **Late for delivery → Show in table** for late orders or **Of these, steps not planned → Show in table** for late orders with missing steps. The active filter reads **Forecaster: late for delivery** or **Forecaster: late, steps not planned**. Date range and status filters are paused for either choice; remove the Forecaster filter to use them again. **Partly planned** identifies work with steps still not assigned.

**Not planned at all → Show list** opens **Orders that are not planned**. These orders have a delivery date but no operation assigned to a machine or hall pool, so they appear on no planning board. Open an order and choose **Assign steps** to select machines, or **Open in planner**. Follow the [planning workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

### Open the Forecaster Help

Click the information icon beside **Forecaster** on the summary card, in its details, or in the table column heading. You can also select **How does the Forecaster work?** at the bottom of the card's details. Clicking the card's information icon opens the help directly, without opening the card's details.

The help explains expected times, colours, and missing planning details. The Forecaster never changes your plan by itself. See the [full explanation](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt) or the [planning workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner) before deciding how to respond.

### Use Company Priority Levels

Priority names, numbers, and colors follow the levels configured by your administrator. The same levels are used in imports and assignment planning. See [Priority Levels in the Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel) for presets and customization.

## Filters, Refresh, and Summary Counts

All filters combine with **AND**. When a **Customer, Order, Product, or Machine** filter is active, the **date range is ignored**. For example, selecting a customer can show matching orders outside the date window. Sorting covers every order, not just the loaded page. Auto-refresh reloads every **60 seconds**.

Summary cards show the company-wide total on top and the number in the current view underneath. Each order counts once. Grouped planned dates span the whole order; each assignment row has its own planned and actual window.

Choose planned or actual dates beside the date pickers to decide which dates the range filter searches. Planned dates describe the scheduled window; actual dates show when production really ran. On a grouped order row, the planned window covers the whole order. Expand it to see each assignment's own window in the same columns.

## Status and Delivery Warnings

| English status | German label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unplanned | Ungeplant | No schedule yet. |
| Planned | Geplant | Scheduled, not started. |
| Released | Freigegeben | Approved to start; the exact transition into this status is unconfirmed. |
| In Progress | In Bearbeitung | Work underway. |
| Hold | In Wartestellung | Paused. |
| Completed | Abgeschlossen | Finished. |
| Cancelled | Storniert | Called off. |

Order status follows its assignments. **Live** means an interval is running now. The **Forecaster** delivery count concerns open orders. A warning about a planned end or slow production is not by itself a late-delivery count; read the order’s expected finish against its delivery date.

## Quantity and Time Short Forms

**PDA Entry / BDE-Eintrag** uses operator quantity; **Cycle Count** uses sensor counts. This choice changes the progress display, not bookings. **Mat. / MA** and **Work / AA** are separate scrap categories; Total is input + MA + AA.

**Seq / Nr.** is operation sequence; **Prep. / Rüst** is preparation; **Prod.** is production; **Dev. / Abweichung** is actual minus planned time. A negative time deviation means faster than planned. **Cycles Per Product / Press per Product** defines cycles required for a finished part. For formulas, see [calculations](/en/platform-intro/concepts/calculations).

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users find an order quickly and drill into it.
- Users create a new product or order.
- Users identify planning or production issues across one or more orders.

### Limits and Empty States
- Filters combine with AND; Customer, Order, Product, or Machine filters override the date range.
- Sorting and filtering apply across the complete order list, not only the currently visible result page.
- Orders without deeper structure may need the detail page before they can be planned well.
