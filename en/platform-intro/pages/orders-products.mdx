## Overview
Help users review production orders and products in one place, then open deeper order editing when needed.

Users open it from the dashboard navigation.

## Access
<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Admin, Manager
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/orders-products`
</Callout>

Admins and planners who work with incoming production demand.

## What You Can See
- Order and product views with filters, date range controls, and search.
- Customer, product, quantity, and progress context.
- Product-level highlights and order analysis summaries.

## What You Can Do
- Search and filter orders or products.
- Switch between card and table-style views.
- Open a specific order detail page.
- Start product or order creation flows.
- Review high-level planning and production insight for tracked orders.

## Main Entities
- Production Order
- Product
- Customer
- Work Order
- Assignment

## Outcomes and Limits
### Common Outcomes
- Users find an order quickly and drill into it.
- Users create a new product or order.

### Limits and Empty States
- Results depend on the selected date range and filters.
- Orders without deeper structure may need the detail page before they can be planned well.
