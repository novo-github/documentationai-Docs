## Overview

Help users monitor and analyze the complete production-order dataset, then open deeper order editing when needed.

Users open it from the dashboard navigation.

## Access

<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Visibility: Protected
- Primary routes: `/orders-products`
</Callout>

## What You Can See

- A table-focused order workspace with server-backed pagination.
- Filters for customer, order, product, machine, date range, assignment status, and live production.
- Summary cards for Total, Planned, In Progress, Completed, Live, and Needs Attention.
- Customer codes, operation sequence numbers, planned cycle times, duration, deviation, and production-performance explanations.
- Grouped orders with individual assignments.
- Progress based on either PDA production entries or detected cycle counts.
- Material scrap, work scrap, and produced totals that include recorded scrap.

## What You Can Do

- Search, filter, and sort across the complete order dataset.
- Expand grouped orders to review individual assignments.
- Open a specific order detail page.
- Start product or order creation flows.
- Identify orders that need attention because of exceeded durations, degraded cycle times, or overdue dates.
- Select multiple orders for detailed analysis.
- Switch the progress source, enable automatic refresh, and save column visibility preferences.

## Main Entities

- Production Order
- Product
- Customer
- Work Order
- Assignment

## Outcomes and Limits

### Common Outcomes
- Users find an order quickly and drill into it.
- Users create a new product or order.
- Users identify planning or production issues across one or more orders.

### Limits and Empty States
- Results depend on the selected date range and filters.
- Pagination, sorting, and filtering operate on the server-backed order dataset.
- Orders without deeper structure may need the detail page before they can be planned well.
