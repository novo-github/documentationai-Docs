## Overview
Confirm the user’s identity with a one-time code.

Users are redirected here after an auth step that requires OTP.

## Access
<Callout kind="info" title="Access details">
- Roles: Any User
- Visibility: Public
- Primary routes: `/verifyOTP`
</Callout>

Users who must complete an OTP step during access or recovery.

## What You Can See
- OTP entry fields and verification actions.

## What You Can Do
- Enter and submit the one-time code.

## Main Entities
- User
- One-Time Passcode

## Outcomes and Limits
### Common Outcomes
- The user clears the verification step and continues.

### Limits and Empty States
- Expired or incorrect codes block the flow until corrected.
