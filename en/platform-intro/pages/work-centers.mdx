## Overview

**Access:** You see the machines and work centers you are allowed to access. Only administrators can create them or change their configuration. Order and planning actions require access to those pages. See [Roles and access](/en/platform-intro/concepts/roles-and-access).

A **Work center** is a workplace without an AVA. Use it as a destination for orders and assignments, for example work at a manual assembly station.

## What You Can See

Work cards also show handovers: **Handed over** marks a part finished here whose remainder continues elsewhere. A grey badge shows the destination or the source of incoming work and the good parts already made. Hover for details. See [handover details](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail#handed-over-work).

Open **Machines → Work centers**. **Orders today** shows today's booked work, including running and finished steps. **Up next** shows the first 10 waiting steps in queue order, with numbered positions, planned times, and priorities. Running work appears in **Orders today** instead of the waiting queue.

Each list shows two cards at a time. Scroll to review more work; longer lists also advance automatically and pause while you interact. Click a card to open the order if you have access. With no work booked today, the list shows **No orders worked today yet.** See [Machines](/en/platform-intro/pages/machines) for list controls and limits.

The **Work center** label distinguishes these destinations in order assignments and selection lists. Hall Plan shows their running orders instead of machine KPIs.

## What You Can Do

- Choose a work center when assigning an operation in order management or planning.
- Review today’s booked work and the waiting queue from its card.
- Switch back to the machine view to review live machine states.

Follow [Add a Machine or Work Center](/en/platform-intro/workflows/add-machine) for setup and [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner) to place work.

## Settings and Their Effect

Administrators choose **Kind → Work center** during creation or in the machine configuration. No AVA can be assigned while this kind is selected. Only the applicable configuration tabs are available.

When converting a machine with AVAs, **Switch to work center?** lists the devices that will be unassigned. **Unassign and switch** removes those assignments and stops all KPI calculation for that machine. Review this effect before confirming.

To collect sensor readings again, change **Kind** to **Machine**, save, then assign an AVA in the AVA tab. Changing the kind alone does not start data collection. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).

## Downtime Reasons

Administrators can also set **Downtime reasons** for a work center. Choose all company reasons or a fixed selection in its [machine configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration). Without machine data, interval assistance cannot suggest times or check past intervals.

## Work Centers in Version 2 Reports

A work center has no sensor, so Version 2 shows only its operations, from their intervals. It has no OEE, availability, performance, machine states or cycles anywhere in the report. It is excluded from machine tiles, findings and the machine table in **Summary**, and from **Days**, **Shifts**, **Downtime**, **Cycles** and **All data**. Production without an order also excludes work centers.

Open **Summary → Work centers**, after the machine table. Compare **Work center**, **Operations**, **Completed**, **Orders**, **Production in intervals (h)**, **Produced** and **Scrap**. **Preparation (h)** appears between **Production in intervals (h)** and **Produced** only when there was preparation. Click a name to open its page.

The page has a purple tab and the same work-center icon as on the platform. Its subtitle shows work center, hall and period. A sentence explains the absence of OEE. Tiles show **Operations worked on** with the completed count, **Orders worked on**, **Production time in intervals** with preparation hours when present, and **Produced** with scrap when recorded. **Operations on this work center** follows. These hours come from operation intervals, not sensor measurements.

Order figures require at least 5 intervals of real operations in the report period, excluding test orders. Below that minimum, the **Summary → Work centers** row and the work center’s page show **PDA page barely used in this period**. Once the minimum is met, a work center without operations shows **No operation intervals in this period** in the row and **This work center has no operation intervals in this period.** on its page.

The icon also follows the work-center name in **Orders**, **Up next**, the summary section and the page title; **Notes** explains it. See [Report Versions](/en/platform-intro/pages/report-versions#work-centers) and [review work centers in a report](/en/platform-intro/workflows/reports#review-work-centers).

## Limits

Work centers have no live sensor state, OEE, performance, or availability. They are excluded from machine-state counters and sensor analytics, including Trend Analysis. An empty KPI display does not mean zero performance.

Rules that depend on machine or sensor measurements never trigger for work centers. Check the notices in [Alert Rules](/en/platform-intro/pages/alert-rules) and [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator) before relying on a rule.
