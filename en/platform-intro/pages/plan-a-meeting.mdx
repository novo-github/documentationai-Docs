## Overview

**Access: Admin, User and Machine operator.** You must be signed in. **Help & Support** is at the right edge, even when no meetings are available. Use a computer or a tablet with a wide view. The window is unavailable on phones and the PDA page.

Find help for the page you are on without leaving the Watchmen Platform. Follow [Get Help for Your Current Page](/en/platform-intro/workflows/help-support) or [Book a Meeting with Our Team](/en/platform-intro/workflows/plan-a-meeting).

## What You See

Choose **Help & Support** at the right edge to open the window. In the Admin Panel, you can also use **Help & Support** in the top bar, beside the guides button. The window opens with these sections in order:

- **Ask the AI assistant** appears only if you have AI access. Enter your question or choose one of up to two example questions about using the current page.
- **Talk to our team** appears only while meetings are offered. Each card has a name, an icon and short information where available. Select a card to open its calendar, even if only one meeting is offered.
- **Learn this page** shows the page name when available, a matching guide with its progress when available, and related documentation cards. The cards show whether they explain a page, give steps, answer common questions or provide background. **All documentation** opens the full documentation.

At the bottom, “Something not working? **Let us know**” opens the feedback form. The ⓘ beside the title and section headings explains each part.

The card names and information follow your app language. If that text is unavailable, you see the other available language. The booking calendar uses the booking tool's language, which may differ from the Watchmen Platform language.

During the first two weeks, all tabs at the right edge slide out one after another about once a day; after that, about once a week. This happens a few minutes after you open the Watchmen Platform, only when you have not typed, clicked or scrolled for a few seconds and nothing is open. Nothing opens. If your device reduces motion, the tabs briefly light up instead.

## What You Can Do

- Enter a question under **Ask the AI assistant** and choose **Ask**, or press Enter. Selecting an example question also asks it immediately. The window closes and the assistant opens. It sends the question once earlier messages are visible and any current answer has finished. Text you already typed in the chat box stays there.
- Use the round play button on the guide card under **Learn this page** to start or continue it. The window closes and the guide walks you through the page. See [Interactive Guides](/en/platform-intro/pages/interactive-guides).
- Open a documentation card or **All documentation**. The documentation opens in a new tab, in German when your app is German and otherwise in English.
- Choose **Let us know** to open feedback with **Bug** selected. Describe what happened and send the form to report it. The **Feedback** tab at the right edge opens the same form with **Feature request** selected by default. These messages go to Novo AI; company tickets remain separate.
- Select a meeting card, choose a day and time, then complete the booking in the calendar. Opening it books nothing.
- Use the back-arrow button at the top of the booking view to return to Help & Support, also when only one meeting is offered.
- Use the button for opening the calendar in a new tab if you prefer to book there.
- Close the window with × or Esc.

Your first and last name and your company are filled in when available. Your email is filled in only if it is verified; otherwise, enter it in the booking form. Check the details shown before completing the booking. Some meetings also include additional guests automatically.

## After Booking

**Meeting booked** means your meeting is confirmed. The confirmation shows the meeting, date and time in your app language, and tells you where the confirmation email with the meeting link is being sent.

**Request sent** means the meeting still needs confirmation. You will receive an email once it is confirmed.

If a meeting link is available, choose **Open meeting link**. Choose **Done** to close the confirmation.

## Limits and Loading Problems

If you see **The calendar could not be loaded here.**, choose **Open in a new tab** to continue. The calendar can still appear in the dialog if it finishes loading later.

Without meeting offers, only **Talk to our team** is hidden. Without a matching guide or documentation cards, you can still use **All documentation**. The window has no phone action-bar entry. The language of the booking calendar is controlled by the booking tool.

If you close the assistant before a question from Help & Support is sent, it waits in the chat box for you to send yourself. It will not be sent automatically when you reopen the assistant.

See [Meeting Questions](/en/help-center/faq/meetings) for help with missing tabs, email details and confirmations.
