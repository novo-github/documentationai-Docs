## Overview

**Access: Admin, User and Machine operator.** You must be signed in. **Plan a meeting** appears at the right edge only while a meeting is available. Use a computer or a tablet with a wide view; the tab is hidden on narrow screens, including phones.

Book a meeting with the Novo AI team without leaving the Watchmen Platform. Follow [Plan a Meeting](/en/platform-intro/workflows/plan-a-meeting) for the steps.

## What You See

Choose **Plan a meeting** at the right edge to open a centered dialog. If there is one meeting option, its calendar opens immediately. With several options, you first see cards with an icon, a name and short information where available. Select a card to open its calendar.

The card names and information follow your app language. If that text is unavailable, you see the other available language. The booking calendar uses the booking tool's language, which may differ from the Watchmen Platform language.

<a id="edge-tabs" />

During the first two weeks, all tabs at the right edge slide out one after another about once a day; after that, about once a week. This happens a few minutes after you open the Watchmen Platform, only when you have not typed, clicked or scrolled for a few seconds and nothing is open. Nothing opens. If your device reduces motion, the tabs briefly light up instead.

## What You Can Do

- Select a day and time, then complete the booking as shown in the calendar. Opening it does not book a meeting.
- Use the round back-arrow button at the top of the dialog, with the tooltip **Back**, to return to the cards. It appears only when there are several cards. Closing and reopening starts the choice again.
- Use the round button at the top of the dialog with the tooltip **Open in a new tab** if you prefer to book there.
- Use the round button at the top of the dialog with the tooltip **Close** to leave the dialog.

Your first and last name and your company are filled in when available. Your email is filled in only if it is verified; otherwise, enter it in the booking form. Check the details shown before completing the booking. Some meetings also include additional guests automatically.

## After Booking

**Meeting booked** means your meeting is confirmed. The confirmation shows the meeting, date and time in your app language, and tells you where the confirmation email with the meeting link is being sent.

**Request sent** means the meeting still needs confirmation. You will receive an email once it is confirmed.

If a meeting link is available, choose **Open meeting link**. Choose **Done** to close the confirmation.

## Limits and Loading Problems

If you see **The calendar could not be loaded here.**, choose **Open in a new tab** to continue. The calendar can still appear in the dialog if it finishes loading later.

The tab is hidden if no meeting is available. It is also hidden on narrow screens and has no phone action-bar entry. The language of the booking calendar is controlled by the booking tool.

See [Meeting Questions](/en/help-center/faq/meetings) for help with missing tabs, email details and confirmations.
