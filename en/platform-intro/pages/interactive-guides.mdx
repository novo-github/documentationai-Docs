## Overview

**Access: Admin, User and Machine operator.** You must be signed in. You see only guides for your role and pages you can open.

Interactive guides replace a classic onboarding with short walkthroughs on the real Watchmen Platform screen. Follow the highlighted area, read what it means and try actions step by step. You can start any available guide whenever you need it.

## What You See

Open the graduation cap button at the top of the screen to see **Interactive guides**. A red **New** label means there is something to start, such as a new guide or an unfinished assignment. A soft light sweep draws attention to it; it stays still when your device reduces motion. On a phone, the graduation cap button is hidden. Open the ⋮ menu at the top and choose **Interactive guides**. A small dot on the ⋮ button shows when something is new.

The panel shows your getting-started progress, for example **2 of 4**, with a slim gold bar. Each guide has its own progress bar, title, short description and estimated time. After you finish Getting started, **Graduated** replaces its progress bar. This does not mean you have finished every other guide.

Use the search button to open **Search guides**. Combine your search with **All**, **Not done** or **Done**. **This page · 2**, for example, limits the list to the two guides for the page you have open. This filter appears only when that page has guides and starts switched off.

A round white play button starts or continues a guide. Its tooltip says **Start** or **Continue**. A green check means it is done; choose it to repeat the guide, with the tooltip **Done – start again**. **New** and **Assigned** identify guides needing attention. When a guide you finished is updated, it stays done: it keeps its green check and your progress, and it does not show **New** again. Choose the green check to go through the new version.

## Getting Started for Your Role

**Getting started** puts the guides for your role in a suggested order. Start with the highlighted guide. The welcome tours are:

| Role | Welcome tour |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | **Welcome: Explore the platform as an admin** |
| User | **Welcome: Explore your pages** |
| Machine operator | **Welcome: Explore the platform as a machine operator** |

Admins can follow the welcome tour with **Admin Panel: First steps**. Check halls, machine tags, shifts, shift groups and machines, then open a machine's settings. Add or edit something only if needed. The follow-up **Machine settings: Every tab explained** walks through the settings in their screen order. Work centers skip settings they do not have.

**Profile: Set up your profile** covers your details, language, emails, sign-in security and assistant preferences. Admins also have **Admin Panel: Bring your team in** for invitations and the company license key.

Other guides are grouped by area or learning path, such as **Company setup**, under **All guides**. You can choose them independently. Follow [Learn with Interactive Guides](/en/platform-intro/workflows/interactive-guides).

## While a Guide Runs

The guide shows the way through the side menu and highlights the page content. Follow the action described, or choose **Next** to continue reading and **Back** to review a step. Choose **Done** to finish when offered. If a step offers **Skip**, you can leave it without saving. Actions you choose to save affect your real work; opening a guide alone changes nothing.

If you leave the route of the guide or click elsewhere to do other work, it pauses. **Guide paused** appears below the top bar with **Resume** and **End**. **Resume** takes you back to the step where you left off; **End** stops the current walkthrough. Your saved progress remains available through **Continue** in the panel. After a reload, **Guide paused** appears with **Resume** and **End**.

At the end, a fitting follow-up may appear as **Next:** followed by its title, with **Start** and **Later**. Getting-started sequences use **Continue** and **Later**, with **Skip this guide** to move past the offered guide. A follow-up is offered only if it is available to you. If an action opens its page in another tab, that tab can offer the next guide too; you do not have to return to the first tab to start it.

## Guides in the AI Assistant

An empty chat shows **Guides for this page**, or **Guides for you** when none fit the page. **See all guides** opens the guides panel. After a how-to answer, the AI assistant may suggest a fitting guide as a card. The card shows the guide’s title, estimated time, progress if already started, and a short reason. Use the round play button with the tooltip **Start**, or **Continue** for a guide in progress, or choose **Not now**. A suggestion waits for your choice. Starting any guide closes the assistant side panel so you can follow the screen. Some information tips also offer **Show me**.

## Points and Personal AI Credits

You earn 10 points the first time you finish each guide. Repeating it or finishing an updated version earns no extra points. You receive a one-time 50-point bonus when you have finished all guides currently available to you. Finishing Getting started alone is not enough for this bonus. New guides added later do not take the bonus away.

Your available points appear beside the gold star in the panel. With AI access and a company that limits AI use with credits, choose **Convert**. Check the points, credits and expiry date, then confirm with **Convert**. This converts your whole available balance: 1 point becomes 1 personal AI credit, usable for one month. For example, credits converted on November 20 remain usable through December 20.

If **Convert** is absent, you may have no points or no AI access, or your company may not limit AI use with credits. Your points keep counting while the company does not limit AI use.

Personal credits belong to you. Personal credits are used before company credits. While you have them, you can keep using the AI assistant even when the company’s credits or your monthly limit (**Limit per person** or your individual limit set by an Admin) are used up. Their use does not count toward that limit. While personal credits remain, the chat shows **Your credits:** with the amount and expiry date. See [your profile](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile) for account preferences.

## Assigned Guides and Team Progress

Admins can assign guides under **Admin Panel → Interactive guides**, using **People** or **Guides**. A role assignment also applies to people who receive that role later. Your assignments appear under **Assigned to you** in the guides panel, with the assigner's name when available. A guide you already finished counts as done. There are no due dates. In the team progress views under **Admin Panel → Interactive guides**, a person who finished an earlier version of a guide still shows **Updated**, which counts as done.

Assignment emails are on by default. In your profile's **Newsletter Preferences**, change **Email me when interactive guides are assigned to me**, then choose **Save Preferences**. You must verify your email address before changing this switch. Turning emails off leaves the assignments visible in the panel. [Assign Guides and Follow Team Progress](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assign-interactive-guides) explains the Admin steps.

## Limits and Help

Open a hall plan or an order before starting its guide. A machine guide can ask you to choose a machine first. Guides are unavailable on a kiosk display and while the PDA page is in full-screen mode. If a guide no longer fits the screen, read its notice and stop or return later.
