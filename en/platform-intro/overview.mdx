## Overview

Platform Intro collects the platform page reference and the task-based workflow guides in one place, so you can move from orientation to action without losing context.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Choose the view that matches your goal">
  Use **Pages** when you want to understand what a screen is for. Use **Workflows** when you want a guided sequence for completing a task.
</Callout>
