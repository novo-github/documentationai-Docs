## Goal

Review machines side by side using the same date and shift context.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Entry points: `/versus`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Versus page](/en/platform-intro/pages/versus).

### Preconditions
- At least two comparable machines are available.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the comparison page" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the comparison page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the machines to compare" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select the machines to compare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date or time window to evaluate" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose the date or time window to evaluate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the charts and summary blocks for the" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the charts and summary blocks for the selected machines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the comparison set or time range until the" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Adjust the comparison set or time range until the comparison answers the question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Which machines should be compared together.
- Whether the comparison should focus on a short operational window or a broader review period.

## Expected Result

Users can quickly see where one machine outperforms or underperforms another.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- The page is less useful when the machines do not share similar operating patterns.
- A narrow time range may show sparse data.
