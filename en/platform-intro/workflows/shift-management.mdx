## Goal
Define the shifts and shift groups that the platform uses for planning and machine calculations.

## Before You Begin
Admins responsible for factory calendars and shift structure.

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Roles: Admin
- Entry points: `/admin-panel`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Admin Panel page](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

### Preconditions
- The user has admin access.

## Steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the shift management area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Create or edit the needed shifts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Group shifts where the platform expects a reusable shift group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 5" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Revisit machines or scheduler views if the new shifts should be applied immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points
- Whether a new shift should stand alone or belong to an existing group.

## Expected Result
The platform can use the shift setup for weekly planning and daily machine interpretation.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases
- A shift definition can exist before it is assigned to any machine.
- Editing a shared shift structure may affect multiple machines.
