---

## Goal
Keep the operator list accurate so scheduling and production tracking reflect the right people.

## Before You Begin
Administrators managing workforce records.

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Roles: Admin
- Entry points: `/admin-panel`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Admin Panel page](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

### Preconditions
- The user has admin access.

## Steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the operator management area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Add a new operator or edit an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the operator details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the updated operator list in planning or machine-related pages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points
- Whether the user should create a new operator or update an existing one.

## Expected Result
Operators are available for scheduling and production-related workflows.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases
- Old or duplicate operator records make planning harder to trust.
- A new operator may exist in the platform before being assigned anywhere.
