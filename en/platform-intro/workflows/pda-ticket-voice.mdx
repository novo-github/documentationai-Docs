title: "Create a Ticket by Voice on the PDA Page"

---

## Goal

Create a ticket from your spoken description without filling in each detail by hand.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admins, Users and Machine operators need access to the machine, AI assistant access and permission to create tickets. The machine's BDE access template must allow **Fill in tickets with AI**. An Admin can check the [template setting](/en/platform-intro/pages/bde-access-templates#pda-ai).
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- Open the machine's PDA page → **Tickets**.
- Have the problem, people responsible and deadline ready. Include an order number if relevant.
- Each filling in counts as one AI credit, like a **Fast** answer.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the card's microphone" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Tap the microphone in the corner of **Add Ticket**. Recording starts immediately. Say what happened, which machine, who should take care of it and by when. For example: “The spindle on the Hermle C42 is leaking oil. Anna and Tom should check it by Friday. Order 4711 is affected.”

    Stop when finished. Recording also stops by itself after 2 minutes and keeps what you said as text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your words" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In **Your words**, correct the text or choose **Dictate more** to add to it. Choose **Fill with AI** to open the filled-in ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the ticket details" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the name, description, people, machines, orders, tags and due date. A sparkle marks each field filled by AI; a pencil appears after you change it.

    If a **Not found:** message names something, choose the matching person, machine, order or tag in the ticket yourself. To search **Orders**, type at least 2 characters of the order number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the ticket" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Press **Create Ticket**. Until then, no ticket has been created. If you try to leave with unsaved input, choose **Create ticket**, **Discard** or **Keep editing** in **Create this ticket?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with another problem if needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If your words described several tickets, only the first is filled in. After creating it, tap the card's microphone again: **Your words** opens with the rest instead of starting a recording. Check the text, fill in and create the next ticket. If the hint asks you to create a new ticket for the rest, describe that problem again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Want to fill in the ticket yourself? Tap the card away from the microphone to open an empty ticket.
- Need to change a detail? Correct it in the panel before **Create Ticket**.
- Need to leave without losing your input? Choose **Keep editing** in the closing question. **Discard** removes unsaved input.

## Expected Result

Your ticket is created with the details you checked. When there is another problem in your description, its words are ready for the next ticket after the first is created.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- No microphone on the card: ask an Admin to check your AI assistant access, ticket access and the machine's template. Without a template, this feature is off.
- Credits are used up: fill in the ticket yourself.
- Filling in failed: your words stay in the pop-up. Correct them or try again.
- The name is missing: enter a name when **Please give the ticket a name.** appears, then create the ticket again.
- Saving is unavailable during dictation: finish the recording and wait for the text, then save.
