## Goal

Save useful knowledge for colleagues at the machine, find an existing entry or ask the AI assistant without leaving the PDA page.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admins, Users and Machine operators need access to the machine and **Knowledge Base**. To use **Ask AI**, you also need AI assistant access and the machine's template must allow **Ask AI in the knowledge base tab**. Only the person who added an entry or an Admin can edit it.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- Open the relevant machine's PDA page.
- Have a tip, photo, document or question ready.
- Entries saved here are for the whole company, subject to machine access.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open knowledge and choose the order" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Knowledge Base**. In the order picker, check the operation marked **Running now**, which is pre-selected when work is running. Choose another operation marked **Up next** or **In the pool** if needed. Choose **No order** for knowledge about the machine alone. The product follows the chosen order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check existing knowledge" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Knowledge for this machine**, use **This machine**, **This order** and **This product** to show the entries you need. Enter a word in **Search**, or choose **Load more** for older entries. Tap an entry to read its text, picture or document. **Open in knowledge base** opens it in a new browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a useful tip or document" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In **Add to knowledge base**, type or dictate a note. Use **Take or add a photo** to open the camera on a tablet, or **Add a file** for a document. Check **Machine**, **Order** and **Product**. Use **Do not link** on the order or product to leave that link out; the machine stays linked.

    Choose **Save**. The text becomes a note and each photo or file becomes a separate entry with the same links. Wait for the new entries to be read and show their titles and summaries. Colleagues with access to this machine can find them in the knowledge base and through the AI assistant. Admins receive no email for these entries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question when AI is allowed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    To ask instead, write your question in the same box, optionally add a photo or file, and choose **Ask AI**. Read the answer below the box and tap its sources to check them. Follow-up questions and offered answer options continue the same conversation. Choose **Keep going** when offered. Review prepared changes before **Confirm** or **Cancel**.

    **New question** starts a new conversation. **Open in chat** opens this conversation in a new browser tab. The answer itself keeps you on the PDA page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct an entry when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open an entry you added, or one you may edit as an Admin, and choose **Edit**. Correct its title, type, summary or note. For a note written in this tab, you can also change **Text**. Dictation is available for text, summary and note. Choose **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Sharing knowledge with colleagues? Choose **Save**. Asking the assistant a question? Choose **Ask AI**.
- Does a tip apply to every order on this machine? Choose **No order**, or remove the order and product links before saving.
- Need to manage the document on the Knowledge Base page? Choose **Open in knowledge base** in its preview.

## Expected Result

Saved knowledge is linked to the right machine and chosen work. Colleagues with machine access can find it. If AI is allowed, you can read answers and continue the conversation in the tab.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- The knowledge tab is missing: ask an Admin to check your Knowledge Base page access.
- **Ask AI** is missing: ask an Admin to check your AI assistant access and the machine's [template setting](/en/platform-intro/pages/bde-access-templates#pda-ai). Without a template, AI is off here. You can still save knowledge.
- An older note or uploaded file has no editable text: you can change its title, type, summary and note, but not its text here.
- You need a private note: use the [Knowledge Base page](/en/platform-intro/workflows/knowledge-base) and keep **Private** selected. Entries saved from the PDA page are for the whole company, subject to machine access.
