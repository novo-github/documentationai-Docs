## Goal

Control live production tracking for a machine and record what happened during the shift.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Entry points: `/machines/:machineId/dataAcquisition`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Data Acquisition (PDA) page](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition).

### Preconditions
- The machine has a queue or a live order to work on.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PDA for the target machine" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the PDA for the target machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the available assignments and select the job" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the available assignments and select the job to work on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start or mark preparation depending on the current" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Start or mark preparation depending on the current shop-floor situation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop the work interval when the run finishes or" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Stop the work interval when the run finishes or pauses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter produced quantity and other completion" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Enter produced quantity and other completion details when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply timeline reasons" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Apply timeline reasons if downtime or special events need explanation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review open tickets or knowledge entries when the" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review open tickets or knowledge entries when the issue requires support context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether the current time belongs to preparation, active production, or a stop.
- Whether the job should use an existing assignment or a live immediate order.

## Expected Result

The machine timeline, interval list, and production records reflect what happened on the floor.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Users may need to stop an interval before switching to a different one.
- Missing quantity or reason details can leave the production story incomplete.
- A live issue may need both a timeline reason and a support ticket.
