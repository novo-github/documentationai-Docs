## Goal

Control live production tracking for a machine and record assignments, produced quantities, scrap, and interval context.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Any role with access to this machine.
- Entry points: `/machines/:machineId/dataAcquisition`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Data Acquisition (PDA) page](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition).

### Preconditions
- The machine has a queue or a live order to work on.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PDA for the target machine" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the PDA from Machine Details. If you are already viewing another machine's PDA, select its name in the top navigation bar and choose the target machine from the list grouped by hall. Only machines you can access appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the available assignments and select the job" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review open assignments or use a time-window view, then select the job to work on. Check the operation sequence number and available operation or assignment notes for context. Assignment columns and sorting can be personalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find unplanned work when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If the required operation is not planned, search for it and assign it directly to the machine from the PDA page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start production or preparation" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Start or mark preparation depending on the current shop-floor situation. If another assignment is already running, review the warning before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop the interval when work finishes or pauses" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Stop the work interval when the run finishes or pauses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter quantities and completion details" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    When stopping or continuing an interval, enter produced quantity, material scrap, work scrap, and other completion details when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply timeline reasons" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Apply timeline reasons if downtime or special events need explanation. Review idle and stopped entries after switching between running, paused, and stopped states to check that the record reflects what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review related tickets and knowledge" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review open tickets or knowledge entries when the issue requires support context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Correct an Interval Time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quick editor" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Double-tap the start or end time of the interval you want to correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the timestamp" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **+2m**, **+5m**, or **+30m**, or the corresponding minus buttons. Repeated taps add up: two taps on **+5m** move the timestamp forward by ten minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the resulting time" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the corrected start or end time. Use the full date/time picker when the required correction needs a specific date or a more precise time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the Data Acquisition V3 Display

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose whether to show interval bars" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open Data Acquisition V3 settings. Under **Timeline display**, turn **Show intervals in graph** on or off to suit the detail you want in the graph.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set automatic refresh frequency" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Auto-refresh interval**, choose how often assignments and hall pools refresh automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sort assignments" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the sort/filter options. Enable **Sort by Queue Position** to display the order set by dragging assignments, or turn it off to choose sorting rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the order of sorting rules" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select a field such as priority or date and choose ascending or descending order. Add further rules to break ties, then drag them into the order in which they should take precedence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review Quantities and Completion

Keep material scrap and work scrap in their separate fields when stopping or continuing an interval. Review the breakdown and combined scrap total in [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products) or [Order Analysis](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail); displayed total production includes submitted scrap.

Starting and stopping production updates the related order status. An assignment is marked complete when the produced quantity exactly matches the planned quantity. If an over-production prompt appears, review the quantities and order completion before continuing.

## Claim Work, Book During a Run, and Finish Safely

1. If work is in the **Pool**, claim an assignment from your machine's hall. If needed, search an unplanned operation or use **Find/Create Immediate Order**.
2. Use **Prep** for preparation, then **Start** for production. Review concurrent-order or unfinished-previous-step prompts before continuing.
3. Use **Mid Booking** to enter good quantity, **Mat. / MA**, and **Work / AA** while the order runs. It closes and restarts the interval. If restart fails, the quantities remain saved; press Start rather than booking them again.
4. In **Cumulative** mode, enter the full counter, for example 1450, rather than the increment.
5. At Stop, review the quantities. If over plan, choose **Put on hold** or **Mark as finished**. Label downtime in **Downtime reasons**.

Deleting an interval cannot be undone. Operators cannot open Orders & Products or Alert Rules; use the permitted PDA views or ask your admin for help. [PDA Assistant warnings](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition) can flag stale data and mismatched machine/order states.

## Respond to an Assistant Warning

An administrator must enable warnings for this machine first. Anyone with access to its PDA page can review them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read all active warnings" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select **View all warnings**. In **Active warnings**, read the order, duration, and issue. If **Live data is stale** appears, other warnings may be unreliable; use **Refresh now** and check whether fresh data arrives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address the cause" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the available action after checking the machine and order. **Select an order** helps find the work that should be running. **End order** lets you end a forgotten running order. **Set downtime reason** takes you to the reason selection. Confirm that the recorded work matches what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snooze a reminder when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Snooze one warning or choose **Snooze all** to hide and silence reminders for the configured duration. They remain listed under **Snoozed**. Use **Resume now** to bring one back sooner. If the cause persists, it returns when the snooze ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Control sound on your device" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Enable sound** or **Mute**. Tap the page once to allow browser sound. If it remains silent, ask an administrator to check sound settings, severity, and active-shift restrictions in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration). **Info** warnings are always silent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether the current time belongs to preparation, active production, or a stop.
- Whether the job should use an existing assignment or a live immediate order.
- Whether an unplanned operation should be assigned directly to the machine.
- Whether to continue when another assignment is already running.

## Expected Result

The machine timeline, interval list, and production records reflect what happened on the floor, including recorded material and work scrap.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Users may need to stop an interval before switching to a different one.
- Missing produced, scrap, or reason details can leave the production story incomplete.
- A live issue may need both a timeline reason and a support ticket.
- Recorded total production includes submitted scrap quantities.
