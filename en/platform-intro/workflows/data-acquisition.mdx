## Goal

Control live production tracking for a machine and record assignments, produced quantities, scrap, and interval context.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Any role with access to this machine.
- Entry points: `/machines/:machineId/dataAcquisition`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Data Acquisition (PDA) page](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition).

### Preconditions
- The machine has a queue or a live order to work on.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PDA for the target machine" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the PDA from Machine Details. If you are already viewing another machine's PDA, select its name in the top navigation bar and choose the target machine from the list grouped by hall. Only machines you can access appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the available assignments and select the job" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review open assignments or use a time-window view, then select the job to work on. Check the operation sequence number and available operation or assignment notes for context. Assignment columns and sorting can be personalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find unplanned work when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If the required operation is not planned, search for it and assign it directly to the machine from the PDA page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start production or preparation" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Start or mark preparation depending on the current shop-floor situation. If another assignment is already running, review the warning before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop the interval when work finishes or pauses" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Stop the work interval when the run finishes or pauses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter quantities and completion details" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    When stopping or continuing an interval, enter produced quantity, material scrap, work scrap, and other completion details when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply timeline reasons" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Apply timeline reasons if downtime or special events need explanation. Review idle and stopped entries after switching between running, paused, and stopped states to check that the record reflects what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review related tickets and knowledge" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review open tickets or knowledge entries when the issue requires support context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check Active Assistance and Automatic Ending

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assistance status" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    On Data Acquisition V3, tap **PDA Assist** at the right edge. It appears only while at least one assistance option or warning is on. Read what it does on this machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the current action" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Read **Right now**. An amber dot means automatic ending is counting down. Green means there is no countdown; a brief glow follows a time correction. Check the order and remaining minutes against what is happening on the machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change unwanted automatic ending" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If work should remain open, ask an administrator to turn off **Auto-end forgotten intervals** and select **Save** in the machine settings. Snoozing a warning or muting sound does not stop it. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose or Undo an Assisted Time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start or stop the interval" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the normal start or stop action. Assistance must be enabled for this machine. If a choice appears, compare **Now** and **After** and read which order gains or loses time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or keep the time" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose an offered time or expand **Other times** for earlier options. The most recent choices come first. Use the information icon beside the title to learn why data gaps under 10 minutes and interruptions under 2 minutes are ignored. Keep the time you pressed if no suggestion fits. Closing the initial dialog also keeps it. If a choice is no longer usable, keep the pressed time or try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct an automatic adjustment" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Undo** in the adjustment notice to restore the pressed time. **Change…** opens the options; choose another time or **Back to the time you pressed**. The timeline updates immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check automatically ended work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    An **Auto-ended** interval needs a quantity check. Book missing quantities afterwards. Snoozing or muting warnings does not prevent automatic ending; ask an administrator to change **Auto-end forgotten intervals** if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Label Downtime with an Available Reason

1. Open **Downtime reasons** and choose the tile matching the interruption. Read its code and name. Use category chips to narrow the list when available.
2. To correct an existing timeline label, open the period’s reason picker and choose an available reason, or **Remove label** to clear it.
3. If the reason is rejected because availability changed, review the updated list and choose again. Ask an administrator if the required reason is missing. See [Downtime Reasons FAQs](/en/help-center/faq/downtime-reasons).

## Correct an Interval

You need access to the machine's Data Acquisition V3 page, or access to the order page when editing from Order Management. The interval must already exist. Operators use Data Acquisition.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interval editor" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Data Acquisition V3, open the order's interval list. Tap a start or end time once to open **Edit interval** on **Start** or **End**. Select the pencil to start on **Quantities**. From [Order Management](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail), open the interval's menu and choose **Edit interval**; this also opens **Quantities**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved values" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Read the order, interval number, type, times, and quantities in the summary. Dates use day/month when shown, for example 20/10. **Still running** means the interval has no end yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct the start or an existing end" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Start** or **End**. On a touch screen, turn the day, hour, and minute wheels. On a computer, use **−** and **+** or type hours and minutes. **−5 min** and **+5 min** work on either device: two taps on **+5 min** move the time forward ten minutes. The other time stays visible on its card; tap it to switch sides. For a running interval, use the normal stop action before correcting its end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct quantities and type if needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Quantities**, enter **Good qty.**, **Material Scrap**, and **Work Scrap** with the keyboard or touch number pad. If cumulative mode is selected, enter the full machine counter reading. Choose **Production** or **Preparation** if the saved type is wrong. A running interval cannot switch to a type already running on the same order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save together" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Switching tabs keeps your edits. Check the changed times, the new duration beside **New length:** when both times exist, and any proposed type change in the summary. Select **Save**. **Interval saved.** confirms success; the table and timeline update. Use **Cancel** to discard edits before saving. If you already selected **Save**, check the table: cancelling cannot undo a save that finishes afterwards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the Data Acquisition V3 Display

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose whether to show interval bars" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open Data Acquisition V3 settings. Under **Timeline display**, turn **Show intervals in graph** on or off to suit the detail you want in the graph.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set automatic refresh frequency" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Auto-refresh interval**, choose how often assignments and hall pools refresh automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sort assignments" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the gear icon in the top bar to open **Card Display Settings → Sort Order**. Enable **Sort by Queue Position** to display the order set by dragging assignments, or turn it off to choose sorting rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the order of sorting rules" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose a rule such as priority or date and choose ascending or descending order. The rules form a numbered list: rule 1 decides first, and later rules break ties. To change their precedence, change the selection in a line’s list, remove a rule with the red remove button, or add a line with **Add sort rule**. **Reset to default** restores the default rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where waiting orders appear" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    With **Sort by Queue Position** off, click **Add sort rule**. A new numbered rule line appears. Select **Waiting on a previous step** in that line’s list. Instead of the up/down arrow, this rule has a button that starts at **At the bottom**, placing waiting orders after other work. Click it to switch to **At the top** and review waiting orders first. This rule always takes first place. Your remaining rules sort work within each group. With **Sort by Queue Position** on, choose **Orders waiting on a previous step → At the bottom**. Running and on-hold orders stay on top, followed by work that can start and then waiting work. Queue order stays the same within each group. Choose **Keep in place** to keep the usual queue display.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the machine list and pool" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Your waiting-order choice applies to the machine's order list and **Pool** and is saved for your account. Both options are off until you choose them. Once no earlier step remains unfinished, the order joins work that can start at the next refresh. These options are not offered in Assignment Planner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review Quantities and Completion

Keep material scrap and work scrap in their separate fields when stopping or continuing an interval. Review the breakdown and combined scrap total in [Orders & Products](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products) or [Order Analysis](/en/platform-intro/pages/order-detail); displayed total production includes submitted scrap.

Starting and stopping production updates the related order status. An assignment is marked complete when the produced quantity exactly matches the planned quantity. If an over-production prompt appears, review the quantities and order completion before continuing.

## Claim Work, Book During a Run, and Finish Safely

1. If work is in the **Pool**, claim an assignment from your machine's hall. If needed, search an unplanned operation or use **Find/Create Immediate Order**.
2. Use **Prep** for preparation, then **Start** for production. Review concurrent-order or unfinished-previous-step prompts before continuing.
3. Use **Mid Booking** to enter good quantity, **Mat. / MA**, and **Work / AA** while the order runs. It closes and restarts the interval. If restart fails, the quantities remain saved; press Start rather than booking them again.
4. In **Cumulative** mode, enter the full counter, for example 1450, rather than the increment.
5. At Stop, review the quantities. If over plan, choose **Put on hold** or **Mark as finished**. Label downtime in **Downtime reasons**.

Deleting an interval cannot be undone. Operators cannot open Orders & Products or Alert Rules; use the permitted PDA views or ask your admin for help. [PDA Assist warnings](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition) can warn about stale data and mismatched machine/order states.

## Respond to an Assistant Warning

An administrator must enable warnings for this machine first. Anyone with access to its PDA page can review them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read all active warnings" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select **View all warnings**. In **Active warnings**, read the order, duration, and issue. If **Live data is stale** appears, other warnings may be unreliable; use **Refresh now** and check whether fresh data arrives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address the cause" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the available action after checking the machine and order. **Select an order** helps find the work that should be running. **End order** lets you end a forgotten running order. **Set downtime reason** takes you to the reason selection. Confirm that the recorded work matches what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snooze a reminder when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Snooze one warning or choose **Snooze all** to hide and silence reminders for the configured duration. They remain listed under **Snoozed**. Use **Resume now** to bring one back sooner. If the cause persists, it returns when the snooze ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Control sound on your device" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Enable sound** or **Mute**. Tap the page once to allow browser sound. If it remains silent, ask an administrator to check sound settings, severity, and active-shift restrictions in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration). **Info** warnings are always silent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether the current time belongs to preparation, active production, or a stop.
- Whether the job should use an existing assignment or a live immediate order.
- Whether an unplanned operation should be assigned directly to the machine.
- Whether to continue when another assignment is already running.

## Expected Result

The machine timeline, interval list, and production records reflect what happened on the floor, including recorded material and work scrap.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- If **Save** is unavailable, change a value or resolve the time message. A changed end must be after the start; changed times must not be in the future. Wait if **Saving…** is shown.
- If **Saving did not work. Please try again.** appears, the editor stays open. Review your entries and try saving again.
- If a running interval cannot be loaded in Order Management, try opening it again. It stays closed until its saved values can be read.
- You cannot clear an interval's saved end. To end a running interval, use the normal stop action.
- Users may need to stop an interval before switching to a different one.
- Missing produced, scrap, or reason details can leave the production story incomplete.
- A live issue may need both a timeline reason and a support ticket.
- Recorded total production includes submitted scrap quantities.
