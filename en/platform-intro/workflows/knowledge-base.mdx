## Goal

Add useful knowledge to the Watchmen Platform, check it and choose who can see it.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admins, Users and Machine operators need access to **Knowledge Base**. Only the person who added a private document can share it. The person who added a document or an Admin who can see it can edit its details, retry processing or delete it.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- Have a file or written note ready. Check the [supported formats and limits](/en/platform-intro/pages/knowledge-base#processing).
- Decide whether to keep it private or share it with your company.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose who can see it" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Knowledge Base**. In the upload area, keep **Private** selected to make the document visible only to you, including when you ask the AI assistant. Choose **Whole company** if you want to share it immediately. If you add a file or note this way as a Machine operator, company Admins receive an email. Admins cannot see other people's private documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your document" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Attach your file and upload it, or write and save a note. Wait for the document to be read. Select it in the list to check its status. If processing fails, follow the reason shown and use **Try again** when offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the document details" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In **About this document**, compare **Title**, **Type**, **Summary** and **Answers questions like** with the document. The sparkle marks **AI-generated** details. Use the pencil with **Edit** to correct the title, type, summary or optional note, then **Save**. Changed details show **Edited** and stay when the document is read again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review suggested links" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the suggested machines, orders and products. Confirm correct links and reject incorrect ones. Choose **Confirm all** if every suggestion is right. Confirmed machine links affect which people can see a company document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it when you are ready" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    To share your private document, choose **Share with whole company**, read the confirmation and choose **Share**. When a Machine operator shares a document or adds a file or note for the whole company, company Admins receive an email; sharing as an Admin or User sends no email. There is no approval step. Skip this step to keep the document private.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and return to the source" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the eye button with **Open** to preview the document. Bookmark its address or send it to a colleague who has access. In an AI answer, selecting a document source opens the preview at the cited page or section. Download the file if the preview shows only the beginning or is unavailable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Need only your private documents? Choose **Private** above the list. **Whole company** shows shared documents you may see; **All** combines both.
- Want to stop sharing? The person who added the document or an Admin who can see it can choose **Make private** and confirm. It then belongs privately to the person who added it. An Admin making someone else's document private loses access too.

## Expected Result

Your document is available to the people allowed to see it. Its checked details and links help you understand what it covers, and its preview lets you read the source.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- No readable text found: upload a clearer copy, such as a sharper photo or the original file. Trying the same unreadable copy again does not help.
- A colleague cannot open it: check whether it is private, whether they have Knowledge Base access and whether confirmed machine links limit their access. Sending the address does not grant access. You always see your own documents.
- A company document linked only to machines you cannot access stays hidden unless you added it. Documents with no confirmed machine link remain visible under your machine access.
- Before deleting, check that you no longer need the document. Deletion cannot be undone; **Make private** keeps it for the person who added it.
