## Before You Begin

**Who can do this: Every signed-in role for their own account.** Open [User Profile](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile), route `/user`. 2FA adds a six-digit **OTP (One-time password)** after the normal sign-in step.

## Choose and Complete a Method

1. Open the profile's two-factor authentication settings.
2. Choose **Email code** to receive the sign-in code by email, or **Authenticator app**.
3. For an authenticator, scan the QR code with the app.
4. Enter the code from that app to finish setup. Do not stop after scanning alone.
5. If you cannot finish, use **Cancel and revert to email**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Abandoning authenticator setup halfway can leave the account using the authenticator method without a working code, locking you out at the next sign-in. Complete the code confirmation or revert to email before leaving.
</Callout>

## Expected Result and Troubleshooting

The selected method supplies the code for the second sign-in step. **Trust this device for next 30 days** skips that step on the trusted device for 30 days. Review signed-in devices in **Active Sessions**.

If setup is incomplete while you are still signed in, use the email-revert action. If you are already unable to sign in, use your organization's support path; recovery steps beyond this are not established by the reference. See [Help Center](/en/help-center).
