title: "Create a Product"
description: "Add a product record so future orders and work orders can reference it consistently."
---

## Goal

Create a reusable product entry that can be selected in future production orders.

## Before You Begin

Admins or planners who manage production master data.

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Roles: Admin, Manager
- Entry points: `/orders-products`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Orders & Products page](/en/platform-intro/pages/orders-products).

### Preconditions
- The user can access product management actions.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product creation flow" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the product creation flow from the orders and products area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product name or external identifier" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Enter the product name or external identifier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add units or planning values" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Add units or planning values if the team tracks them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach an image when visual identification matters" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Attach an image when visual identification matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the product" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the product so it becomes searchable for order creation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether the product needs only a basic identifier or fuller planning data.

## Expected Result

The product becomes available in product search and order-related flows.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Duplicate or inconsistent product naming makes later search harder.
- A product can exist before it is used in any order.
