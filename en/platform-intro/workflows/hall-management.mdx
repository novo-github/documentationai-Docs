title: "Manage Halls"
description: "Create, review, and maintain the hall structure used to group machines across the platform."
---

## Goal
Keep the factory hall structure accurate so machines can be grouped and viewed correctly.

## Before You Begin
Administrators responsible for plant structure.

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Roles: Admin
- Entry points: `/admin-panel`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

### Preconditions
- The user has the right administrative access.

## Steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the hall management area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Create a hall or open an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Set the hall name and any hall-level details the platform asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the hall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 5" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Revisit machine placement if the hall structure changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points
- Whether a hall should be created, renamed, or retired.

## Expected Result
Machines can be grouped by the correct hall in planning and monitoring views.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases
- Changing hall structure may require follow-up updates on machine records.
- Empty halls can exist before machines are assigned to them.
