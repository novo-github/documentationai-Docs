## Goal

Assign learning tasks and see which assigned guides your team has finished.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admin only. Open **Admin Panel → Interactive guides**. You see your own company’s people and available guides.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- Interactive guides are available in your company.
- The guide is available to the people you want to assign it to.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a person" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **People** and select a row. Under **Available**, tick the guides the person should complete and choose **Assign**, or the button showing the number of guides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign by guide when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Alternatively, open **Guides** and select a guide. Under **Assign to**, choose **People** and pick recipients, or **A whole role** and pick a role. Choose **Assign**. Everyone with that role receives it, including people who receive the role later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review completion" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Return to **People** to see assigned completion and last activity; open a person to inspect their guide status. Under **Guides**, open a guide to see **Who has it** and their progress. A previous finish already counts as done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove an assignment when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose the ✕ beside an assignment, with the tooltip **Remove assignment**. Remove a role assignment from the guide’s list; it cannot be removed for just one person from that person’s list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Assign to one person for an individual task; assign to a whole role for onboarding that also covers later joiners.
- A search by name or email appears in People when there are more than 25 people.

## Expected Result

Recipients see the guide under **Assigned to you**. Assignment emails can notify people with a verified email who have kept those emails switched on.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Assignments have no due dates.
- People see only guides they can access.
- Switching assignment emails off in the profile keeps the assignment visible.
