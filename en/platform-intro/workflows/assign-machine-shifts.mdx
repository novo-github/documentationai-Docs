---
title: "Assign Machine Shifts"
description: "Plan or update the shift schedule for one or more machines over a selected week."
---

## Goal
Set the planned shift coverage for machines across a selected week.

## Before You Begin
Admins or planners responsible for weekly machine coverage.

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Roles: Admin, Manager
- Entry points: `/scheduler`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Scheduler page](/en/platform-intro/pages/scheduler).

### Preconditions
- The machine list is available.
- Shift definitions already exist.

## Steps
<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select one or more machines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose the week to plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the machine rows and daily columns for that week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Mark the shifts that should apply on each day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 5" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the pending changes when the weekly plan looks correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points
- Whether to plan a single machine or a filtered group.
- Whether to keep pending edits or clear them before saving.

## Expected Result
The weekly shift plan is stored and reused by machine-level views and reporting.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases
- An empty state appears until machines are selected.
- Unsaved changes remain local until the user clicks save.
- The view is week-based, so users may need to move backward or forward to reach the right planning window.
