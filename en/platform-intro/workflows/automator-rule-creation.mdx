## Before You Begin

**Who can do this: Admin only.** Open [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator) and start creating a rule. Know which machines to monitor and who should receive alerts. Check the chosen template's prerequisites; sensor-based rules do not trigger for work centers.

## Create and Review

1. Choose **Start from a template** or **Start from scratch**. A template prefills settings but creates nothing yet. Check its listed prerequisites.
2. Name the rule and select machines. No selection means all machines. If a warning names selected work centers and machine or sensor measurements, the rule never triggers for those work centers. Review the destinations and conditions before continuing.
3. Check the conditions and thresholds. For example, **Preparation taking too long** starts at 120% of planned setup time; **Production behind expected pace** starts at a 15% shortfall. Adjust these for your process. Preparation and scrap values update with PDA entries.
4. Set the check frequency, required duration, active days, active-shift restriction, and cooldown. Review every value marked **From the template – please review**, then select **Mark as reviewed**.
5. Choose email recipients, response time, and escalation recipients. Decide whether the alert should resolve automatically after its condition clears and whether to send another email on resolution.
6. In **Review**, read **What this alert will do** and **Your settings**. Check warnings about missing recipients, all-machine scope, or a switched-off rule. Without recipients, alerts appear in Events but nobody receives an email. Escalation also needs its own recipients.
7. Save and check the rule in the list. Open a triggered event to inspect its details before acknowledging or resolving it.

## Create a Delivery or Delay Alert

1. As an administrator, start a rule and choose conditions from **Forecaster**. Select **Delivery Date at Risk** and a comparison greater than 0 to catch any expected finish after delivery.
2. Choose the machines and review the condition’s units. Late finishes, overdue starts, and long holds use minutes. **Assignments Pushed** and **Delivery Dates Pushed** count affected work.
3. Keep schedule conditions in their own rule. They cannot be combined with machine or order conditions. The active-shift restriction is not used for this rule.
4. Choose recipients, review timing and the rule summary, then save. Open a resulting event and use **Show in Gantt** to inspect the cause before changing the plan.

See [Forecaster alerts](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator) for all six conditions.

## Expected Result and Common Problems

An enabled, saved rule checks its selected machines and raises alerts when its conditions and timing are met. A switched-off rule stays saved until enabled.

If no alert appears, check the rule's status, machines, active days, shifts, required data, and minimum duration. A template alone is not a saved rule. If options fail to load, select **Retry**. If an alert appears without an email, check recipients; if escalation sends no email, check escalation recipients too.

Acknowledging an event and resolving it are different actions. See [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator) for response times and event details, and [Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition) for production entries.
