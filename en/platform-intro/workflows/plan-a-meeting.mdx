## Goal

Book a meeting with the Novo AI team and check whether it is confirmed or awaiting approval.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admins, Users and Machine operators can book a meeting through **Help & Support** while signed in and a meeting is available. Start at the right edge of the screen, using a computer or a tablet with a wide view.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- The **Help & Support** tab is visible. It is hidden on narrow screens, including phones.
- **Talk to our team** offers at least one meeting.
- You have an email address where you can receive the confirmation.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting calendar" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Help & Support → Talk to our team** at the right edge. Select the meeting card, even if only one is offered, to open its calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a day and time" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select an available day and time in the calendar. If you chose the wrong meeting, use the round back-arrow button at the top of the dialog with the tooltip **Back** to return to Help & Support. It is available even with one meeting option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your details and complete the booking" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the details shown and complete the booking as directed by the calendar. Your name and company are filled in when available. Only a verified email is filled in; otherwise, enter your address in the form. Opening the calendar alone books nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the date, time and email information. **Meeting booked** means confirmed; **Request sent** means you must wait for confirmation by email. If offered, use **Open meeting link**. Choose **Done** to close.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Calendar not loading? Choose **Open in a new tab** when the loading message appears, or use the round button at the top of the dialog with the tooltip **Open in a new tab**.
- Several meetings available? Read the card names and short information before choosing. Each reopening starts the choice again.

## Expected Result

You see either a confirmed meeting or a request awaiting confirmation. A confirmed meeting comes with email information; a request is followed by an email once confirmed.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Missing tab: use a wider screen or a wider tablet view. Help & Support is unavailable on phones and the PDA page. If **Talk to our team** is missing, no meetings are offered.
- Empty email: enter your address in the booking form. An unverified account email is not filled in.
- Different calendar language: the booking tool controls its own language; changing the app language changes the cards and confirmation text.
