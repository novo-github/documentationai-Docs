---
title: "Manage Alert Rules"
description: "Create, edit, scope, and review alert rules so users are notified when machine conditions cross important thresholds."
---

## Goal

Keep alert logic useful, targeted, and easy to review.

## Before You Begin

Administrators who maintain proactive notifications.

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Roles: Admin
- Entry points: `/alert-rules`, `/alert-rules/:machineId`, `/machines/:machineId/alert-rules`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Alert Rules page](/en/platform-intro/pages/alert-rules).

### Preconditions
- The user has admin access.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the alert rules page globally or from a" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the alert rules page globally or from a specific machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review existing rules by machine group or global" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review existing rules by machine group or global scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new rule or edit an existing one" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Start a new rule or edit an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger condition, threshold, scope," icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose the trigger condition, threshold, scope, recipients, and active time windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule and confirm it appears in the rule" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the rule and confirm it appears in the rule list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a rule when you need to inspect past alerts" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Expand a rule when you need to inspect past alerts or resolve existing alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether the rule should apply to all machines or one machine.
- Whether an alert needs to be resolved, reopened, or deleted from history.

## Expected Result

The platform can notify the right people when the selected conditions happen.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Too many rules can generate noisy notifications.
- A rule that is too broad may affect more machines than intended.
