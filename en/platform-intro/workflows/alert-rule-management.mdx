## Goal

Keep alert logic useful, targeted, and easy to review.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Admin, or a User with page access to **Alert Rules**; never Machine operator.
- Entry points: `/alert-rules`, `/alert-rules/:machineId`, `/machines/:machineId/alert-rules`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Alert Rules page](/en/platform-intro/pages/alert-rules).

### Preconditions
- Admin, or a User with page access to **Alert Rules**; never Machine operator.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the alert rules page globally or from a" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the alert rules page globally or from a specific machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review existing rules by machine group or global" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review existing rules by machine group or global scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new rule or edit an existing one" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Start a new rule or edit an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger condition, threshold, scope," icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose at least one machine, the trigger condition, a duration of at least 1 minute for time thresholds, recipients, and active time windows.

    For **Continuous Idle Duration** or **Continuous Off Duration**, switch on **Also outside the shift** if you need alerts at night, at weekends, between shifts or on machines without shifts. Leave it off to watch only during shifts. It is off by default and is not offered for other alert types.

    Check **Days of Week**. The selected days still apply outside shifts, using the company's local day when the continuous idle or off period exceeds your threshold.

    Read any notice about machines with active shifts before saving. It tells you whether the rule never fires for these machines or how many selected machines it will skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule and confirm it appears in the rule" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select **Create** for a new rule or **Update** for an existing rule. Confirm it appears in the rule list. If you enabled **Also outside the shift**, this label appears beside its conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a rule when you need to inspect past alerts" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Expand a rule when you need to inspect past alerts or resolve existing alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review Channel Costs and Data Prerequisites

Before choosing SMS, review the **€0.10 per message per user** cost and explicit-consent requirement. One message to three users costs €0.30. With no recipients, alerts are still recorded at no cost. Webhooks can use Basic, Bearer, or custom-header authentication.

Sensor-based rules do not trigger for work centers. Read active-shift limitations shown by the rule. Use [Automator](/en/platform-intro/pages/automator) for its separate admin-only conditions, timing, escalation, and event workflow.

Check the hall groups when choosing machines. **Work center: never triggers** identifies a workplace for which the rule cannot alert. Review this after changing a machine to a work center.

## Decision Points

- For **Continuous Idle Duration** or **Continuous Off Duration**, whether to watch only during shifts or also outside them.
- Which machines the rule should monitor.
- Whether an alert needs to be resolved, reopened, or deleted from history.

## Expected Result

The platform can notify the right people when the selected conditions happen.

For **Continuous Idle Duration** and **Continuous Off Duration**, each uninterrupted period exceeding your threshold gives one alert, without repeats across shifts. This applies whether **Also outside the shift** is off or on.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Too many rules can generate noisy notifications.
- A rule that is too broad may affect more machines than intended.
