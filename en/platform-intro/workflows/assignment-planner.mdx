## Goal

Turn unassigned work into machine queues or shared hall pools and review how future assignments fit across machines and time.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Admin, or an account with **Assignment Planner** page access, plus the relevant machine access.
- Entry points: `/assignment-planner`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Assignment Planner page](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).

### Preconditions
- Machines are available for planning.
- Orders or operations already exist in the system.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the visible hall or machine list" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select up to 25 machines and the halls whose pools you need. Pools do not count toward the machine limit. Narrow work with order tags, order and operation priorities, or the status of individual assignments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open search with Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) or the search icon in **Filter & Sort**. Search for an order, product, or customer. Choose **Search in → Unassigned**, **Hall pools**, **Machines**, or **Everything**. Esc or × clears and closes the search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a machine or hall pool" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drag the operation onto a machine column when its destination is known. If several machines in a hall could perform it, drag it into that hall's Hall Pool or assign a new operation there. Use the readiness badge to see when a machine can claim the pooled work.

    For work without a connected sensor, choose a destination marked **Work center**. Review the destination before confirming; the work center shows orders without sensor KPIs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Reorder work inside the queue to adjust the planned sequence. Enable **Sort by Queue Position** in the sort/filter options to display that manual order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update key details" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Update key details such as status, dates, or priority when needed. Review or edit operation and assignment notes to keep instructions with the work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the future schedule" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open Assignment Gantt and select up to 25 machines. Close the machine selection menu to apply your choices. Inspect the timeline for overlaps and open a related order when needed. Gantt has no hall-pool columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange future assignments when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Improve plan**, compare the alternatives, and review the assignments that would change before choosing **Save as plan**. Running and on-hold work keep their place. Follow the detailed comparison steps below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the queue changes" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Let the planner save the queue changes back to the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep Hall Pools Beside the List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the placement" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    On a wide screen, open **Hall pools → Where to show them → Beside the list**. Your choice is saved for your account. Choose **In the board** to return pools to the machines’ layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the halls" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Which pools to show**, tick the halls you need. This also changes the hall filter. Each pool appears beside **Unassigned operations** and stays in view while you scroll through the machines. If the list is hidden, pools start at the left edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make room and place work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Collapse pool** to leave a narrow strip. If your search covers the pool, close it first so the work lands at the very end. Otherwise, a drop on the strip can land among the cards. **Unassigned** does not filter pools. You can drop onto the strip even while it is collapsed. Started work opens the handover dialog; after confirmation, its remainder goes to the top. Click the strip or use **Expand pool** to see its cards again. This browser remembers collapsed pools for the current company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Search the List, Pools, or Machines

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Press Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) or select the search icon in **Filter & Sort**, even when the unassigned list is hidden. Enter an order, product, or customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to search" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Search in**, choose **Unassigned** for the list, **Hall pools** for pooled work, **Machines** for work on machines, or **Everything** for all three. Other areas keep showing their cards. The default is **Unassigned**, and your choice is remembered. **Machines** does not search machine names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the matches and clear search" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If a queue has more than 40 matches, use **Show 40 more**, or the smaller remaining number, to reveal more. When the search includes the unassigned list, check its found count. Press Esc or × to close and clear search; other filters remain in effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A link with a search uses **Everything** for that visit without replacing your saved choice.

## Move Work to Another Queue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an assignment that has not started" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Find the card in its machine or hall-pool column. For pooled work, check the readiness badge before choosing the machine that will take it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag the card to its destination" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drag it directly onto the destination machine or hall-pool column. You do not need to unassign it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its queue position" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    With **Sort by Queue Position** on, drop the card at the position you want. Wait for the move to finish saving, then check its position and operation priority. With sorting rules on, **Moves** chooses the start or end of the queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Started, stopped work uses the handover steps below. Running work must stop first. Finished and cancelled work cannot change destination.

<a id="hand-over-work" />

## Hand Over Started Work to Another Queue

Administrators and users with access to the Orders page can choose any hall pool or another machine they can access. Every user needs access to the operation’s current machine. Other users can only return the operation to the pool of its current machine’s hall, in Assignment Planner or Data Acquisition. The operation must have started and then stopped. Finished or cancelled operations cannot be handed over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check that the work is stopped" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    A running card stays locked with **Running. Stop it on the machine before moving it.** Use the normal stop action in [Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition) and record the quantities first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag the stopped operation to its destination" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drop the card on a hall-pool or another machine column. The handover dialog opens even if started work is marked **Planned** or **Released**. Moving within the same column reorders the queue and can also change the operation priority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review quantities and pass on the reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check **Planned**, **Done here**, and **Left**. Review **Quantity left**: only good parts reduce the suggested remainder. Change it if parts need to be made again, or leave it empty if unknown. If nothing is left, finish the operation unless more parts are needed. Add **Reason (optional)** for the next operator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and review the queues" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the destination, then choose **Back to pool** or **Move** for a machine. There is no automatic undo for a handover. The original machine keeps its times and good parts, marked **Handed over**. The rest goes to the top of the destination queue, even when dropped onto a collapsed pool. Both queues update. The operation remains open until the remainder is finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, if 40 of 100 planned parts are good, the original machine keeps 40 and the next machine receives 60. Hover over a grey badge to check the destination or source and any reason. If the operation changed during review, inspect the refreshed queues before trying again.

## Reorder Work and Check Its Priority

Use your normal Planner and machine access. The link between queue order and operation priority must be enabled in the company settings described below.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use queue-position mode" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Filter & Sort → Sort by Queue Position**. Clear filters and any search covering the queue if you need to see every position and its number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the operation" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drag the card to its new position within the machine or hall pool. Running and on-hold cards keep the positions you set. Running work can move within its column; stop it before changing its destination. Wait for each move to save before reordering the destination again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the changed priority" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Read the notice and the dashed **OP** chip. Moving ahead of more urgent or equally urgent work within the queue can raise the priority; moving behind can lower it. With the default levels, moving the second of two OP70 operations to the top makes it OP100. To change a tie, the next company level must fit between the surrounding operations. A priority that already fits can stay unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep or undo the priority change" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select **Undo** in the priority notice if you want the old priority back. The card stays where you dropped it. For a split operation, check all parts: the new priority appears everywhere, but this drag does not reorder the other queues.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Expected ends and date checks refresh after the move. Hover over the date marker to review its explanation. If expected ends stay the same, hover over **Manual queue order**. The chip appears only while **Filter & Sort → Sort by Queue Position** is on. For forecasts following planned or delivery dates, its explanation says why dragging does not change expected ends and how **Improve plan** can set new planned dates. See [queue and priority details](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner#queue-order-and-operation-priority).

## Set an Operation Priority and Filter the Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the priority to change" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    On an operation or queue card, select the dashed **OP** chip to set **Operation priority**. Use **P** to change **Order priority** instead. The two values are independent. In queue-position mode, changing **Operation priority** can move the card and any split parts in other accessible queues. Equal-priority cards keep their relative order. Administrators can switch off this link between priority and position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose matching priorities" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Order priority** and **Operation priority** to select one or more values. Type a number if it is absent from the legend. With both filters set, work must match both in the unassigned panel and machine columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the remaining filters" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If work disappears, review the status, tags, and machines. For missing work in the unassigned list, also check the import filter. It narrows only that list to one ERP upload. Remove any unwanted order, product, or customer filter chip above the board. Open the information icon for **Assignment Planner Guide** for help.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sort and Highlight Planning Cards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose manual order or sorting rules" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In the sort/filter options, enable **Sort by Queue Position** to use the order set by dragging assignments. Turn it off to sort by fields such as priority or date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set rule precedence" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Operation Planned Start** or **Operation Planned End** to sequence individual operations. Choose **Order Planned Start** or **Order Planned End** to keep an order’s operations together. Set ascending or descending order. Add rules to break ties and drag them into the order of precedence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Emphasize the details you need" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Highlight** and select fields from Order, Work Order, Product, Operation, Planning, and Assignment. Check the cards for your selected highlights, such as priority, due date, or customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Re-sort a Queue

You need planning access and access to the machine. The machine or hall pool must contain work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the preview" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Assignment Planner, enable **Filter & Sort → Sort by Queue Position**. Choose **Re-sort** on the last line of the machine or hall-pool header. In Assignment Gantt, use **Re-sort** on the machine row. The proposal includes active work hidden by filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what determines the sequence" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **1 · Sort by**, choose **Order priority**, **Operation priority**, **Planned end**, **Delivery date**, or **Order date**. The company default is listed first and selected when supported. Urgent priorities or early dates come first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep selected operations in place" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Clear **Can move** for any operation that must keep its exact place. **Select none** fixes all operations; **Select all** allows them to move. Running and on-hold work can move in this sort. Wait until **Updating…** disappears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and apply" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review current and new places, the chosen priority or date, and **Work left**. Use **Columns** for optional details or click a row for **Details**. Select **Apply sequence** to save, or **Cancel** to leave the queue unchanged. Priorities do not change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or undo" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Undo** in the confirmation to restore the previous sequence. If the queue changes during review, check the refreshed proposal. In Gantt, if **Undo** cannot be saved, the message **The old sequence could not be put back. The board now shows the current plan.** appears and the board reloads. Check the current queue before changing it again. This is a one-time sort; later imports join the end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With queue sorting on and no filters or relevant search, card numbers show the positions in each queue. Clear priority, operation priority and tag filters, restore the usual status selection, and clear search in that area if numbers are missing.

See [Re-sort a Queue](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner#re-sort-a-queue) for sorting rules and limits.

## Set How the Company Uses Priorities

You need an administrator account. These settings affect priority sorting, queue moves, and the operation priorities in searched proposals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the priority settings" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Admin Panel → Priority Levels → How priorities are used** below the levels table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the urgency direction" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Which number is more urgent?**, choose **Higher number = more urgent** or **Lower number = more urgent**. Higher numbers are the default. Applying SAP, Epicor, or Infor presets also sets their direction; resetting the levels restores higher numbers first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether queue and priority affect each other" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Leave **Queue order and operation priority stay in step** on to link dragging and card priority changes. Turn it off to let dragging change only order and priority changes leave cards in place. Open **How does this work?** to review examples with your levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that the choice was saved" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Each setting saves immediately. Wait for **Setting saved.** If saving fails, the previous choice returns; try again. An amber notice remains visible while the link is off. Review a queue in Assignment Planner using **Sort by Queue Position**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your own sorting-rule directions remain as set. See [company priority settings](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel#how-priorities-are-used).

## Save or Use Company Sorting

You need an administrator account to save the company default. Other users with access to the relevant screen can reset their own sorting to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set and save the company choices" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    As an administrator, open **Filter & Sort** in Assignment Planner. Set the mode, sort rules, and **Moves** choice, then select **Save as company default**. Wait for **Saved as company default**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the default in the Planner" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If you have no personal sorting, you start with the company default. To replace personal sorting, use **Reset to company default** in the sort section. It saves the current company mode, rules, and **Moves** choice as your own settings. Without a company default, **Reset to default (no company default set yet)** restores the standard choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the default at the terminal" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Data Acquisition V3, open **Card Display Settings → Sort Order → Reset to company sorting**, then **Save**. The button shows a tick and is unavailable when the sorting already matches. Without a company default, use **Reset to default (no company default set yet)**, then **Save**. Queue-mode choices for running and waiting orders remain unchanged; sort rules are replaced. Add any special running- or waiting-order rules again if needed. Follow the [display setup steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#configure-the-data-acquisition-v3-display).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A saved personal choice or reset does not follow later company-default changes. Reset again when you want to copy newer company choices.

## Review Forecaster Warnings Before Changing the Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check which date the warning refers to" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Forecaster check** in Assignment Planner and review the selected date. The card’s date marker compares the job with this date: red means late and amber means **at risk**. An on-time operation with an expected end shows a small clock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check why this operation ends then" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Hover over its date tab or small clock. Read **Expected end** and the date checks, then **Based on** for assumptions. Under **Why this time**, check the machine, earlier operation, or shift it waits for. **Earlier operations of this order** helps you find missing machine assignments. If details fail to load, hover again.

    Long-held, never-started, and undated operations still get expected times. **No forecast** means hall-pool work or an unavailable forecast. Assign pool work to a machine and review the result. If an earlier operation has no machine yet, the later operation’s time may be too optimistic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the explanation" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    For general help, open **Forecaster check → How does the Forecaster work?**. This opens the help without changing your selected date check. Read what the colours mean and when times may be too early, then close the help to return to planning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the expected times" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt). Open **View options → Board → Bars show → Expected times** to include delays or **Planned dates** to inspect the plan. The information icon explains the bars. You can also change **View options → Board → Row height**; both choices are your own and take effect immediately. Open **Attention** to review the most important warnings first. Follow the related order to check operations without a machine or date. Also check planned durations, machine shifts, and whether current work has been recorded on the PDA/BDE.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether to change the plan" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Reading the forecast changes nothing. If you want to compare waiting-job sequences, open **Improve plan**. Review the comparison before choosing **Save as plan**. Saving changes planned dates and queues, can be undone, and never changes delivery dates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the Forecaster has no expected end, the Planner only compares the job's date with today. If expected times are unavailable in Assignment Gantt, you see planned times without delays while the Forecaster tries again. Neither view confirms that delivery will be on time.

## Find Late Orders and Plan Missing Operations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the orders needing work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    With access to Orders & Products, open **Forecaster**. Choose **Late for delivery → Show in table** or **Of these, operations not planned → Show in table**. Date range and status filters pause; order, product, customer, and machine filters still apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find orders absent from the board" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Not planned at all → Show list**. Open an order and select **Assign operations** or **Open in planner**. These orders have a delivery date but no operation on a machine or in a hall pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the missing earlier operation" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Assignment Gantt, inspect **Attention → Earlier operation not planned**. Follow **Show that operation**, then **Assign to a machine** when available. Select a machine you may plan on. An operation waiting in a hall pool needs a machine too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check dates and the new forecast" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    The assignment uses the operation’s own planned dates. If no planned start exists, set dates before treating it as planned. Review the recalculated times for later operations and check whether the order still risks missing delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare and Save a Plan

You need planning access and access to the machines you want to change. Have waiting work on those machines. Check missing earlier operations, durations, and shifts before relying on forecast improvements.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Improve plan for the right machines" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Assignment Planner, enable **Sort by Queue Position** and select the machine header’s sparkle **Improve plan**. Choose **This machine** or **All machines in &lt;hall&gt;** and select machines. In Gantt, these controls appear only with planning access. The toolbar sparkle beside **Forecaster** covers the board’s machines; a row sparkle covers that machine or its hall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the options" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **1 · Choose an option**, compare late orders and delay against today. Choose **Best operation sequence** or a fixed rule. Within the searched sequence, choose **Keep priorities**, **Least delay**, or **Fewest changes**. **Recommended** marks the suggestion. Read **Why this sequence?** for its reasons. With **Delivery date first**, use Gantt to compare the fixed rules. **Best operation sequence** is greyed out because a searched sequence cannot change expected ends; no search starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what may move" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    For **Best operation sequence**, clear **Can move** for work that must keep its exact place. Use a machine group’s checkbox for all its operations. Running and on-hold operations stay in place. The table first shows only operations that change place or whose status changes. Select **Show all (N more operations)** for the rest, including unchanged machines, or **Show only the changes** to return. Fixed rules have no **Can move** controls. Wait for any **Updating…** to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what saving changes" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **2 · What changes**, review queue places, **Expected end** before and after, priority changes, and the count of new planned dates. Operations outside your selection with changed expected ends appear under **Also changes expected ends on other machines**. Click a row for **Details** and check its assumptions. In Gantt, **Show on board** previews an alternative fixed rule without saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or keep searching" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Search longer** when offered if you want more sequences checked. **Stop and show the best so far** ends the search with what it found. When satisfied, select **Save as plan**. This keeps delivery dates, original operation dates, and the company’s forecast rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or undo the saved plan" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the updated board. **Undo** restores the saved queue, priority, and planned-date changes. If a conflict offers **Recalculate**, review a new proposal before saving. In Gantt, if **Undo** cannot be saved, the message **The old sequence could not be put back. The board now shows the current plan.** appears before the board reloads. Check the current plan before making another change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Distribute Pool Work

You need planning access, access to the chosen machines, and waiting operations in a hall pool. In Assignment Planner, enable **Sort by Queue Position**. The distribution button is absent when the forecast uses **Delivery date first**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the hall pool proposal" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Distribute to machines** on the last line of the hall-pool header. The dialog is titled **Distribute &lt;hall&gt; pool to machines**. Select the machines to use. The pool list also includes work hidden by your board filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose work to place and work to keep still" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Leave **Place** checked for pool operations to include. Clear it to leave an operation in the pool. Clear **Can move** on existing machine operations that must keep their exact place. No pool work can be inserted ahead of those fixed operations. A machine group’s checkbox covers all its existing operations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review destinations and remaining pool work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Compare the option cards **Keep priorities**, **Least delay**, and **Fewest changes**, with one marked **Recommended**. Each compares late orders and delay with leaving the work in the pool. The summary shows, for example, **3 operations placed, 1 stays in the pool.** and **N late orders instead of M if they stayed in the pool.** It compares late orders after placing with what would happen if the work stayed in the pool. Read each placed row’s **→ &lt;machine&gt;, place N** and expected end. Reasons for unplaced work start with **Stays in the pool:**. Review priority changes, new planned dates, and effects on other machines. If the search did not reach an operation, try **Search longer** when offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and check" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Wait for **Updating…** to finish, then choose **Apply this sequence**. Check the cards on their machines. **Undo** returns placed work to the pool in its previous order and restores the other saved changes. If the pool changes, review the updated list or choose **Recalculate** when offered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In **Improve plan**, select **All machines in &lt;hall&gt;**. If the pool has waiting work, **Also place the N operations waiting in the pool** appears, initially unchecked. Check it to show pool rows with **Place** boxes, then select the operations to include. See [pool distribution](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner#distribute-pool-work-to-machines) for limits and reasons work stays in the pool.

## Move One Assignment After Checking Its Effect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the move" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open an assignment in Assignment Gantt. Select **Move → Earlier**, **Later**, **Run next**, or **To machine…**. Read the explanation if the action is unavailable. Running, on-hold, and finished work stays in place. Recorded work prevents a machine change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review other work before saving" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the expected start and end, other assignments pushed later, and changes to late orders. Save after reviewing the assignments that will be replanned on the machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or undo the move" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the updated queue. Use **Undo** if needed. If someone has changed the queue since, automatic undo is unavailable; review it and move the assignment back by hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the Plant’s Forecast Rule

Only Admins and Super Admins can change company planning settings. Everyone else who can open Gantt can read them and sees **Ask an admin to change these.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shared settings dialog" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Assignment Gantt, open **View options → Planning**. **View options → Board** holds your own display choices, which take effect immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company rule and any preview" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review **How does your plant decide what runs next?** and the [ordering choices](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt#planning-settings). If you are previewing a different rule on the board, the note **The board is showing a preview: &lt;rule&gt;.** names it here. Previewing does not change the company rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the company choices" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose the rule and set **Hold limit (working hours)** between 1 minute and 720 hours, then choose **Save**. Confirm the displayed forecast rule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only working time counts toward the hold limit. Without planned shifts, 24 hours count as the full limit. Once exceeded, **On hold for a long time** appears. The operation stays in the forecast and keeps its queue position; later work still waits for it.

## Review Conflicts After an Order Import

When [Order Upload](/en/platform-intro/pages/erp-upload-planner) flags a conflict for manual resolution, use the order and operation details in the import summary to find the affected work in the planner. Review its current destination, status, notes, and related order before changing the plan.

Move an eligible assignment to the intended queue when appropriate. If work has started, review its recorded quantities and stop it before handing over the remainder. Finished work stays on its machine. The import summary explains when a decision could not be applied because production changed after review.

## Check Move Eligibility and Queue Changes

Unstarted planned or released work moves directly. Started, stopped work opens the handover dialog. Running work must stop first, and finished or cancelled work stays on its machine. Up to 25 machines can be shown; hall pools do not count toward that limit.

For moves without a dialog, with **Sort by Queue Position** enabled, a drop sets the exact position. Running and on-hold assignments keep the position you set within their column. With it disabled, sorting rules control the view and **Moves** chooses the start/end destination. If another change refreshes the queue while you reorder, redo the reorder against the refreshed queue.

Use [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt) to inspect the timeline. Its drag-to-reschedule mode is **Experimental and read-only**; use supported planner controls for changes.

Use the view switch to open Assignment Gantt for a timeline review. Use its **Move** controls for eligible work or return to Assignment Planner to work with queues.

## Decision Points

- Whether to hand over stopped work to a hall pool or directly to another machine.
- Whether to keep pools beside the list or within the board.
- Whether to search only the list, pools, machines, or all three.
- Whether to choose a specific machine now or leave the work in a hall pool.
- Whether a job should stay unassigned, move to another machine, or be removed from a queue.
- Which machines should appear in the Gantt timeline.
- Whether overlap warnings should be resolved manually or through automatic arrangement of future assignments.
- Whether expected times include all operations, durations, and shifts before you rely on them.
- Whether to save a compared plan or leave the existing plan unchanged.
- Whether to keep a priority change caused by dragging or use **Undo** to restore the priority while keeping the position.

## Expected Result

The machine queue reflects the latest planning decision, future assignments follow the accepted arrangement, and active production remains unchanged.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- You can show up to 25 machines plus hall pools. Gantt shows up to 25 machines without pool columns.
- If no pool appears, tick a hall under **Hall pools → Which pools to show**. Use a wider screen if the placement menu is unavailable.
- If a queue shows only 40 cards, use **Show 40 more**, or the smaller remaining number. The count and summary include every card in the queue, also the ones not shown yet.
- A hall pool loads up to 500 assignments; a machine queue loads up to 100. With **Queue Position** on, dropping an unassigned operation into a large queue adds the work but does not save its chosen position. This affects hall pools with more than 500 active assignments and machine queues with more than 100. The Planner shows **Could not persist queue reorder. Local changes were rolled back.** and reloads the queue. Check the queue before trying again. Reordering work already in a large pool is still saved.
- If a change cannot be saved, the Planner shows an error and reloads the queues involved. Check them before trying again.
- If filters hide all machines, the planner shows an empty planning surface.
- Removing an assignment sends the work back out of the planned queue rather than completing it.
- If your search covers a queue, close it before dropping at the end so work lands at the very end. Otherwise, the drop can land among its cards. **Unassigned** leaves machine queues and pools unfiltered.
- If a dragged order does not match the displayed order, check whether **Sort by Queue Position** is enabled or sorting rules are active.
- Running work must stop before handover. Finished and cancelled work stays on its machine.
- Review the actual saving result in **Improve plan**. Compare each option’s predicted result before saving; some work must keep its place.
- **Times may be too early**: review missing machines or dates, planned durations, shift plans, and current production entries before relying on the finish.
- Warning counts differ between pages: Assignment Gantt covers the displayed machines from last week to three weeks ahead; Orders & Products counts late deliveries across the company.
