title: "Plan Machine Assignments"

## Goal

Turn unassigned work into machine queues or shared hall pools and review how future assignments fit across machines and time.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Admin or Page Access `assignment_planner`, with relevant Machine Access.
- Entry points: `/assignment-planner`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Assignment Planner page](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner).

### Preconditions
- Machines are available for planning.
- Orders or operations already exist in the system.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the visible hall or machine list" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select up to 25 machines and the halls whose pools you need. Pools do not count toward the machine limit. Narrow work with order tags, order and operation priorities, or the status of individual assignments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open search with Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) or the search icon in **Filter & Sort**. Search for an order, product, or customer. Choose **Search in → Unassigned**, **Hall pools**, **Machines**, or **Everything**. Esc or × clears and closes the search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a machine or hall pool" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drag the operation onto a machine column when its destination is known. If several machines in a hall could perform it, drag it into that hall's Hall Pool or assign a new operation there. Use the readiness badge to see when a machine can claim the pooled work.

    For work without a connected sensor, choose a destination marked **Work center**. Review the destination before confirming; the work center shows orders without sensor KPIs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Reorder work inside the queue to adjust the planned sequence. Enable **Sort by Queue Position** in the sort/filter options to display that manual order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update key details" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Update key details such as status, dates, or priority when needed. Review or edit operation and assignment notes to keep instructions with the work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the future schedule" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open Assignment Gantt and select up to 25 machines. Close the machine selection menu to apply your choices. Inspect the timeline for overlaps and open a related order when needed. Gantt has no hall-pool columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange future assignments when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Improve plan**, compare the alternatives, and review the assignments that would change before choosing **Save as plan**. Running and on-hold work keep their place. Follow the detailed comparison steps below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the queue changes" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Let the planner save the queue changes back to the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep Hall Pools Beside the List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the placement" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    On a wide screen, open **Hall pools → Where to show them → Beside the list**. Your choice is saved for your account. Choose **In the board** to return pools to the machines’ layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the halls" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Which pools to show**, tick the halls you need. This also changes the hall filter. Each pool appears beside **Unassigned operations** and stays in view while you scroll through the machines. If the list is hidden, pools start at the left edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make room and place work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Collapse pool** to leave a narrow strip. If your search covers the pool, close it first so the work lands at the very end. Otherwise, a drop on the strip can land among the cards. **Unassigned** does not filter pools. You can drop onto the strip even while it is collapsed. Started work opens the handover dialog; after confirmation, its remainder goes to the top. Click the strip or use **Expand pool** to see its cards again. This browser remembers collapsed pools for the current company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Search the List, Pools, or Machines

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Press Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) or select the search icon in **Filter & Sort**, even when the unassigned list is hidden. Enter an order, product, or customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to search" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Search in**, choose **Unassigned** for the list, **Hall pools** for pooled work, **Machines** for work on machines, or **Everything** for all three. Other areas keep showing their cards. The default is **Unassigned**, and your choice is remembered. **Machines** does not search machine names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the matches and clear search" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If a queue has more than 40 matches, use **Show 40 more**, or the smaller remaining number, to reveal more. When the search includes the unassigned list, check its found count. Press Esc or × to close and clear search; other filters remain in effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A link with a search uses **Everything** for that visit without replacing your saved choice.

## Move Work to Another Queue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an assignment that has not started" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Find the card in its machine or hall-pool column. For pooled work, check the readiness badge before choosing the machine that will take it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag the card to its destination" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drag it directly onto the destination machine or hall-pool column. You do not need to unassign it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its queue position" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose the start or end of the destination queue and review the resulting plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Started, stopped work uses the handover steps below. Running work must stop first. Finished and cancelled work cannot change destination.

## Hand Over Started Work to Another Queue

Administrators and users with access to the Orders page can choose any hall pool or another machine they can access. Every user needs access to the step’s current machine. Other users can only return the step to the pool of its current machine’s hall, in Assignment Planner or Data Acquisition. The step must have started and then stopped. Finished or cancelled steps cannot be handed over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check that the work is stopped" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    A running card stays locked with **Running. Stop it on the machine before moving it.** Use the normal stop action in [Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition) and record the quantities first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag the stopped step to its destination" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Drop the card on a hall-pool or another machine column. The handover dialog opens even if started work is marked **Planned** or **Released**. Moving within the same column only reorders the queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review quantities and pass on the reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check **Planned**, **Done here**, and **Left**. Review **Quantity left**: only good parts reduce the suggested remainder. Change it if parts need to be made again, or leave it empty if unknown. If nothing is left, finish the step unless more parts are needed. Add **Reason (optional)** for the next operator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and review the queues" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the destination, then choose **Back to pool** or **Move** for a machine. There is no automatic undo for a handover. The original machine keeps its times and good parts, marked **Handed over**. The rest goes to the top of the destination queue, even when dropped onto a collapsed pool. Both queues update. The step remains open until the remainder is finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, if 40 of 100 planned parts are good, the original machine keeps 40 and the next machine receives 60. Hover over a grey badge to check the destination or source and any reason. If the step changed during review, inspect the refreshed queues before trying again.

## Set an Operation Priority and Filter the Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the priority to change" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    On an operation or queue card, select the dashed **OP** chip to set **Operation priority**. Use **P** to change **Order priority** instead. The two values are independent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose matching priorities" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Order priority** and **Operation priority** to select one or more values. Type a number if it is absent from the legend. With both filters set, work must match both in the unassigned panel and machine columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the remaining filters" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If work disappears, review the status, tags, and machines. For missing work in the unassigned list, also check the import filter. It narrows only that list to one ERP upload. Remove any unwanted order, product, or customer filter chip above the board. Open the information icon for **Assignment Planner Guide** for help.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sort and Highlight Planning Cards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose manual order or sorting rules" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In the sort/filter options, enable **Sort by Queue Position** to use the order set by dragging assignments. Turn it off to sort by fields such as priority or date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set rule precedence" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Operation Planned Start** or **Operation Planned End** to sequence individual steps. Choose **Order Planned Start** or **Order Planned End** to keep an order’s steps together. Set ascending or descending order. Add rules to break ties and drag them into the order of precedence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Emphasize the details you need" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Highlight** and select fields from Order, Work Order, Product, Operation, Planning, and Assignment. Check the cards for your selected highlights, such as priority, due date, or customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sort a Queue by Priority and Date

You need access to the Planner and the machine, plus an administrator account or a user account with access to the Orders page. Operators cannot use this action. The machine or hall pool must already contain work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the queue preview" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Filter & Sort**, enable **Sort by Queue Position**. In the machine or hall-pool header, choose **Sort by priority and date**. The preview covers the active queue, including cards hidden by filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the priority and review" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Each opening starts with **Order priority**. Choose **Operation priority** to use step priorities instead. Higher priorities come first, then earlier planned operation starts. Check proposed positions and the previous positions of moved work. Running orders stay first, followed by on-hold orders; **Stays on top** marks these groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or leave the order unchanged" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Apply order** to save the shared sequence. Choose **Cancel** to keep the existing order. If the queue changed during review, check the updated proposal before applying. If loading fails, retry; applying remains unavailable until loading succeeds. If no positions change, there is nothing to apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or undo the result" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the queue after **New order applied**. Select **Undo** in that confirmation to restore the previous order. If undo fails, review the reloaded queue before adjusting it. You can still drag cards to change the sequence. New imports join the end; this action does not keep sorting automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With queue sorting on and no filters or relevant search, card numbers show the positions in each queue. Clear priority, operation priority and tag filters, restore the usual status selection, and clear search in that area if numbers are missing.

## Save or Use Company Sorting

You need an administrator account to save the company default. Other users with access to the relevant screen can reset their own sorting to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set and save the company choices" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    As an administrator, open **Filter & Sort** in Assignment Planner. Set the mode, sort rules, and **Moves** choice, then select **Save as company default**. Wait for **Saved as company default**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the default in the Planner" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    If you have no personal sorting, you start with the company default. To replace personal sorting, use **Reset to company default** in the sort section. It saves the current company mode, rules, and **Moves** choice as your own settings. Without a company default, **Reset to default (no company default set yet)** restores the standard choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the default at the terminal" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Data Acquisition V3, open **Card Display Settings → Sort Order → Reset to company sorting**, then **Save**. The button shows a tick and is unavailable when the sorting already matches. Without a company default, use **Reset to default (no company default set yet)**, then **Save**. Queue-mode choices for running and waiting orders remain unchanged; sort rules are replaced. Add any special running- or waiting-order rules again if needed. Follow the [display setup steps](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition#configure-the-data-acquisition-v3-display).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A saved personal choice or reset does not follow later company-default changes. Reset again when you want to copy newer company choices.

## Review Forecaster Warnings Before Changing the Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check which date the warning refers to" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Forecaster check** in Assignment Planner and review the selected date. The card's coloured tab compares the job with this date: red means late, amber means **at risk**, and green means on time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the explanation" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **How does the Forecaster work?** at the bottom of the menu. This opens the help without changing your selected date check. Read what the colours mean and when times may be too early, then close the help to return to planning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the expected times" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt). In the view options, choose **Expected times** to include delays or **Planned dates** to inspect the plan. Open **Attention** to review the most important warnings first. Follow the related order to check steps without a machine or date. Also check planned durations, machine shifts, and whether current work has been recorded on the PDA/BDE.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether to change the plan" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Reading the forecast changes nothing. If you want to compare waiting-job sequences, open **Improve plan**. Review the comparison before choosing **Save as plan**. Saving changes planned dates and queues, can be undone, and never changes delivery dates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the Forecaster has no expected end, the Planner only compares the job's date with today. If expected times are unavailable in Assignment Gantt, you see planned times without delays while the Forecaster tries again. Neither view confirms that delivery will be on time.

## Find Late Orders and Plan Missing Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the orders needing work" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    With access to Orders & Products, open **Forecaster**. Choose **Late for delivery → Show in table** or **Of these, steps not planned → Show in table**. Date range and status filters pause; order, product, customer, and machine filters still apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find orders absent from the board" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Not planned at all → Show list**. Open an order and select **Assign steps** or **Open in planner**. These orders have a delivery date but no operation on a machine or in a hall pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the missing earlier step" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    In Assignment Gantt, inspect **Attention → Earlier step not planned**. Follow **Show that step**, then **Assign to a machine** when available. Select a machine you may plan on. A step waiting in a hall pool needs a machine too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check dates and the new forecast" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    The assignment uses the step’s own planned dates. If no planned start exists, set dates before treating it as planned. Review the recalculated times for later steps and check whether the order still risks missing delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare and Save a Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the machines and compare" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Improve plan** in Assignment Gantt. Compare **Today** with the other orderings for the machines on the board. Check **Deliveries at risk**, **Total lateness**, and the list of assignments that would move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Separate the preview from the saving result" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Show on board** to preview an alternative without saving. Read what saving would actually do: running, on-hold, and blocked assignments keep their place. If saving would worsen delivery, return with **Back to today** and review missing planning steps instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Save as plan** and review the changed queue places and dates, the effect on orders, and **Left as they are**. Confirm only the intended changes. Delivery dates and the plant’s ordering rule stay unchanged. If someone changed the plan during review, nothing is saved; check the reloaded board again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the board and Assignment Planner for the new queue and Watchmen dates. Use **Undo** to return to the previous plan if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Move One Assignment After Checking Its Effect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the move" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open an assignment in Assignment Gantt. Select **Move → Earlier**, **Later**, **Run next**, or **To machine…**. Read the explanation if the action is unavailable. Running, on-hold, and finished work stays in place. Recorded work prevents a machine change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review other work before saving" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check the expected start and end, other assignments pushed later, and changes to late orders. Save after reviewing the assignments that will be replanned on the machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or undo the move" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the updated queue. Use **Undo** if needed. If someone has changed the queue since, automatic undo is unavailable; review it and move the assignment back by hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the Plant’s Forecast Rule

Administrators open **Planning settings → Planning** in Assignment Gantt. Choose **How does your plant decide what runs next?** and review the [ordering choices](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt). Set **Hold limit (working hours)** between 1 minute and 720 hours, then save. Confirm the displayed forecast rule. Other users can compare alternatives without changing this company setting.

Only working time counts toward the hold limit. Without planned shifts, 24 hours count as the full limit. Once exceeded, paused work stops holding the machine in the forecast and work behind it moves up.

## Review Conflicts After an Order Import

When [Order Upload](/en/platform-intro/pages/erp-upload-planner) flags a conflict for manual resolution, use the order and operation details in the import summary to find the affected work in the planner. Review its current destination, status, notes, and related order before changing the plan.

Move an eligible assignment to the intended queue when appropriate. If work has started, review its recorded quantities and stop it before handing over the remainder. Finished work stays on its machine. The import summary explains when a decision could not be applied because production changed after review.

## Check Move Eligibility and Queue Changes

Unstarted planned or released work moves directly. Started, stopped work opens the handover dialog. Running work must stop first, and finished or cancelled work stays on its machine. Up to 25 machines can be shown; hall pools do not count toward that limit.

For moves without a dialog, with Queue Position enabled, running and on-hold assignments stay at the top and a drop sets the exact position. With it disabled, sorting rules control the view and **Moves** chooses the start/end destination. If another change refreshes the queue while you reorder, redo the reorder against the refreshed queue.

Use [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt) to inspect the timeline. Its drag-to-reschedule mode is **Experimental and read-only**; use supported planner controls for changes.

Use the view switch to open Assignment Gantt for a timeline review. Use its **Move** controls for eligible work or return to Assignment Planner to work with queues.

## Decision Points

- Whether to hand over stopped work to a hall pool or directly to another machine.
- Whether to keep pools beside the list or within the board.
- Whether to search only the list, pools, machines, or all three.
- Whether to choose a specific machine now or leave the work in a hall pool.
- Whether a job should stay unassigned, move to another machine, or be removed from a queue.
- Which machines should appear in the Gantt timeline.
- Whether overlap warnings should be resolved manually or through automatic arrangement of future assignments.
- Whether expected times include all steps, durations, and shifts before you rely on them.
- Whether to save a compared plan or leave the existing plan unchanged.

## Expected Result

The machine queue reflects the latest planning decision, future assignments follow the accepted arrangement, and active production remains unchanged.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- You can show up to 25 machines plus hall pools. Gantt shows up to 25 machines without pool columns.
- If no pool appears, tick a hall under **Hall pools → Which pools to show**. Use a wider screen if the placement menu is unavailable.
- If a queue shows only 40 cards, use **Show 40 more**, or the smaller remaining number. The count and summary include every card in the queue, also the ones not shown yet.
- A hall pool loads up to 500 assignments; a machine queue loads up to 100. With **Queue Position** on, dropping an unassigned operation into a large queue adds the work but does not save its chosen position. This affects hall pools with more than 500 active assignments and machine queues with more than 100. The Planner shows **Could not persist queue reorder. Local changes were rolled back.** and reloads the queue. Check the queue before trying again. Reordering work already in a large pool is still saved.
- If a change cannot be saved, the Planner shows an error and reloads the queues involved. Check them before trying again.
- If filters hide all machines, the planner shows an empty planning surface.
- Removing an assignment sends the work back out of the planned queue rather than completing it.
- If your search covers a queue, close it before dropping at the end so work lands at the very end. Otherwise, the drop can land among its cards. **Unassigned** leaves machine queues and pools unfiltered.
- If a dragged order does not match the displayed order, check whether **Sort by Queue Position** is enabled or sorting rules are active.
- Running work must stop before handover. Finished and cancelled work stays on its machine.
- Review the actual saving result in **Improve plan**. A preview can look better than the result that can be saved because some work must keep its place.
- **Times may be too early**: review missing machines or dates, planned durations, shift plans, and current production entries before relying on the finish.
- Warning counts differ between pages: Assignment Gantt covers the displayed machines from last week to three weeks ahead; Orders & Products counts late deliveries across the company.
