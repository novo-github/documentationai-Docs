---
title: "Review Machine History"
description: "Analyze historical machine performance across a longer date range."
---

## Goal

Understand how a machine performed across days or weeks instead of only one live day view.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Entry points: `/machines/:machineId/machineHistory`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Machine History page](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-history).

### Preconditions
- Historical data exists for the machine.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open machine history from a machine detail view" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open machine history from a machine detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the start and end dates for the review window" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Set the start and end dates for the review window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the zero-data filter on or off depending on" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Turn the zero-data filter on or off depending on whether non-production days should count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review KPI averages, charts, timeline reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review KPI averages, charts, timeline reason summaries, and trend blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the date range until the review period" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Adjust the date range until the review period matches the business question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether to include zero-output days.
- Whether the question needs a short troubleshooting window or a longer performance review.

## Expected Result

The user can explain historical machine behavior with trend and summary evidence.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Very short date ranges can feel similar to the live day page.
- Very long date ranges may hide short operational problems.
