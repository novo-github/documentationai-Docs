## Goal

Understand how a machine performed across days or weeks instead of only one live day view.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Any role with access to this machine.
- Entry points: `/machines/:machineId/machineHistory`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Machine History page](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-history).

### Preconditions
- Historical data exists for the machine.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open machine history from a machine detail view" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open machine history from a machine detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the start and end dates for the review window" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Set the start and end dates for the review window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose shifts or the whole day" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select the shifts you want to review. The charts follow your selection, including active shifts. Open the shift details to check the times, or choose **Whole day** for the full day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the zero-data filter on or off depending on" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Turn the zero-data filter on or off depending on whether non-production days should count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review KPI averages, charts, timeline reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review KPI averages, charts, timeline reason summaries, and trend blocks. **Machine History** for **1W**, **1M**, **3M** or any chosen range now counts time since the last state change, as does the [**Timeline** report](/en/platform-intro/pages/timeline-analysis) over several days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the worked order" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Find **Orders** below the summary. Use **Zoom in** or Ctrl and scroll to inspect a short stretch. Drag empty chart space to move through time. Choose **Whole period** to fit the chart again. Click a bar or list row to select a step; nothing is selected initially.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the booking with machine activity" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Read **Booked time** and **Machine states** in the panel. The step totals include its work on this machine outside the selected period. Hover over a bar to inspect only that stretch, and compare it with the state row underneath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the order through its steps" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review **Steps of this order**, the delivery date, and completed steps. Steps on other machines are visible but cannot be selected here. With order-page access, choose **Open order** to open the full order in a new tab. Close the panel to clear the selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the date range until the review period" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Adjust the date range until the review period matches the business question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether to include zero-output days.
- Whether the question needs a short troubleshooting window or a longer performance review.
- Use a bar’s details for one worked stretch, or the panel for the step’s full recorded work on this machine.

## Expected Result

The user can explain historical machine behavior with trend and summary evidence.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Very short date ranges can feel similar to the live day page.
- Very long date ranges may hide short operational problems.
- When the sensor sends no signal for more than 20 minutes, the last state counts only until the last signal. For a [sensor powered by the machine](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration), that silent time counts as **Off**.

**Waiting for the next active shift** means the machine has not started its next shift yet. Planned shifts from its group are shown until idle, production, or preparation starts an active shift. This does not change the shift mode.

The **Orders** chart and state row show at most the last 3 months of your selection. Move the period to review older work. Changing it clears the selected step. An empty orders message means no work was booked in that period; missing machine-state data is reported separately. Reviewing the chart and panel changes no records.
