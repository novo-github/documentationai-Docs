## Goal

Understand what a machine is doing right now or on a selected day.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Any role with access to this machine.
- Entry points: `/machines/:machineId`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Machine Details page](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-details).

### Preconditions
- The machine already has data available for the chosen date or shift.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a machine from the Machines page" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    On **Machines**, select the magnifier beside sorting, use the floating magnifier on a phone or tablet, or press Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac). Type a machine name and choose **Open →**, or choose halls, tags, states, or a running order to narrow the cards before opening one. See [Search and Filter](/en/platform-intro/pages/search-and-filter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to the right date using the date controls" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Move to the right date using the date controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between shift tabs to focus on a specific" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Switch between shift tabs to focus on a specific shift or all shifts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the main KPIs, cycle information, timeline" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Review the main KPIs, cycle information, timeline blocks, and recent assignment context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the worked operations" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Worked** to see work recorded on the selected day. Open the information icon at the end of a card to check actual times, quantity, **Time in intervals**, and available **Machine states**. For a longer period, choose **See earlier days in the machine history**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what comes next" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select **Up next**. Read the running operations at the top, then the numbered waiting operations. This queue is independent of the selected day. Use status filters to narrow it; queue numbers keep their place. To update an operation, open its status menu and choose the status. Completing an operation removes it from the queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check alert activity" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Check alert activity if the machine is showing warning conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open related actions" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open related actions such as PDA, history, or alert rules when a deeper follow-up is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review another machine on the same day" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Select the machine name in the breadcrumb path at the top of the page and choose another machine. The day stays selected. The list groups machines by hall and checks the current machine. With more than 8 machines, use **Search machines…**. Press ↓ and Enter to choose, or Esc to close.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Whether to review the whole day or a single shift.
- Whether a detected issue needs immediate PDA action, later analysis, or an alert-rule change.
- Choose **Worked** for recorded work on the selected day, **Up next** for the current queue, or [machine history](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-history-review) for several days.

## Expected Result

The user can understand machine state, recent activity, and likely next action without leaving the detail context.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Sparse data on the selected date can make charts or shift blocks look empty.
- A machine may show alerts without requiring immediate intervention if the condition is already known.

The overview card shows only the first 10 waiting operations; the machine page shows the full queue. A completed operation leaves **Up next** but remains in **Worked** on days with recorded work.
