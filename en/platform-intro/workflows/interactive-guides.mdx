## Goal

Learn a screen at your own pace and keep your progress.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admins, Users and Machine operators can follow the guides available to their role and page access. Sign in to the Watchmen Platform.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- You can open **Interactive guides** using the graduation cap at the top. On a phone, the cap is hidden: open the ⋮ menu at the top and choose **Interactive guides**. A small dot on the ⋮ button shows when something is new.
- For an order or hall plan guide, open that order or hall plan first.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your guide" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open **Interactive guides** with the graduation cap, or on a phone through the ⋮ menu at the top. Choose the highlighted **Getting started** guide, search, or use **This page** with its guide count. The welcome tour matches your role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start and follow the screen" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use the round play button with the **Start** tooltip. Read the highlighted area and follow the action or choose **Next**. Use **Skip** when offered to leave an optional step without saving. Real actions you save affect your work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause and return when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Navigate elsewhere or click outside the highlighted task to pause. In **Guide paused**, choose **Resume** to return or **End** to stop. Later, open the panel and use the play button with **Continue** to resume saved progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish or continue to another guide" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Done** when offered. A follow-up can appear with **Start** and **Later**; Getting started uses **Continue**. You can also repeat a finished guide with the green check, **Done – start again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use your points if conversion is offered" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Your first finish earns 10 points. In the panel, choose **Convert** beside your points when available. Check the credits and expiry date, then confirm with **Convert**. Your whole balance becomes personal AI credits at 1 credit per point, usable for one month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Need to do other work? Pause and return later.
- No conversion offered? It requires points, AI access and a company that limits AI use with credits.

## Expected Result

Your guide progress is saved. Completed guides show a green check. Personal credits appear in the chat while any remain.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- A disabled start action can mean you must open a hall plan or order first.
- A machine guide may ask you to choose a machine.
- Replaying a guide or completing an updated version earns no extra points. The one-time 50-point bonus requires all guides available to you, beyond Getting started.
