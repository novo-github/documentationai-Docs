## Before You Begin

**Who can do this: Every signed-in role for their own account.** Start in [User Profile](/en/platform-intro/pages/user-profile), route `/user`, and make sure you can receive email at the new address.

## Change and Confirm

1. Select **Change email**.
2. Enter the new address and request the change.
3. Open the verification link sent to the new address.
4. After confirmation, sign in using the new email.

## Expected Result and Common Problems

The current email stays active until the new address is confirmed. Entering a new address alone does not replace the sign-in address. If you have not confirmed the link, use the current email for sign-in and check the new inbox.

See [two-factor authentication](/en/platform-intro/workflows/two-factor-authentication) for the separate second sign-in step and [Help Center](/en/help-center) for account-specific support.
