## Goal

Set up reasons operators can recognize and choose which machines offer them.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Required access: Administrator. Save a new machine before selecting its reasons.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

Check existing reasons before creating another. Agree names and codes with your team.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a machine’s reasons" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    As an administrator, open **Machine Settings → Downtime reasons**. You can also create and edit reasons in the timeline reason area of the [Admin Panel](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose all reasons or a fixed list" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **All reasons (default)** to include future reasons automatically. Or choose **Only selected reasons** and tick at least one. Search and filter the list as needed. **Select all visible** adds visible reasons; clearing removes the entire selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit a reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **New reason**, or the edit action beside an existing reason. Enter its name and description. Optional **Code** must be unique within the company and becomes uppercase. Choose or enter a **Category**. Use **Pick colour**, enter a colour value, or choose **Random colour**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose availability" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Under **Available on machines**, tick where the reason should be offered. When creating from a machine’s settings, that machine starts ticked. Unticking a machine marked **All reasons** gives it a fixed list of the other current reasons. Future reasons will no longer appear there automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check the machine selection" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the reason, then check the machine’s selection and save the tab if it still has unsaved changes. If the reason was created but its machines could not be saved, keep editing it, check the machine list, and save again. Do not create a duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the selection to other machines" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Use **Apply to other machines**, choose destinations, and review the replacement notice. Confirm to replace their reason lists with the current selection or all-reasons mode. Check the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automatic Rules

Machine selections limit manual assignment. Automatic timeline rules still use company-wide reasons. Configure them in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

## Decision Points

Choose all reasons for automatic access to future additions, or a fixed list for a shorter operator selection. Reuse a shared reason when its meaning is the same. Editing it affects every machine.

## Expected Result

Operators see the reasons allowed on their machine. Charts show codes, and their explanations show categories when provided.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- A duplicate code cannot be saved. Choose another code or edit the existing reason.
- You cannot remove a machine’s only remaining reason through the availability checklist. Give the named machine another reason first, or keep this one ticked.
- Deleting a reason keeps past downtime entries but removes their reason. A machine losing its last selected reason then offers all reasons. Read the deletion notice before confirming.
- If someone changes the selection at the same time, the latest selection reloads. Review it and retry.
- An already open terminal picks up new availability on its next refresh. A rejected reason selection also updates its list.
