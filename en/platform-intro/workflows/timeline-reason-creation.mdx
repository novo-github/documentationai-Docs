## Goal

Set up standardized reasons that users can apply to timeline events and downtime.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
- Required access: Admin only.
- Entry points: `/admin-panel`
</Callout>

Begin on the [Admin Panel page](/en/platform-intro/pages/admin-panel).

### Preconditions
- The user has admin access.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the timeline reason management area" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Open the timeline reason management area from the admin side of the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Start a new reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the reason name and any grouping or visual" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Enter the reason name and any grouping or visual details the platform requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the reason" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Save the reason so it becomes available in machine and PDA flows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Type, Level, and Automatic Rules

Reasons have name, description, color, type, and level 1–5. The frontend reference does not establish the effects of type and level; confirm them with your product contact instead of assuming severity or precedence.

Automatic application uses the reason's specific rule, its DEFAULT rule, then Global rules for Off, Idle, or Preparation. Configure these in [Machine Configuration](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration).

## Decision Points

- Whether a new reason should be added or an existing one renamed or reused.

## Expected Result

Users can choose the new reason when labeling machine timeline activity.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- Too many overlapping reasons can make reporting inconsistent.
- Renaming a shared reason affects how users understand past and future labeling.
