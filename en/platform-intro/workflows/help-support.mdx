## Goal

Find an answer or the right help for the page you are using.

## Before You Begin

<Callout kind="info" title="Workflow access">
Admins, Users and Machine operators can open **Help & Support** while signed in on a wide screen. The AI section requires AI access. This window is unavailable on phones and the PDA page.
</Callout>

### Preconditions

- Use a computer or a tablet with a wide view.
- Open the page you need help with.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open help for the page" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Choose **Help & Support** at the right edge. In the Admin Panel, you can also use the button with the same name in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the help you need" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    To ask about using the page, enter a question under **Ask the AI assistant** and choose **Ask** or press Enter. You can also select an example question. The window closes and the assistant opens. It sends the question once earlier messages are visible and any current answer has finished.

    To learn the page, use **Learn this page**. Start or continue the guide with its round play button when a guide card is shown. Choose a documentation card for an explanation or steps in a new tab. **All documentation** opens the full documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the team when needed" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    For a call, select a card under **Talk to our team** when offered and follow [Book a Meeting with Our Team](/en/platform-intro/workflows/plan-a-meeting).

    For a platform problem, choose **Let us know**. The feedback form opens with **Bug** selected. Describe what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Complete and send the form to report it to Novo AI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decision Points

- Want a walkthrough? Choose the guide card when available.
- Want to read at your own pace? Open a documentation card.
- Want to suggest an improvement instead? The **Feedback** tab at the right edge opens the feedback form with **Feature request** selected by default. Choose the type that fits your message.

## Expected Result

You have asked the assistant, opened a guide or documentation, or sent your problem to Novo AI. Opening the feedback form alone sends nothing.

## Common Problems and Edge Cases

- No AI section: you need AI access. Use the guide and documentation instead, or ask your Admin about access.
- No meeting cards: no meetings are currently offered. Other help remains available.
- No guide or related documentation: use **All documentation**.
- A question has not sent yet: wait for any current answer to finish. If you close the assistant first, the question waits in the chat box for you to send yourself. Text you had already typed stays there.
- Wrong documentation language: links use German when the app is German and otherwise English.
