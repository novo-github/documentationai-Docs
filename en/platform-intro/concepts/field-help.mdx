## What the ⓘ Explains

On almost every page, a small ⓘ appears beside fields, section and tab headings, and the page name at the top. The help is available in English and German, with the same look everywhere.

It explains what a setting does on the Watchmen Platform and why or when you would change it. Sometimes it includes an example or tells you where to find a value, such as the machine’s rating plate.

## Open and Close the Help

Point at ⓘ, tap it on a tablet or phone, or use the keyboard to select it and press Enter or Space. Press Esc or tap outside to close it. Opening the help changes nothing.

## Ask AI

**Ask AI** gives you a short explanation with a concrete shop-floor example. That answer costs no credits, even when your company’s credits are used up. Your entered values are not sent. A setting that was explained before is answered right away.

On the PDA page, the answer appears word by word in a dark bar at the bottom, where the **PDA Assist** messages appear. Close it with × or Esc. It answers that one question only, with no follow-up questions and nothing saved in your chats.

On other pages, **Ask AI** opens the assistant side panel. The answer stays in your chats. Questions you type afterwards use credits as usual.

The action appears only when you are signed in and have access to the AI assistant and AI chats. Help on the sign-in, sign-in code and registration pages and on the feedback form has no **Ask AI**.

## Help in the Planner

The ⓘ beside **Filter & Sort** explains the bar. Choose **Open the page guide** inside it to open the **Assignment Planner Guide**. This replaces the separate information icon.

## Help When Signing In

**Remember me** and **Trust this device for next 30 days** each have an ⓘ beside them.
