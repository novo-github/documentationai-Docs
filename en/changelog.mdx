  **Release date:** October 20, 2026

  ### New Features

  #### [Keep Hall Pools in View Beside the List](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner)
  {/* feature: planner-pool-columns */}

  In Assignment Planner on a wide screen, open **Hall pools → Where to show them → Beside the list**. Choose halls under **Which pools to show**. Each pool stays beside **Unassigned operations** while you scroll through machines. **In the board** remains the default, and your placement choice is remembered.

  Use **Collapse pool** to make space. The narrow strip still accepts cards dropped at the end of the pool. Click the strip to expand it. Follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

  #### [Choose Where to Search for Work](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner)
  {/* feature: planner-pool-columns */}

  The search box above the unassigned list is gone. Previously it filtered the list, hall pools, and machines together. Search now starts with **Unassigned**, which filters only the unassigned list. Choose **Search in → Everything** to search all three as before, or choose **Hall pools** or **Machines**. **Machines** searches work on machines, not machine names.

  Open search with Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) or the search icon in **Filter & Sort**. On the Planner, this shortcut opens Planner search instead of the browser’s find-in-page. The bar shows **Tip: press Ctrl+F to search orders, products and customers**; on a Mac, the tip shows ⌘F.

  Your choice is remembered. A link with a search uses **Everything** for that visit without changing your saved choice. Esc or × closes and clears the search. Follow the [planning workflow](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).

  ### Improvements

  #### [Plan Across 25 Machines at Once](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-planner)
  {/* feature: planner-25-machines */}

  Select up to 25 machines in Assignment Planner or [Assignment Gantt](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt). Previously, the Planner allowed 10 machines and hall pools combined, and Gantt allowed 10 machines. Hall pools now do not count toward the Planner limit. Gantt has no hall-pool columns.

  With many machines, the Planner opens and refreshes faster. After you move a card, only the queues involved update. Gantt no longer reloads after every tick in the machine menu. It reloads once when you close the menu to apply your selection.

  Planner queues initially show 40 cards. Use **Show 40 more**, or the smaller remaining number, to reveal the next cards. The count and summary include every card in the queue, also the ones not shown yet. Follow [Plan Machine Assignments](/en/platform-intro/workflows/assignment-planner).
</Update>

  ### New Features

  #### [Understand Delivery Warnings Before Changing Your Plan](/en/platform-intro/pages/assignment-gantt)
  {/* feature: forecaster-explainer */}

  You can now open **How does the Forecaster work?** to learn what expected times mean, how to read the colours, and when an order may finish later than shown. On **Orders & Products**, the order page, or **Assignment Gantt**, click the information icon beside **Forecaster**. The dot is amber only while automatic ending counts down; otherwise it is green. A brief glow marks a time correction. Follow [Run Data Acquisition](/en/platform-intro/workflows/data-acquisition).

  ### Improvements

  #### [Set Up Assistance with Examples and Warning Previews](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration)
  {/* feature: pda-bde-assist */}

  Administrators can now set production and setup options separately under **Machine Settings → PDA Assist → Start time** and **End time**. Existing machines keep their saved behaviour. For example, setup can end at 08:40 when production began, even if you press **Stop** at 08:46.

  Open **How changes are applied → How are the times chosen?** for six step-by-step examples and this machine's time limits. Use the information icon beside a warning to preview its delay, severity, and sound choice before saving. The preview follows your unsaved settings.

  When you first enable a section with no saved options, start and end correction or three recommended warnings are preselected. Review them and select **Save**. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).

  #### [Choose Times That Match What the Machine Did](/en/platform-intro/pages/data-acquisition)
  {/* feature: pda-assist-real-events */}

  Assistance could use an older production stretch or a time at a limit where nothing happened. It now uses real machine events, most recent first, ignores data gaps under 10 minutes and other interruptions under 2 minutes, and leaves times beyond the automatic limit for your choice; the same rules guide [interval reviews](/en/platform-intro/workflows/improve-intervals).

  #### [Change Settings Only When You Select Them](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration)
  {/* feature: machine-settings-checkboxes */}

  Clicking descriptions or empty space could change settings or selections. In machine settings, hall selections, and the reason form's machine list, use the checkbox or its title or name; descriptions and empty space no longer change the selection.

  #### [Let Shifts Follow Machine Activity](/en/platform-intro/pages/machine-configuration)
  {/* feature: active-shifts */}

Set **Minimum shift length**, **Stop that ends a shift**, and **Maximum shift length** to control its end. Before the minimum is reached, a shorter stop such as lunch stays within the shift as off time. After the minimum, the first stop ends it.   Switching on removes scheduled shifts from today onward, but keeps shifts already recorded today. The assigned shift group stays in place and is unused. Switching off ends the running active shift immediately and creates scheduled shifts from the group from today. Some alert rules do not fire for these machines; review their notices. Follow [Configure a Machine](/en/platform-intro/workflows/machine-configuration).

  While confirmation is pending, use **Resend** after the displayed wait or **Cancel change**. Administrators can also request changes for users who are not operators from user management; the user must still confirm the new address. Follow [Change Your Email Address](/en/platform-intro/workflows/change-email).
