## Overview

Create a new ML project in Novo AI in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to `https://dashboard.novo.ai` and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    Click "New Project" and enter a project name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a dataset">
    Choose your CSV or JSON file and upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup">
    Click "Create". Your project is ready for exploration.
  </Step>
</Steps>
