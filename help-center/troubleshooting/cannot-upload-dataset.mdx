---
title: Cannot Upload Dataset
description: Fix dataset upload issues in Novo AI.
---

## Symptoms

- Error: "Upload Failed"
- Progress stuck at 0%

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check file size">
    Ensure your file is &lt; 500MB (max upload size).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use supported format">
    Only CSV and JSON are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry with stable connection">
    Slow internet may interrupt uploads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">Clear browser cache if issues persist.</Callout>
