## Overview

To use Novo AI, create your first account and project.

## Steps

1. Visit `https://dashboard.novo.ai`.
2. Sign up with your email or provider.
3. Click "New Project".
4. Name your project and save.

You are now ready to add datasets, train models, and explore Novo AI features.
